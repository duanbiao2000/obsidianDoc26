# Logical Chains: How to Depth-Develop IELTS Body Paragraphs

A weak IELTS body paragraph stops at a surface-level claim. A Band 7+ paragraph develops that claim through a vertical chain of cause and effect.

**Formula:** 
`Idea` → `Explanation` → `Deeper Reason` → `Consequence` → `Final Result`

## The 4-Step Build
1. **State the Idea:** Write your topic sentence clearly.
2. **Ask "Why?":** Explain the mechanism (how or why it happens).
3. **Dig Deeper (Second "Why?"):** Hit the underlying psychological, economic, or social root.
4. **Push Consequences:** Ask "So what?" twice. Map the immediate impact, then the long-term individual or societal result.

## Example: Smartphone Overuse
- **Idea:** Smartphones reduce face-to-face contact.
- **Explanation:** Texting is safer and more convenient than live conversation.
- **Deeper Reason:** Direct interaction demands emotional labor and risks social rejection or awkwardness.
- **Consequence 1:** Younger generations experience chronic loneliness and atrophy their social skills.
- **Consequence 2:** This spikes rates of anxiety and depression, destroying the ability to maintain real relationships or work in teams.

## High-Yield Transition Tools
- This happens because...
- Consequently...
- In the long run...
- Ultimately...

# British Academic Discourse: The Core Framework

High-scoring writing is driven by critical thinking and rigorous argumentation, not just grammar.

## 1. The CEI Model (Intellectual Tension)
Every body paragraph needs a closed loop:
- **Claim (C):** Must be debatable. Never state plain common sense.
- **Evidence (E):** Use authoritative, time-sensitive data.
- **Implication (I):** Explain the mechanism and include a counter-argument or nuance.

## 2. The Mini-Argument Formula
Stop aiming for mere correctness; aim for depth.
- **Formula:** `Viewpoint` + `Boundary` + `Metric` + `Counter-argument`
- **Example (Remote Work):**
  - **Claim:** Remote work boosts productivity.
  - **Boundary:** Restricted to task-driven jobs, excluding creative collaboration.
  - **Metric:** Measured by output per unit, not logged hours.
  - **Counter:** Guard against long-term social isolation and turnover.

## 3. Active Listening Tiers for Logic Desensitization
Deconstruct logical fallacies and qualification markers through active listening:
- **Level 1 (Mapping):** LSE Public Lectures.
- **Level 2 (Debating):** Oxford Union Debates.
- **Level 3 (Deconstructing):** PhD Viva voce recordings.

## Step-by-Step CEI Paragraph

- **Draft:** Online public university courses reduce educational inequality.

### 2. Evidence (E) - Authoritative Support
- **Draft:** For instance, MIT OpenCourseWare data shows that millions of learners from developing economies access top-tier engineering and science lectures without tuition costs.
- **Why it works:** It uses a real institutional precedent rather than a vague generalization ("studies show").

### 3. Implication (I) - Mechanism + Boundary/Counter-argument
- **Draft:** This democratizes human capital development by bypassing traditional financial gatekeepers. However, this model only bridges knowledge gaps, not credential gaps; without formal assessment and degrees, self-taught learners still face institutional hiring barriers.
- **Why it works:** It explains *how* the evidence proves the claim, then instantly introduces a boundary and a counter-argument to create intellectual depth.
