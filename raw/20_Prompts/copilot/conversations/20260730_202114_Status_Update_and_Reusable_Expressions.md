### 5 Reusable Expressions for Professional Status Updates

1. **Combined [A] with [B]**
   - *Meaning:* To integrate two different tools or methods into a single workflow.
   - *Example:* "...combining custom Gems, Gemini Live, and Canvas."

2. **Is fully operational**
   - *Meaning:* A professional way to say something is set up, tested, and working smoothly.
   - *Example:* "The loop from active input to automated quizzes is fully operational."

3. **Shifted from [A] to [B]**
   - *Meaning:* Highlights a deliberate change in strategy or mindset.
   - *Example:* "Shifted from passive Q&A to a guided training loop."

4. **Targeted [X] refinement**
   - *Meaning:* Precise improvement on a specific skill or output.
   - *Example:* "...focusing on daily 15-minute speaking drills, targeted writing refinement, and spaced repetition."

5. **Sync up on**
   - *Meaning:* A standard, natural colocation in tech/business for having a quick meeting or alignment discussion.
   - *Example:* "Let me know if you want to sync up on any of these frameworks!"
