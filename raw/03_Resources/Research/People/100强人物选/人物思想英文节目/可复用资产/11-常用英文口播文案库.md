# 11｜常用英文口播文案库

> 这是“句法与节奏资产”，不是万能台词库。每期最多借用 1–2 个结构；必须替换为该人物独有的事实、矛盾和语言。重复的是清晰度，不是措辞。

## A. 从人物矛盾开场

- `This is not a story about how ____ became successful. It is a story about what ____ was trying to solve.`
- `____ spent a lifetime doing ____. That is why this question matters: ____?`
- `The strange thing about ____ is not ____. It is ____.`
- `Before ____ became known for ____, there was a more difficult problem: ____.`
- `A life can look coherent from far away. Up close, it is usually built around a contradiction.`

## B. 把人物问题连接到当下

- `We meet this problem in a different form every day.`
- `The setting has changed. The pressure has not.`
- `For many of us, the modern version of this problem looks like ____.`
- `The question is not historical. It arrives whenever ____.`
- `We may not share ____’s circumstances, but we can recognize the structure of the problem.`

## C. 从生平转向思想

- `That experience did not give ____ a complete philosophy. It gave ____ a problem.`
- `The point is not that this event caused everything that followed. It made one question impossible to ignore.`
- `Over time, the answer became visible in ____’s work.`
- `This is where biography becomes more than biography.`
- `What changed was not only ____’s career. It was the way ____ understood ____.`

## D. 解释一个概念

- `In academic language, this is called ____.`
- `In ordinary life, it means ____.`
- `The useful distinction is between ____ and ____.`
- `This is less mysterious than it sounds.`
- `You can notice it when ____.`
- `The idea is not ____. The idea is ____.`

## E. 使用文本与引文

- `In ____’s own words, “____.”`
- `The sentence is easy to misread. It does not mean ____.`
- `Read in context, the point is more demanding: ____.`
- `The value of this line is not its elegance. It changes where we place responsibility.`

## F. 转向现实检验

- `Let’s test this in an ordinary situation.`
- `Imagine that ____.`
- `Take a familiar example: ____.`
- `Here is where the idea becomes either useful or empty.`
- `The question is not whether we can eliminate ____. It is what we do next.`

## G. 局限、反例与克制

- `We should be careful here.`
- `This is useful, but it is not the whole picture.`
- `Agency is not omnipotence.`
- `A personal practice cannot, by itself, repair a structural problem.`
- `The principle travels. The circumstances do not always travel with it.`
- `We can borrow the principle without borrowing the entire life.`
- `The risk begins when ____ becomes an excuse for ____.`

## H. 收束与开放问题

- `So perhaps the better question is not ____. It is ____.`
- `The point is not to become ____. It is to notice ____ in our own lives.`
- `Keep the question, not the slogan.`
- `What are you treating as ____, when it may only be ____?`
- `What would change if you stopped measuring ____ by ____?`
- `This is not an answer to keep. It is a distinction to test.`

## I. 评论区邀请（任选一句）

- `Which part of this idea survives when you remove the person’s advantages?`
- `Where do you think this principle becomes dangerous—or incomplete?`
- `If you could borrow one distinction from this life, which one would it be?`
- `What modern situation would put this idea under the greatest pressure?`
