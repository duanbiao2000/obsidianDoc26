# EP001｜Ryan Holiday｜英文逻辑脊柱与试读稿

> 状态：设计试读稿，不是完整录制稿。内部来源标记须在完整脚本中保留，发布版不显示。

## A. 英文逻辑脊柱（8 句）

1. `This episode is not about Stoicism as an ancient philosophy. It is about how to keep acting when outcomes are uncertain.`
2. `The modern problem is that we often confuse public feedback with a final judgment on our worth.`
3. `[F:RH-F-01~03] Ryan Holiday’s early career placed him close to the machinery of attention: strategy, media, controversy, and publicity.`
4. `That history does not prove a conversion story. It makes a tension visible: how do you seek attention without becoming governed by it?`
5. `[Q:RH-Q-01~02] His modern Stoic answer emphasizes perception, action, and will—the part of a difficult situation that still asks something of us.`
6. `[C:RH-C-01] In ordinary life, this means separating our preparation and response from the outcome we cannot command.`
7. `[L:RH-L-01] But agency is not omnipotence; personal practice cannot erase structural injustice, illness, or the need for support.`
8. `The useful question is: what are you treating as a verdict, when it may only be feedback?`

## B. 开场试读｜约 75–90 秒

### [00:00–00:25] COLD OPEN

**画面任务：** 自制图形：通知、点赞、绩效数字、拒信标题快速出现后停住；人物姓名在第 12 秒出现。不要使用未授权人物影像。

**Spoken English:**

> Ryan Holiday made a career out of understanding attention. He wrote about how headlines travel, how controversy spreads, and how public opinion can be shaped. Then he spent the next decade writing about ego, stillness, and self-control.
>
> That is not a contradiction to skip over. It is the key to his work.

**中文意译参考：**

> Ryan Holiday 的职业起点，是理解注意力如何流动。
>
> 他写过标题如何传播、争议如何扩散、公众意见如何被塑造。
>
> 但接下来的十年，他却持续写自我、平静与自我克制。
>
> 这不是可以略过的矛盾。
>
> 它恰恰是理解他作品的钥匙。

### [00:25–01:15] WHY NOW

**画面任务：** `THE TENSION｜Feedback is not a verdict`；用抽象的求职系统、内容数据与项目进度条，不使用可识别个人私信或真实拒信。

**Spoken English:**

> Most of us do not live in a palace or command an army. But we do live inside a system of scores. A number on a screen. A rejected application. A performance review. A project that fails for reasons we can only partly see.
>
> The danger is not that these signals matter. They do. The danger is that we turn them into a verdict: *I did not get the result, so I must be the failure.*
>
> This episode is not about becoming indifferent to outcomes. It is about a harder discipline: taking responsibility for your actions without handing your identity over to the score.

**中文意译参考：**

> 我们大多数人不住在宫殿里，也不统率军队。
>
> 但我们确实活在一套不断打分的系统里。
>
> 屏幕上的数字、一封拒信、一场绩效评估，或一个因为你无法完全看见的原因而失败的项目。
>
> 危险不在于这些信号重要——它们确实重要。
>
> 危险在于，我们把它们变成了一纸判决：我没有得到结果，所以我一定失败了。
>
> 这期不是教人对结果无动于衷。
>
> 它讨论的是一种更难的训练：对行动负责，但不把自我认同交给分数。

## C. 口播与发音注记

- **Ryan Holiday**：`RYE-an HOL-i-day`
- **Stoicism**：`STOH-uh-siz-um`
- **Epictetus**：`ep-ik-TEE-tus`
- **Marcus Aurelius**：`MAR-kus aw-REE-lee-us`
- `verdict` 应慢读并停顿；屏幕同步显示 `判决 / final judgment`。
- `indifferent to outcomes` 不是“漠不关心”，下一句必须立即纠正为“not handing your identity over to the score”。

## E. 完整脚本前的写作提醒

- 继续沿用“注意力悖论”而不是扩展为 Holiday 全部人生。
- `The Life` 部分最多 240 词；让作品和思想占更大空间。
- `The Text` 段应先引用 Holiday 的实践性表述，再说明古典斯多葛的语境，避免把当代转译当成原典原意。
- `The Limit` 至少 120 词，不能在最后用一句“当然也有局限”带过。
