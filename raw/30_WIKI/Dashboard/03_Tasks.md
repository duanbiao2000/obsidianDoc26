## ✅ 行动看板

> 📦 需要插件：**Tasks**（Settings → Community plugins → 搜索 "Tasks"）
> 任务写法：`- [ ] 内容 📅 2026-06-30 #标签`

not done
(due before tomorrow) OR (due today)
sort by due
limit 15

tasks
