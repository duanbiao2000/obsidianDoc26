# ⚡ IELTS Speaking 速查



## 评分四维

| 维度 | 缩写 | 核心 |

|------|------|------|

| Fluency & Coherence | FC | 不卡顿 + 逻辑连接 |

| Lexical Resource | LR | 用词精准 + 不重复 |

| Grammatical Range & Accuracy | GRA | 复杂句 + 零低级错误 |

| Pronunciation | PR | 重音 + 语调 + 连读 |



## Part 1 万能开头

- Well, to be honest...

- I'd say... / I suppose...

- That's a good question. I think...

- Not really, but... / Absolutely, I...



## Part 2 时间分配 (2min)

| 段落 | 时长 | 做什么 |

|------|------|--------|

| 点题 | 15s | 直接回答 who/what/where |

| 展开 | 60s | 故事 + 细节 + 感受 |

| 深化 | 30s | 原因 + 对比 + 影响 |

| 收尾 | 15s | 总结 + 反思一句 |



## Part 3 高分句式

- It depends on the context, but generally speaking...

- There's a compelling argument that..., although some would counter...

- From a broader perspective, I'd argue that...

- That's actually a nuanced issue — on one hand... on the other...

- I think the underlying question here is really about...
