## **实战演示：串题示例**

**这5个看似不同的问题：**

- Should governments invest in public transport?
- How can cities reduce pollution?
- Should governments regulate private cars?
- Why do people prefer driving to public transport?
- Will cities change in the future?

**用框架1回答：**

> The fundamental role of government is to balance individual convenience with collective welfare. People prefer driving because it's personally convenient, but it creates negative externalities—pollution, congestion, resource depletion—that harm society. So governments typically use regulations (emission standards), incentives (tax benefits for public transport), and campaigns (environmental awareness) to encourage sustainable choices. When designed well, these policies can reshape behavior on a large scale.

**用框架3对比：**

> In developed countries with good infrastructure, public transport works well, so regulation is effective. But in developing regions, people rely on cars for necessity, not choice. Context matters—one-size-fits-all policies often fail.

**用框架5展望：**

> As technology evolves—electric vehicles, autonomous transit, remote work—the transport landscape will shift fundamentally. Governments will need adaptive policies rather than static regulations.
