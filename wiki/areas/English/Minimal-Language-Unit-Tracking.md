# Minimal-Language-Unit-Tracking

**Type**: Technique  
**Category**: English Expression & Acquisition

## Core Idea

Systematically extract and categorize the smallest reusable language units (lexical chunks) from authentic materials and organize them by usage scenario.

---

## Extraction Categories (from the source)

- Daily conversation / Informal
- Literary description
- Stand-up / Humorous
- Police reports / Formal writing
- Academic writing
- News reporting
- Emotional & Psychological states
- Storytelling
- Body / Medical scenes
- Opinion & Logic expression

---

## High-Value Examples

- out of nowhere
- be totaled
- get checked out
- shatter the serenity
- mind one's own business
- get rear-ended
- at approximately
- extensive damage
- contributing factors
- spin out of control

---

## Practice Method

After extracting chunks, immediately create:
- Scenario-based lists
- Original example sentences
- Fill-in-the-blank exercises
