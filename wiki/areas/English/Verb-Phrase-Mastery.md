# Verb-Phrase-Mastery

**Type**: Core Technique  
**Category**: English Expression

## Overview

A systematic approach to collecting, categorizing, and deploying high-value verb phrases for both speaking and writing, organized by functional themes (learning, attention, sleep, critical thinking, etc.).

---

## Key Categories

### 1. Learning & Memory

- form synapses between neurons
- reinforce neural pathways
- consolidate long-term memories
- strengthen memory retention

### 2. Attention & Focus

- pay full attention to the task
- eliminate distractions
- avoid frequent context switching

### 3. Critical Thinking & Logic

- pressure-test the core arguments
- expose the underlying assumptions
- trace the claim back to first principles
- rebuild the thesis on first principles

### 4. Red-Teaming & Challenge

- rip the thesis to shreds
- poke holes in the opponent's logic
- fall into the correlation-causation trap

## Recommended Top 10 Universal Verb Phrases

1. pay full attention to
2. reduce phone/screen usage
3. increase mental alertness
4. prioritize sufficient sleep
5. consolidate long-term memories
6. space out learning over time
7. take regular breaks
8. embrace mistakes bravely
9. learn from failures
10. strengthen memory retention
