# Chunk-Based-Speaking

**Type**: Concept  
**Category**: English Speaking

## Definition

口语不是一个词一个词拼出来的，而是大量**固定或半固定表达块**的组合。

## Core Shift

从“单词思维”转向“表达块思维”。

**错误做法**：
最近 = recently  
压力 = pressure  
很大 = very big  
→ My pressure is very big.

**正确做法**：
- I've been under a lot of pressure lately.
- Things have been pretty stressful recently.
- I've got a lot on my plate these days.

## Key Principle

**不要只背单词，要积累可直接使用的表达块。**
