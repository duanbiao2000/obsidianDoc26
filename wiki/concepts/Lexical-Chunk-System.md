# Lexical-Chunk-System

**Type**: Foundational Framework  
**Category**: English Expression & Acquisition

## Definition

**Lexical Chunk** (also called Chunk) is the smallest meaningful, reusable, multi-word unit that native speakers store and retrieve as a whole. It is the primary unit for rapid fluency improvement.

---

## Key Terminology Hierarchy

1. **Lexical Chunk** (most recommended)
   - Smallest meaningful, holistic, directly usable unit (1–4 words)

2. **Formulaic Sequence / Formulaic Language**
   - Pre-fabricated, stored as a whole, used without on-the-spot construction

3. **Pragmatic Chunk**
   - Carries communicative functions (turn-taking, hedging, emphasis)

4. **Collocation**
   - Word co-occurrence patterns

---

## Why Lexical Chunks Matter

- Bypasses grammar construction in real-time speech
- Highest correlation with perceived fluency
- Directly transferable between speaking and writing
