# Topic-Extension-Technique

**Type**: Conversation Strategy  
**Category**: English Speaking (especially IELTS Part 3)

## Core Principle

Use a powerful "golden sentence" as a **thesis statement** in the middle of a narrative to elevate the response from storytelling to philosophical reflection.

---

## Signature Sentence (High-Value)

> "Both will mainly require you to deal with setbacks, tradeoffs, and compromise along the way."

This sentence works across many high-frequency IELTS topics:

- Facing challenges
- Adapting to change
- Achieving difficult goals
- Career development
- Lifelong learning
- Decision making

---

## Usage Pattern

1. Tell a specific story
2. Insert the golden sentence as a turning point / summary
3. Break it down into **Setbacks → Tradeoffs → Compromise**
4. End with personal growth or insight

---

## Why It Works

- Shows **systematic thinking**
- Demonstrates depth beyond surface description
- Provides natural structure for Part 2 & Part 3
