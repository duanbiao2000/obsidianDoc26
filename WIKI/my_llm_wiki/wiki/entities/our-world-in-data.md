# Our World in Data

Our World in Data is an online publication that presents research and data on the world's largest problems — poverty, disease, hunger, climate change, war, existential risks, and inequality — through interactive data visualizations and clear explanations.

## Key Features
- Interactive charts and visualizations on global trends
- Research-based articles with open data access
- Covers health, environment, technology, demographics, and more
- Free to use with Creative Commons licensing

## Relevance to Wiki
Our World in Data is a key resource for [[self-directed learning]] in global affairs and data literacy. It complements [[Statista]] for statistics, [[Gapminder]] for global development data, and [[Worldometers]] for real-time statistics.
