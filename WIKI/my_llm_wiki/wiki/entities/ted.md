# TED

TED (Technology, Entertainment, Design) is a media organization that posts talks from experts and thought leaders on a wide range of topics, including science, technology, business, education, and personal development. All talks are free to watch.

## Key Features
- Thousands of free talks from world-class thinkers
- Covers science, technology, business, education, and more
- Short, focused presentations (typically under 18 minutes)
- Available in multiple languages with subtitles

## Relevance to Wiki
TED is a valuable resource for [[self-directed learning]], providing exposure to diverse ideas and perspectives. It complements [[Huberman Lab Podcast]] for science-based content and [[Psychology Today]] for psychology topics.
