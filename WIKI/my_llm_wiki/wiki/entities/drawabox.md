# Drawabox

Drawabox is a free online resource that teaches drawing fundamentals from absolute scratch, focusing on constructional drawing and spatial reasoning.

## Key Features
- Free drawing lessons from beginner level
- Focus on constructional drawing and spatial reasoning
- Structured curriculum with exercises and homework
- Community feedback and critique

## Relevance to Wiki
Drawabox is a key resource for [[self-directed learning]] in art and design fundamentals, complementing [[Figma]] for digital design and [[Canva]] for template-based design.
