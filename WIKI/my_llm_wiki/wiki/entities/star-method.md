# STAR Method

The STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) is a structured framework for answering behavioral interview questions. It is widely recommended for Australian job interviews as a way to demonstrate competencies through concrete examples.

## Structure

- **Situation**: Describe the context or background of the specific event
- **Task**: Explain the challenge or responsibility you faced
- **Action**: Detail the specific steps you took to address the situation
- **Result**: Share the outcomes and what was achieved

## Relevance to Australian Job Seeking

The guide by [[Living Simply Australia]] identifies the STAR method as the standard approach for Australian behavioral interviews. Common interview mistakes include failing to use this structure, providing vague general answers, or focusing too heavily on one element while neglecting others.

## Application for Inexperienced Candidates

For candidates with [[education-first-resume-strategy|little or no local experience]], the STAR method can be applied to experiences from study, volunteering, sports clubs, university societies, or casual work. Transferable skills demonstrated through STAR responses are valued equally to professional experience.

## Australian Interview Context

In the Australian workplace, interviewers expect direct, honest, and specific responses. The STAR method aligns with the Australian preference for straightforward communication and results-oriented thinking. Candidates should practice STAR responses for common behavioral questions and be prepared to elaborate on any part of the structure.
