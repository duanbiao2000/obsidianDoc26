# Seneca Learning

Seneca Learning (senecalearning.com) is a free adaptive learning platform covering most [[A-level]] subjects. It uses [[spaced repetition]] algorithms to help students consolidate knowledge. The source recommends it for knowledge consolidation across a wide range of subjects.
