# Yasir Khan

Yasir Khan is a speaker and author known for his work on communication and memorability. He delivered a TEDx talk titled "How to Speak so People Remember You," where he introduced the **[[SHARE Framework]]**.

## Key Contributions

*   **SHARE Framework:** A methodology for structuring speech to maximize retention, focusing on Standing Out, Highlighting what matters, Addressing the audience, making content Repeatable, and invoking Emotion.
*   **Memorability Thesis:** Advocates the principle that "You cannot be valuable if you’re not memorable," shifting the focus of communication from transmission to retention.
