# Kosmo / Cōpio

Kosmo (also known as Cōpio) is a non-mainstream indie-style note-taking and inspiration board application that uses 1990s internet aesthetics for visual brainstorming and creative collage. It is rated **S Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Key Features

- **Visual brainstorming** — Drag-and-drop interface for creating mood boards and idea collages.
- **Retro aesthetic** — 90s internet-inspired visual design.
- **Indie development** — Small team, community-driven development.
- **Creative focus** — Designed for visual thinkers and creative professionals.

## Strengths

- **Unique approach** — Stands out from traditional note-taking tools.
- **Creative inspiration** — The visual, collage-like interface encourages creative thinking.
- **Niche appeal** — Strong fit for designers, artists, and visual thinkers.

## Weaknesses

- **Niche audience** — Not suitable for traditional note-taking or project management.
- **Limited functionality** — Lacks features like databases, task management, or collaboration.
- **Small community** — Less support and fewer integrations than mainstream tools.

## Comparison with Other Tools

- [[notion]] — Notion is more versatile and structured; Kosmo is more creative and visual.
- [[concepts]] — Both are visual tools, but Concepts is a whiteboard/drawing app; Kosmo is more focused on collage and inspiration.
