# Futurelearn

Futurelearn is a British massive open online course (MOOC) platform offering free short courses from top universities and cultural institutions worldwide.

## Key Features
- Free short courses from top universities
- Covers arts, business, healthcare, science, technology, and more
- Structured learning with video, articles, and quizzes
- Paid upgrades for certificates and unlimited access

## Relevance to Wiki
Futurelearn is a primary resource for [[self-directed learning]], offering university-level content for free. It complements [[Coursera]] and [[EdX]] as major MOOC platforms.
