# Skillshare

Skillshare is an online learning community with thousands of classes in design, business, technology, and creative arts. It focuses on project-based learning with short, practical video lessons.

## Key Features
- Thousands of classes in creative and practical skills
- Project-based learning approach
- Short, focused video lessons
- Premium subscription required for full access (not fully free)

## Relevance to Wiki
Skillshare is a resource for [[self-directed learning]] in creative and practical skills, complementing [[Canva]] and [[Figma]] for design and [[Coursera]]/[[EdX]] for more academic content. Note that it requires a paid subscription for full access.
