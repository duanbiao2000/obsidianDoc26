# NerdWallet

NerdWallet is a personal finance website that provides tools, calculators, and educational content to help individuals make informed financial decisions. It covers credit cards, banking, investing, loans, insurance, and more.

## Key Features
- Financial product comparisons and reviews
- Educational articles on personal finance topics
- Calculators for loans, mortgages, and investments
- Free access to most content

## Relevance to Wiki
NerdWallet is a key resource for [[self-directed learning]] in personal finance, complementing [[Investopedia]] for broader financial education and [[Trading Economics]] for economic data.
