# Readwise

Readwise is a reading retention tool that helps users remember and review what they've read. It syncs highlights from Kindle, web articles, and other sources, and uses spaced repetition to resurface them for review.

## Key Features
- Syncs highlights from Kindle, web, and other reading sources
- Spaced repetition review of saved highlights
- Exports to [[Notion]], [[Anki]], and other tools
- Daily email digest of highlights

## Relevance to Wiki
Readwise is a key tool for [[生產力優化]] and knowledge management, complementing [[Notion]] for organization and [[Anki]] for spaced repetition. It supports [[self-directed learning]] by improving retention of reading material.
