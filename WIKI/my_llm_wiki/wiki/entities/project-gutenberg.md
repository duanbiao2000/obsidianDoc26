# Project Gutenberg

Project Gutenberg is a volunteer effort to digitize and archive cultural works, offering over 70,000 free eBooks in the public domain. Most books are classic literature available in multiple formats.

## Key Features
- Over 70,000 free eBooks in the public domain
- Available in multiple formats (EPUB, Kindle, HTML, plain text)
- Focus on classic literature and reference works
- Free to download and distribute

## Relevance to Wiki
Project Gutenberg is an essential resource for [[self-directed learning]] in literature and humanities, complementing [[Archive.org]] and [[OpenLibrary]] for broader access to free books.
