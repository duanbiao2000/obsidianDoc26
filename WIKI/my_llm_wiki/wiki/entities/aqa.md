# AQA

AQA (Assessment and Qualifications Alliance) is one of the three main exam boards for [[A-level]] qualifications in England, Wales, and Northern Ireland, alongside [[Edexcel]] and [[OCR]]. AQA sets syllabi and exams for a wide range of A-level subjects.
