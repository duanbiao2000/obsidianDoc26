# Magdalen College

**Magdalen College** (pronounced "Maudlin") was founded in **1458** and is one of the most beautiful of Oxford's 39 colleges. It is famous for its Tudor-period tower and the **May Morning** tradition.

## Key Facts

- **Pronunciation**: "Maudlin" — an archaic pronunciation retained from the college's founding era. Compare with the nearby [[Church of St. Mary Magdalen]] which uses the modern "Magdalene".
- **May Morning**: Every 1st May at 6 AM, the college choir sings a hymn from the top of the tower, a tradition that has taken place for over **500 years**.
- **Architecture**: The iconic tower is a centrepiece of Oxford's skyline.

## Significance

Magdalen's May Morning celebration is one of Oxford's most cherished traditions, drawing crowds from around the world. The college's pronunciation quirk is a notable example of linguistic preservation.
