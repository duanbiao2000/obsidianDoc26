# Merton College

**Merton College** was founded in **1264** by **Walter de Merton**. It is one of Oxford's three oldest colleges and is historically significant as the institution that created the template for the entire [[College System]].

## Key Facts

- **Statutes**: Walter de Merton's written statutes transformed student housing into **self-governing communities** — a model that all subsequent Oxford colleges adopted.
- **Architecture**: The chapel's Gothic tower dates from 1450, and the interior is among the oldest and best-preserved in England.
- **Time Ceremony**: A quirky annual tradition since 1971 — students walk backwards around the quad for one hour when clocks change from BST to GMT.
- **Location**: The largest college in the dense university area, surrounded by Oriel, Corpus Christi, and University College.

## Significance

Merton's statutes are considered the "constitution" of the college system. Without them, Oxford might have remained a loose collection of dormitories rather than the federated institution of self-governing colleges it became.
