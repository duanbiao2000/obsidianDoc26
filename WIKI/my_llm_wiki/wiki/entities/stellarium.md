# Stellarium

Stellarium is a free, open-source planetarium software that renders realistic 3D skies in real time, allowing users to explore the night sky from their computer or mobile device.

## Key Features
- Realistic 3D sky rendering in real time
- Over 600,000 stars from the Hipparcos catalogue
- Constellations, planets, deep-sky objects, and satellites
- Time simulation for past and future skies
- Free and open-source

## Relevance to Wiki
Stellarium is a key resource for [[self-directed learning]] in astronomy, complementing [[NASA]] for space science research and [[PhET Simulations]] for interactive science experiments.
