# Vocational Education and Training (Australia)

Vocational Education and Training (VET) is Australia's vocational education system, offering practical, industry-focused qualifications that provide direct pathways to employment. VET qualifications are nationally recognized under the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF).

## Qualification Levels

- **Certificate I–IV**: Entry-level to skilled qualifications; Certificate III/IV are common requirements for trades and technical roles
- **Diploma**: Advanced skill development for paraprofessional or supervisory roles
- **Advanced Diploma**: Higher-level technical and managerial skills

## Relevance to Employment

The VET system provides an alternative pathway to university for many high-paying careers. The guide by [[Living Simply Australia]] highlights that roles such as Construction Manager, Automotive Electrician, and Digital Marketing Manager can be accessed through VET qualifications combined with industry experience, without requiring a university degree.

## Relation to the White Card

For construction industry roles, the White Card (general construction induction card) is a mandatory VET-aligned safety certification required before entering any construction site in Australia. It is a prerequisite for roles such as Construction Manager and Underground Miner.

## VET and the Australian Job Market

VET qualifications are highly valued by Australian employers because they are developed in consultation with industry and focus on practical, job-ready skills. For newcomers, pursuing a VET qualification can provide both local credentials and the opportunity to build professional networks through work placement components.
