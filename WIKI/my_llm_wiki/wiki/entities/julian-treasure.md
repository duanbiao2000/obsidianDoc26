# Julian Treasure

[[Julian Treasure]] is a sound consultant, author, and speaker known for his work on the power of voice and listening. He has over 12 years of experience in organizational development consulting, blending psychology, yoga, and vocal research.

## Background
Treasure's approach to voice was significantly influenced by his experience at a vocal workshop in Minneapolis in the late 1980s, where he was inspired by the methods of the [[Roy Hart]] group. This experience helped him overcome his own shyness and unlock the full potential of his voice. He is a creator of the **Five Elements Voice Framework**, which categorizes vocal qualities into Earth, Fire, Water, Metal, and Air tones.

## Key Works
*   **"How to Speak So That People Want to Listen"**: A popular [[TED]] talk where he introduces the Five Elements Voice Framework and discusses the importance of intentional voice usage.
*   **Organizational Development**: He applies his understanding of voice and sound to improve communication and collaboration within organizations.

## Philosophy
Treasure believes that voice is a critical tool for connection and that most people underutilize their vocal range. He advocates for breaking unconscious vocal habits to achieve greater authenticity and effectiveness in communication. He also emphasizes the social bonding power of collective voice, such as in choral singing.
