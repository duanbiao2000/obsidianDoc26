# 雅思詞彙資源

此頁面匯總雅思備考所需的主題化詞彙資源，引用自 [[research-更新-雅思-頁面-2026-06-21-093050]]。

## 15大主題詞彙列表

以下為精選核心詞彙與例句：

### 1. 教育與終身學習
- `curriculum` (課程), `pedagogy` (教學法), `literacy` (素養), `vocational` (職業的)
- 例句：The school updated its **curriculum** to include vocational training.

### 2. 環境與永續發展
- `biodiversity` (生物多樣性), `conservation` (保護), `sustainable` (可持續的), `carbon emissions` (碳排放)
- 例句：Investing in **renewable energy** reduces global carbon emissions.

### 3. 科技與人工智能
- `innovation` (創新), `automation` (自動化), `algorithm` (算法), `cutting-edge` (尖端的)
- 例句：AI is **revolutionizing** the way we live.

### 4. 健康與醫療保健
- `epidemic` (流行病), `diagnosis` (診斷), `chronic` (慢性的), `wellness` (保健)
- 例句：**Early diagnosis** leads to more effective treatment.

### 5. 工作與職業發展
- `entrepreneurship` (創業), `productivity` (生產力), `work-life balance` (工作生活平衡)
- 例句：Maintaining **work-life balance** is essential for productivity.

### 6. 社會與全球化
- `globalization` (全球化), `inequality` (不平等), `diversity` (多樣性), `migration` (移民)
- 例句：Globalization increases **interconnectedness** of world economies.

### 7. 政府與政策
- `legislation` (立法), `policy` (政策), `infrastructure` (基礎設施), `accountability` (問責制)
- 例句：The government's economic **policy** reduces unemployment.

### 8. 經濟與消費主義
- `inflation` (通貨膨脹), `recession` (經濟衰退), `consumerism` (消費主義)
- 例句：**Inflation** undermines economic stability.

### 9. 犯罪與法律
- `deterrent` (威懾力), `rehabilitation` (改造), `cybercrime` (網路犯罪)
- 例句：Strict penalties act as a **deterrent**.

### 10. 文化、藝術與傳統
- `heritage` (遺產), `preservation` (保存), `authenticity` (真實性)
- 例句：Cultural **diversity** enriches society.

### 11. 人物描寫
- 聰明：`gifted`, `brilliant`, `talented`
- 勤奮：`diligent`, `conscientious`, `meticulous`
- 性格：`outgoing`, `sociable`, `resilient`

### 12. 地點描繪
- `picturesque` (風景如畫), `vibrant` (充滿活力), `tranquil` (寧靜)

### 13. 事件經歷
- `memorable` (難忘), `rewarding` (有意義), `exhilarating` (令人興奮)

### 14. 交通運輸
- `commute` (通勤), `congestion` (擁堵), `public transport` (公共交通)

### 15. 媒體與廣告
- `social media` (社交媒體), `journalism` (新聞業), `fake news` (假新聞)

## 高分詞彙運用技巧

- **告別簡單詞**：`good` → `beneficial`, `advantageous`; `important` → `crucial`, `vital`, `paramount`
- **掌握搭配詞**：`face challenges`, `achieve goals`, `tackle problems`, `raise awareness`
- **謹慎使用習語**：`a blessing in disguise`, `break the ice`（須注意使用場合）
- **靈活運用連接詞**：`To begin with`, `Furthermore`, `However`, `In conclusion`
