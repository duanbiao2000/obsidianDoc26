# Psychology Today

Psychology Today is a website that provides articles, blogs, and resources on psychology, mental health, relationships, and personal development. It features content from therapists, researchers, and writers.

## Key Features
- Articles on psychology, mental health, and relationships
- Therapist directory for finding local professionals
- Blogs from experts in various fields
- Free access to most content

## Relevance to Wiki
Psychology Today is a key resource for [[self-directed learning]] in psychology and mental health, complementing the [[Huberman Lab Podcast]] for science-based health content and [[Lesswrong]] for rationality.
