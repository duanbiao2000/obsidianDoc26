# Coursera

Coursera is a massive open online course (MOOC) platform that partners with top universities and organizations worldwide to offer courses, specializations, and degrees online. Courses can be audited for free, with paid options for certificates and graded assignments.

## Key Features
- Free audit of university courses from institutions like Stanford, Yale, Princeton, and others
- Wide range of subjects including computer science, data science, business, humanities, and more
- Structured learning paths with video lectures, quizzes, and peer-reviewed assignments
- Paid certificates and degree programs available

## Relevance to Wiki
Coursera is a primary resource for [[self-directed learning]], offering structured university-level content without the cost of formal enrollment. It complements other MOOC platforms like [[EdX]] and [[Futurelearn]].
