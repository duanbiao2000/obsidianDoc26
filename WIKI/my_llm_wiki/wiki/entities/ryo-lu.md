# Ryo Lu

**Ryo Lu** is a designer and engineer known for his work at **Cursor**, **Notion**, and **Stripe**. He is a prominent advocate for the convergence of design and engineering roles in the AI era.

## Key Contributions

*   **Designer-to-Builder Transition**: Argues that modern designers must learn to manipulate code and concepts, not just pixels, leveraging AI tools like **Cursor** to build working systems.
*   **Conceptual Design**: Emphasizes that software design is fundamentally about conceptual structures (e.g., blocks, lists, databases) rather than just visual interfaces.
*   **Learn in Public**: Promotes the methodology of "learning by building" and creating verifiable artifacts as the primary path to career advancement in tech.

## Philosophy

Lu believes that AI lowers technical barriers but raises the requirement for strategic thinking and aesthetic judgment. He advocates for a workflow where designers use AI agents for iterative collaboration, bypassing traditional technical hurdles to focus on system logic and user experience.
