# NASA

The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) is the United States government agency responsible for space exploration, aeronautics research, and scientific discovery. It provides free access to space science research, images, videos, and educational resources.

## Key Features
- Free access to space science research and data
- Educational resources for students and teachers
- High-resolution images and videos from space missions
- Public outreach and citizen science programs

## Relevance to Wiki
NASA is a premier resource for [[self-directed learning]] in space science and astronomy, complementing [[Stellarium]] for night sky exploration and [[PhET Simulations]] for interactive science experiments.
