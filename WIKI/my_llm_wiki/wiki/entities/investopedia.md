# Investopedia

Investopedia is a comprehensive online resource for finance and investing education. It provides articles, tutorials, dictionary definitions, and simulated trading to help individuals understand financial concepts and markets.

## Key Features
- Extensive dictionary of financial terms
- Tutorials on investing, trading, personal finance, and economics
- Simulated trading platform for practice
- Free access to most content

## Relevance to Wiki
Investopedia is a foundational resource for [[self-directed learning]] in finance, complementing [[NerdWallet]] for personal finance and [[Trading Economics]] for economic data.
