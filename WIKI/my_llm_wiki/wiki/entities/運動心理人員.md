# 運動心理人員 (Sport Psychologist)

**運動心理人員** (Sport Psychologist) is a professional who applies [[心理訓練]] (Mental Training) principles to help athletes improve performance, manage pressure, and develop psychological skills. This role is distinct from a clinical psychologist or therapist.

## Key Responsibilities

- **Assessment**: Evaluate athletes' psychological strengths and weaknesses.
- **Training**: Design and implement [[心理技能訓練]] (PST) programs.
- **Crisis support**: Provide [[心理危機干預]] during performance failures or injuries.
- **Education**: Teach athletes and coaches about mental training principles.

## Notable Examples

- **[[Iga Swiatek]]**: The tennis champion's team includes a full-time sport psychologist. She states: "Psychological resilience is the most important factor in tennis."
- **[[國家運動訓練中心]]** (Taiwan): Regularly recruits sport psychologists for athlete development programs.

## Connection to Existing Wiki

- **[[心理指導老師]]**: A broader term for professionals who train mental skills in high-performers (astronauts, military). The sport psychologist is a specialized version.
- **[[心理訓練]]**: The sport psychologist is the practitioner who delivers mental training.
- **[[斯多葛主義]]**: The Stoic "inner citadel" concept — controlling internal responses — is a philosophical framework that sport psychologists operationalize through PST methods.
