# Jordan Gilroy

**Jordan Gilroy** is a designer-developer, podcaster, and former co-founder of **Kin Studio**. He is known for advocating the **Hybrid Builder** model, where individuals possess both design and development skills to eliminate handoff friction and accelerate product creation.

## Background
Gilroy co-founded Kin Studio, an agency attempting to combine web design/development with videography and photography. The venture ultimately ended due to misaligned client needs, as web clients rarely required video services and vice versa. This experience reinforced his belief in focused, integrated capabilities over broad, disjointed service offerings.

## Professional Philosophy
- **Hybrid Advantage:** Gilroy argues that doing both design and development allows for a continuous cognitive loop, removing the interpretation errors common in traditional handoffs.
- **Lean Workflow:** He favors a minimal pre-design process (Brief → Moodboard → Concept) over extensive wireframing, believing that early tangible concepts yield better client feedback.
- **Reputation over Niche:** He rejects strict industry niching, preferring to build a broad referral network based on reputation and quality of work.

## Tools and Techniques
- **Webflow:** Uses Webflow as a low-code platform that pushes him toward custom JavaScript and technical deeper dives.
- **AI Collaboration:** Utilizes AI as a "junior developer" copilot to accelerate problem-solving, while maintaining human oversight for validation.
- **Process Learning:** Learns by observing the operational systems (file structures, naming conventions) of other experts, not just their technical outputs.
