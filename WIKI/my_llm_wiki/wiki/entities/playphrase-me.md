# PlayPhrase.me

PlayPhrase.me is a web-based language learning tool that returns short video clips from movies and television shows for any English phrase a user searches. It provides frequency counts, CEFR level tags, and filters for emotion, polarity, topic, idioms, and questions.

## Features

- **Phrase search**: Enter any English phrase to see clips where it is spoken naturally.
- **Frequency count**: Each result shows how many clips are available, helping learners prioritize high-frequency phrases.
- **CEFR grade**: Every phrase is tagged A1–C2 for difficulty.
- **Filters**: Level, emotion, polarity, topic; toggles for "Only idioms" and "Only questions."
- **Navigation**: Paginated results with skip and theater-mode controls.

## Use Cases

- **Complement to [[影子跟读法 (Shadowing)]]**: Learners can search for phrases they are practicing and hear them in authentic contexts.
- **Reinforcement for [[主题化词汇列表]]**: After studying thematic vocabulary lists, learners can look up phrases containing those words.
- **Building [[口语高分底层逻辑|对话感]]**: Hearing natural intonation, pauses, and emotional delivery helps develop conversational fluency.

## Relationship to Other Tools

Unlike push-based resources such as [[English Practice (YouTube Channel)]] or structured lessons from [[English is EZ with Connor]], PlayPhrase.me is a **search-based reference tool**. The learner controls what to look up, making it ideal for just-in-time learning and reinforcement.

## Limitations

- Not a structured curriculum or course.
- CEFR tagging may show minor inconsistencies.
- No efficacy data or user studies are provided.
