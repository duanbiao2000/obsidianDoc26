# Trading Economics

Trading Economics is a platform that provides economic data, indicators, and forecasts for 196 countries. It covers GDP, inflation, unemployment, trade balance, and other macroeconomic indicators.

## Key Features
- Economic data for 196 countries
- Historical data and forecasts
- Interactive charts and comparisons
- Free tier with limited access; premium subscription for full access

## Relevance to Wiki
Trading Economics is a key resource for [[self-directed learning]] in economics and finance, complementing [[Numbeo]] for cost of living data, [[Investopedia]] for financial education, and [[Our World in Data]] for global statistics.
