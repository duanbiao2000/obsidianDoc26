# Oxford Martyrs

The **Oxford Martyrs** were three Protestant bishops executed for heresy in **1555** on Broad Street, Oxford, under the Catholic Queen **Mary I** (daughter of Henry VIII).

## The Martyrs

- **Thomas Cranmer** — Acting Archbishop of Canterbury, a key figure in the English Reformation.
- **Hugh Latimer** — Bishop of Worcester.
- **Nicholas Ridley** — Bishop of London.

All three were tried at the [[University Church of St. Mary the Virgin]] and burnt at the stake on Broad Street. Their executions were a major flashpoint of the Marian persecution.

## Commemoration

- The **Martyrs' Memorial** (erected in 1843) stands at the edge of where the medieval city walls once stood, commemorating the three bishops.
- The event is often cited as a symbol of religious intolerance and the struggle for conscience.
