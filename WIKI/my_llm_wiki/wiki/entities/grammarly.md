# Grammarly

Grammarly is an AI-powered writing assistant that checks grammar, spelling, punctuation, clarity, engagement, and delivery across various platforms and applications.

## Key Features
- Real-time grammar and spelling checks
- Style and tone suggestions
- Plagiarism detection
- Browser extension and desktop app
- Free tier with premium features available

## Relevance to Wiki
Grammarly is a key tool for [[生產力優化]] and effective communication, complementing [[Canva]] for design and [[Notion]] for organization. It is relevant to [[家庭辦公室設置]] as a writing productivity tool.
