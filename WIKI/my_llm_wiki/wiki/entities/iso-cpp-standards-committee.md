# ISO C++ Standards Committee

The **ISO C++ Standards Committee** is the international body responsible for maintaining and evolving the C++ programming language standard. It operates under the International Organization for Standardization (ISO).

## Governance Model

*   **Open and Democratic:** The committee accepts proposals from any member of the community.
*   **Sub-Groups (SG):** Specialized sub-groups focus on specific areas such as concurrency, libraries, and core language features. These groups review proposals in detail.
*   **Peer Review:** Proposals undergo rigorous peer review to ensure they meet the language's design goals, particularly **[[zero-cost-abstraction]]** and backward compatibility.

## Role in Language Evolution

The committee ensures that C++ evolves in a controlled and consistent manner, balancing the need for new features with the stability required by its large user base. This process allows developers to participate in shaping the future of the language by submitting proposals and engaging in community discussions.
