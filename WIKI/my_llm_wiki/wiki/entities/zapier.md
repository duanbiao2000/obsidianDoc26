# Zapier

Zapier is a web-based automation tool that connects apps and services to automate workflows without coding. It allows users to create "Zaps" — automated sequences triggered by events in one app that perform actions in another.

## Key Features
- Connects over 5,000 apps and services
- No-code automation with visual builder
- Multi-step workflows with conditions and filters
- Free tier with limited tasks; paid plans for higher volume

## Relevance to Wiki
Zapier is a key tool for [[自動化]] and [[生產力優化]], enabling workflow automation without programming. It complements [[Notion]] for organization and [[Grammarly]] for writing, and is relevant to [[家庭辦公室設置]] as a productivity tool.
