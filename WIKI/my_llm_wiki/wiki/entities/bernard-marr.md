# Bernard Marr

**Bernard Marr** is a futurist, strategic advisor, and author known for his work on the future of work, digital transformation, and data strategy. He is the author of *Future Skills: The 20 Skills and Competencies Everyone Needs to Succeed in a Digital World*, which outlines a comprehensive framework for navigating the AI-driven economy.

Marr frequently contributes to major media outlets, including the [[BBC]], where he has emphasized the distinction between technical competencies and the "unique human skills" that remain valuable in an age of automation. His work serves as a key reference for understanding the shifting requirements of professional success in the digital era.
