# Anders Ericsson

Anders Ericsson was a prominent psychologist known for his research on expertise and human performance. He is frequently cited in discussions regarding skill acquisition, particularly in the context of the concept of **deliberate practice**.

In the context of [[IELTS]] Speaking preparation, his work is referenced to argue against the notion that high-level performance is solely due to natural talent. Instead, his research suggests that consistent, structured effort is the dominant variable in achieving mastery, providing a theoretical basis for the importance of sustained study and practice in language learning.
