# Christ Church College

**Christ Church** is one of the largest of Oxford's 39 colleges, founded in **1546** by **King Henry VIII**. It is unique in that its chapel is also **Christ Church Cathedral**, one of the smallest cathedrals in Britain.

## Key Facts

- **Notable alumni**: Lewis Carroll (Charles Dodgson), Albert Einstein (briefly in the 1930s), and King Edward VII.
- **Prime Ministers**: Produced **13 UK Prime Ministers**, the highest of any single Oxford college.
- **Tom Quad** (Great Quadrangle) is the heart of the college.
- During the English Civil War (1644–45), **King Charles I** made Oxford his wartime capital, and Parliament sat in Christ Church's Great Hall.
- **Tom Tower** (1682, designed by Christopher Wren) houses the bell **Great Tom**, which rings **101 times** every night at 9:05 PM — representing the original 100 scholars plus one added later.
- **Alice's Shop**: A 15th-century building across from Christ Church that inspired *The Old Sheep Shop* in Lewis Carroll's *Through The Looking-Glass*.
- **Alice Liddell**, the real-life inspiration for *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*, was the daughter of the Dean of Christ Church.

## Significance

Christ Church's combination of college and cathedral is unique in the Anglican world. Its history intertwines royal patronage, civil war politics, and literary legacy.
