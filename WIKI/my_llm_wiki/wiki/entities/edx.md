# EdX

EdX is a massive open online course (MOOC) platform founded by Harvard and MIT. It offers free courses from top universities and institutions worldwide, with paid options for certificates and verified tracks.

## Key Features
- Free courses from Harvard, MIT, and other top universities
- Wide range of subjects including computer science, engineering, humanities, and business
- Structured learning with video lectures, quizzes, and assignments
- Paid certificates and MicroMasters programs available

## Relevance to Wiki
EdX is a primary resource for [[self-directed learning]], offering university-level content for free. It complements [[Coursera]] and [[Futurelearn]] as major MOOC platforms.
