# Google Scholar

Google Scholar is a freely accessible web search engine that indexes the full text or metadata of scholarly literature across an array of publishing formats and disciplines.

## Key Features
- Free search of academic papers, theses, books, and conference proceedings
- Covers all academic disciplines
- Citation tracking and metrics
- Links to full-text where available
- "Cited by" feature for tracking research impact

## Relevance to Wiki
Google Scholar is a foundational resource for [[self-directed learning]] in academic research, complementing [[PubMed]] for biomedical literature and [[Our World in Data]] for data-driven research. It also connects to [[Perplexity]] as an AI-powered research tool.
