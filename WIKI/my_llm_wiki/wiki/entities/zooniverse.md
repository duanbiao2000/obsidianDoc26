# Zooniverse

Zooniverse is a citizen science platform that allows volunteers to participate in real scientific research across disciplines including astronomy, ecology, history, and medicine.

## Key Features
- Participate in real scientific research projects
- Covers astronomy, ecology, history, medicine, and more
- No scientific background required
- Free to participate

## Relevance to Wiki
Zooniverse is a unique resource for [[self-directed learning]] that enables direct participation in scientific research, complementing [[NASA]] for space science and [[PhET Simulations]] for interactive learning.
