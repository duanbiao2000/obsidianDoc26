# Jamie Pachino

Jamie Pachino is an American screenwriter, playwright, and television producer. She is profiled in the "Top 100 American Screenwriters" source as a representative example of an "American senior screenwriter" with significant achievements in both television and theater.

## Career

Pachino's career spans television, film, and stage. Her television credits include work on series such as *Chicago P.D.*, *Halt and Catch Fire*, *Sneaky Pete*, *The Right Stuff*, and *So Help Me Todd*.

## Theater Works

Her notable plays include *Splitting Infinity*, *Waving Goodbye*, *Aurora's Motive*, and *Theodora: An Unauthorized Biography*. Her plays have been developed or produced at major theaters including Steppenwolf Theatre Company, Long Wharf Theatre, Hartford Stage, Roundabout Theatre Company, and American Conservatory Theatre.

## Awards and Recognition

Pachino has received several awards and honors, including:
- Kennedy Center Fund for New American Plays production grant
- Laurie Foundation Theatre Visionary Award
- Joseph Jefferson Award (Chicago)
- Francesca Primus Prize

## Summary

Jamie Pachino is a versatile writer who has built a successful career across both television and theater, embodying the profile of a seasoned, multi-medium American screenwriter.
