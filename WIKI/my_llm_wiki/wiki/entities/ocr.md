# OCR

OCR (Oxford, Cambridge and RSA Examinations) is one of the three main exam boards for [[A-level]] qualifications in England, Wales, and Northern Ireland, alongside [[AQA]] and [[Edexcel]]. OCR sets syllabi and exams for a wide range of A-level subjects.
