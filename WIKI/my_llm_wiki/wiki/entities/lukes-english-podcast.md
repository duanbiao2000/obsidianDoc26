# Luke's English Podcast

Luke's English Podcast is a popular resource for learning British English through authentic, natural conversation. Hosted by Luke, a native English speaker and teacher, the podcast covers a wide range of topics including language learning strategies, culture, and everyday life. It is known for its engaging, conversational style and is frequently used by intermediate to advanced learners.

## Relevance to This Wiki

The podcast was the source of the 27 idiomatic expressions curated in [[8-journals--27-2026-05-09-lepster中的俚语slang--1gysguc]]. The expressions extracted from the podcast are characterized by their authenticity, usefulness, and transferability to the learner's own spoken English. The podcast serves as a valuable resource for learners seeking to move beyond textbook English and acquire natural, idiomatic language.
