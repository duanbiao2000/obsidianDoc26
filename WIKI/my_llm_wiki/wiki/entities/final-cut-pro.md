# Final Cut Pro

Final Cut Pro is Apple's professional video editing software. It is rated **A Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Key Features

- **Magnetic Timeline** — Non-destructive, clip-based editing.
- **Optimized for Apple Silicon** — Excellent performance on Mac hardware.
- **Proxy workflows** — Efficient editing with high-resolution footage.
- **Built-in effects and transitions** — Extensive library of professional effects.
- **Multicam editing** — Sync and edit multiple camera angles.

## Strengths

- **Performance** — Highly optimized for Mac, especially Apple Silicon.
- **Ease of use** — Intuitive interface for semi-professional users.
- **Stability** — Reliable and crash-resistant.
- **One-time purchase** — No subscription required.

## Weaknesses

- **Mac-only** — Not available on Windows.
- **Less industry standard** — Adobe Premiere is more widely used in professional studios.
- **Limited collaboration** — Fewer collaborative features than cloud-based tools.

## Comparison with Other Tools

- [[adobe-premiere]] — Premiere is the industry standard with deeper integration with After Effects; Final Cut Pro is more user-friendly and performant on Mac.
- [[davinci-resolve]] — DaVinci Resolve has superior color grading and a free tier; Final Cut Pro is more streamlined for editing.
- [[descript]] — Descript is text-based editing; Final Cut Pro is traditional timeline editing.
