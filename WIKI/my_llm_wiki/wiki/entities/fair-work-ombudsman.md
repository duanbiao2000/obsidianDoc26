# Fair Work Ombudsman

The Fair Work Ombudsman is Australia's independent workplace regulator, responsible for ensuring compliance with national workplace laws. Its website (fairwork.gov.au) serves as the primary government resource for employers and employees on workplace rights and obligations.

## Role in Employment

The Fair Work Ombudsman provides authoritative information on:
- Minimum wage rates and pay entitlements
- Leave entitlements (annual, personal, parental, long service)
- Public holiday regulations
- Working hours and overtime
- Employment contract types and conditions
- Anti-discrimination compliance in the workplace
- Termination and redundancy rights

## Relevance to Job Seekers

For newcomers to the Australian job market, the Fair Work Ombudsman is the definitive source for understanding employment conditions. It offers resources in multiple languages, tools for calculating pay and leave, and guidance on workplace rights regardless of visa status. The guide by [[Living Simply Australia]] references it as a key resource for verifying employee protections.

## Key Functions

- Investigating workplace complaints and breaches of workplace law
- Providing free education and resources about workplace rights
- Enforcing compliance with the Fair Work Act and related legislation
- Operating the Anonymous Report tool for reporting workplace issues
- Publishing industry-specific awards and pay guides
