# Librivox

Librivox is a non-profit organization that produces free audiobooks of public domain literature, recorded by volunteers from around the world.

## Key Features
- Free audiobooks of public domain works
- Volunteer-recorded content
- Available in multiple languages
- Downloadable in MP3 and other formats

## Relevance to Wiki
Librivox is a key resource for [[self-directed learning]] in literature, complementing [[Project Gutenberg]] for text versions and [[Archive.org]] for broader media access.
