# Jay W. Forrester

**Jay Wright Forrester** (1918–2016) is recognized as the founder of **System Dynamics**. His background in electrical engineering, digital computing (notably the Whirlwind project and magnetic core memory), and servomechanism control directly informed his translation of feedback control principles into management and social systems.

In the mid-1950s at MIT, Forrester began analyzing production and inventory fluctuations in corporate settings. Through hand simulations of General Electric appliance factories, he demonstrated that employment and inventory instability stemmed from internal decision-making structures rather than external market cycles. Key milestones include the publication of *Industrial Dynamics* (1961) and *Urban Dynamics* (1969). He later collaborated with the Club of Rome on the WORLD model series, which formed the basis for *The Limits to Growth*.
