# Peter M. Senge

**Peter M. Senge** integrated **System Dynamics** into organizational theory through his seminal work *The Fifth Discipline* (1990). He popularized concepts such as mental models, learning organizations, and systemic leadership, bridging the gap between rigorous simulation modeling and practical management application.
