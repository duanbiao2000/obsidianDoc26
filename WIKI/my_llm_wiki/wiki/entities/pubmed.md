# PubMed

PubMed is a free search engine accessing primarily the MEDLINE database of references and abstracts on life sciences and biomedical topics. It is maintained by the United States National Library of Medicine at the National Institutes of Health.

## Key Features
- Free access to over 30 million citations and abstracts
- Covers biomedical literature from MEDLINE, life science journals, and online books
- Links to full-text articles where available
- Advanced search filters and tools

## Relevance to Wiki
PubMed is a foundational resource for [[self-directed learning]] in health and biomedical sciences, complementing [[Google Scholar]] for broader academic search and [[Our World in Data]] for health statistics.
