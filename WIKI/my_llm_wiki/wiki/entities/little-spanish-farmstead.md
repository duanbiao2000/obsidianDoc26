# Little Spanish Farmstead

A YouTube channel documenting the restoration and development of an abandoned 5-acre smallholding in Castellón, Spain, purchased in February 2021. The channel is hosted by [[Harriet and Mauro]], a couple who share their journey of bringing neglected farmland back to productivity, raising animals, and moving toward [[Food Self-Sufficiency]].

## Content Focus

- Practical homesteading techniques: soil preparation, raised bed construction, perennial crop establishment
- Restoration of traditional Mediterranean agroforestry systems (almond terraces, olive trees, carob)
- Animal husbandry (donkeys, chickens)
- Philosophy of food production as community resilience in uncertain times
