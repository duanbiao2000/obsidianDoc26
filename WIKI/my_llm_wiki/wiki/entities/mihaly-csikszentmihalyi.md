# Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi

**Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi** (1934–2021) was a Hungarian-American psychologist, widely recognized as a pioneer and founding figure of **positive psychology**. He is best known for developing the theory of **flow** (心流状态), a mental state of optimal experience characterized by complete immersion, intrinsic pleasure, and a balance between challenge and skill.

## Origin and Seminal Works
Csikszentmihalyi coined the term "flow" based on interviews with artists, athletes, and professionals who described their peak experiences using water metaphors. His seminal works include *Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience* (1990) and *Creativity* (1996).

## Key Components and Conditions of Flow
He identified the necessary conditions for entering a flow state, which include:
- Clear goals
- Immediate feedback
- A match between challenge and ability (balance between challenge and skill)

## Impact and Cross-Domain Applications
His research shifted academic focus from pathology to human flourishing, emphasizing intrinsic motivation, creativity, and the conditions under which people feel most alive and effective. Beyond academia, he extended flow theory to multiple practical domains, including education, sports, workplace productivity, and game design.
