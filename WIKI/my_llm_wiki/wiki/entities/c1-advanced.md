# C1 Advanced (Cambridge English)

C1 Advanced (formerly known as Cambridge English: Advanced or CAE) is a high-level English language qualification offered by Cambridge Assessment English, targeting learners at the C1 level of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). It is widely recognized by universities, employers, and governments worldwide.

## Exam Structure

The exam tests four language skills — Reading, Writing, Listening, and Speaking. The Writing paper is the focus of the [[c1-command-words]], [[c1-report-writing]], [[c1-proposal-writing]], and [[c1-communicative-achievement]] guides in this wiki.

### Writing Paper Tasks

The Writing paper includes two parts:
- **Part 1**: A compulsory essay based on two given texts
- **Part 2**: A choice of three tasks from: article, email/letter, **report**, **proposal**, or review

The source [[8-journals--8-engnotes--21-c1-proposal-or-report--n1sqly]] covers Reports and Proposals specifically.

## Four Grading Dimensions

C1 Advanced writing is assessed on four criteria:
1. **Content**: Coverage of all task requirements with specific supporting details
2. **Communicative Achievement**: Appropriate register, tone, and format for the target reader
3. **Organization**: Logical structure, paragraphing, and cohesive devices
4. **Language**: Grammatical accuracy, vocabulary range, and precision

## Relation to Other Exams in This Wiki

C1 Advanced is distinct from the [[雅思]] (IELTS) exam, which also appears in this wiki. While both test high-level English proficiency, they differ in format, scoring, and recognition contexts. Learners preparing for both exams can benefit from comparing the writing task types, particularly how [[c1-report-writing]] compares to IELTS Task 1 report writing.
