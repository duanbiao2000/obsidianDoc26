# Save My Exams

Save My Exams (savemyexams.com) is a website providing past papers and detailed solutions for multiple exam boards including [[AQA]], [[Edexcel]], and [[OCR]] for [[A-level]] subjects. Some content is paid, but the free portion is substantial. The source recommends it for exam practice.
