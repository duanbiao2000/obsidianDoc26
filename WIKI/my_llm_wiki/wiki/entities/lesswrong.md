# Lesswrong

Lesswrong is a community blog and forum focused on refining the art of human rationality. It discusses cognitive biases, decision theory, epistemology, and practical techniques for improving reasoning.

## Key Features
- Articles and discussions on rationality and decision-making
- Covers cognitive biases, Bayesian reasoning, and epistemology
- Community-driven with high-quality discourse
- Free access to all content
