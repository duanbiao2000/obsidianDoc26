# Lionel Standing

Lionel Standing is a researcher who conducted a 1970s experiment demonstrating the picture superiority effect. His work is cited in the "Stop Information Overwhelm: Visual Learning for 6.5x Better Recall" video as evidence that adding visuals to text improves retention by 6.5 times.
