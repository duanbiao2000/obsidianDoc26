# Woody Allen

Woody Allen (born 1935) is an American film director, writer, actor, and comedian. He is ranked #2 in the "Top 100 American Screenwriters" list.

## Key Works

- *Annie Hall* (1977)
- *Manhattan* (1979)
- *Midnight in Paris* (2011)

## Influence

Allen is known for his distinctive neurotic, intellectual, and witty style, often exploring themes of love, death, and existential angst. He has won four Academy Awards for writing and is a prolific filmmaker with over 50 films.
