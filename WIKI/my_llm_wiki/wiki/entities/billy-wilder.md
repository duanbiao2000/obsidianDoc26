# Billy Wilder

Billy Wilder (1906–2002) was an Austrian-born American filmmaker, screenwriter, producer, and director. He is widely regarded as one of the most brilliant and versatile filmmakers in Hollywood history. In the "Top 100 American Screenwriters" list, he is ranked #1.

## Key Works

- *Double Indemnity* (1944)
- *Some Like It Hot* (1959)
- *The Apartment* (1960)
- *Sunset Boulevard* (1950)

## Influence

Wilder is known for his sharp wit, cynical yet humane worldview, and mastery of multiple genres, including film noir, comedy, and drama. He won six Academy Awards and is a benchmark for screenwriting craft.
