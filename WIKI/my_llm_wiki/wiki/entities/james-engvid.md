# James (ENGVID)

James is the creator and host of the **ENGLISH with James · engVid** YouTube channel. He produces lessons focused on English language learning, conversation skills, and communication strategies.

## Contributions to This Wiki

James created the **S.E.A. Small Talk Method**, a structured technique for building one-sentence conversation starters. The lesson "Have Better Conversations: The S.E.A. Small Talk Method" (published February 27, 2025) is the primary source for the [[s-e-a-small-talk-method]], [[mirror-neurons]] (applied), and [[delivery-paralanguage]] concepts in this wiki.

His teaching approach emphasizes:
- Practical, immediately usable techniques
- Psychological explanations (mirror neurons) to justify methods
- Reducing anxiety for language learners through constraints (one-sentence rule)
- Explicit practice and delivery training
