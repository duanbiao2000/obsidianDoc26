# Aaron Sorkin

Aaron Sorkin (born 1961) is an American playwright, screenwriter, and film director. He is ranked #4 in the "Top 100 American Screenwriters" list.

## Key Works

- *The Social Network* (2010)
- *A Few Good Men* (1992)
- *The West Wing* (1999–2006)
- *Moneyball* (2011)

## Influence

Sorkin is renowned for his fast-paced, witty dialogue, complex characters, and idealistic political narratives. He has won an Academy Award, multiple Emmy Awards, and a Tony Award. His work is a prime example of the crossover between film and television writing.
