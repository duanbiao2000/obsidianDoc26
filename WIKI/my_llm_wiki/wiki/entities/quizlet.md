# Quizlet

Quizlet is a digital flashcard and study tool that allows users to create and share study sets. It offers various study modes including flashcards, learn, test, match, and gravity games.

## Key Features
- Create and share digital flashcards
- Multiple study modes (flashcards, learn, test, games)
- Spaced repetition in "Learn" mode
- Large library of user-created study sets
- Free tier with premium features available

## Relevance to Wiki
Quizlet is a popular study tool for [[self-directed learning]], particularly in language learning. It complements [[Anki]] for spaced repetition and [[Duolingo]] for structured language lessons.
