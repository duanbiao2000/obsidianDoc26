# YouTube Music

YouTube Music is a music streaming service that is part of YouTube Premium. It is rated **S Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Key Features

- **Music streaming** — Access to a vast library of songs and albums.
- **Video integration** — Includes music videos and live performances.
- **DJ sets and long-form content** — Unique access to extended mixes and sets.
- **Background play** — Available with YouTube Premium subscription.
- **Ad-free** — No advertisements with Premium.

## Strengths

- **Unique content** — Access to DJ sets, live performances, and video content not available on other platforms.
- **Integration with YouTube** — Seamless switching between music and video.
- **YouTube Premium bundle** — Includes ad-free YouTube and background play.

## Weaknesses

- **Requires subscription** — Full features require YouTube Premium.
- **Interface** — Some users find the interface less intuitive than Spotify.
- **Discovery** — Recommendation algorithm may be less refined than Spotify.

## Comparison with Other Platforms

- [[spotify]] — Spotify is rated A; YouTube Music is rated S for its unique content (DJ sets, video integration).
- [[apple-music]] — Apple Music is rated C; YouTube Music is preferred for its video content and DJ sets.
- [[soundcloud]] — SoundCloud is rated C; YouTube Music has a broader catalog.
