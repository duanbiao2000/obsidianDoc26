# Edexcel

Edexcel (Educational Excellence) is one of the three main exam boards for [[A-level]] qualifications in England, Wales, and Northern Ireland, alongside [[AQA]] and [[OCR]]. Edexcel sets syllabi and exams for a wide range of A-level subjects.
