# Australian Taxation Office

The Australian Taxation Office (ATO) is the principal revenue collection agency of the Australian government. It administers the tax system, including income tax, goods and services tax (GST), and the superannuation system.

## Relevance to Employment

The ATO plays several key roles in the employment lifecycle:
- **Tax File Number (TFN)**: Required for employment; the ATO issues and manages TFNs
- **Tax Withholding**: Employers withhold tax from wages and remit to the ATO
- **Superannuation**: The ATO regulates compulsory employer superannuation contributions and provides the Super Comparison Tool for selecting funds
- **Tax Returns**: Individuals lodge annual tax returns with the ATO
- **DASP**: The Departing Australia Superannuation Payment allows temporary residents to withdraw their superannuation before leaving the country

## Relevance to Temporary Residents

For migrants, international students, and backpackers, the ATO is the key agency for understanding tax obligations and the [[superannuation-for-temporary-residents]]. Temporary residents can claim a superannuation payout (DASP) after departing Australia, subject to tax deductions.

## Resources

The ATO website (ato.gov.au) provides calculators, guides, and tools for understanding tax obligations, including the Super Comparison Tool for comparing super fund performance and fees.
