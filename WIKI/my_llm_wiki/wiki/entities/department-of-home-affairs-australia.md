# Department of Home Affairs (Australia)

The Department of Home Affairs is the Australian government agency responsible for immigration, border protection, visa processing, and citizenship. Its website (homeaffairs.gov.au) is the primary source for visa information and conditions.

## Relevance to Employment

For anyone seeking employment in Australia, the Department of Home Affairs determines:
- **Visa Work Rights**: Whether a visa permits employment, specific conditions (e.g., hours restrictions for student visas), and limitations on employer or location
- **Visa Subclasses**: Different visas (student, working holiday, skilled migration, temporary graduate) carry distinct work entitlements
- **Visa Compliance**: Working outside visa conditions can result in cancellation and deportation
- **Pathways to Permanent Residency**: Certain employment types may lead to employer-sponsored visas or skilled migration pathways

## Role in the Job Search

The guide by [[Living Simply Australia]] emphasizes that verifying work rights through Home Affairs is the critical first step before beginning any job search. Applicants must understand their visa conditions regarding maximum hours, permitted work types, and duration of stay before applying for positions.
