# think-cell

**think-cell** is a software company known for developing high-performance productivity tools, particularly plugins for Microsoft PowerPoint. It is cited as a case study for an engineering culture that prioritizes technical perfection and deep optimization over rapid feature delivery.

## Engineering Culture

*   **No Arbitrary Deadlines:** The company avoids rigid project deadlines, allowing engineers sufficient time to refine and optimize their code.
*   **Full-Stack C++:** The entire technology stack is built using C++, leveraging its performance capabilities and zero-cost abstractions.
*   **Technical Mastery:** Engineers are encouraged to understand the underlying hardware and memory model, fostering a culture of deep technical expertise.

## Relevance to C++ Development

think-cell demonstrates that C++ is not only suitable for systems programming but also for building highly optimized end-user applications. Their success highlights the value of investing in code quality and long-term maintainability, aligning with the principles of **[[zero-cost-abstraction]]** and **[[incremental-modernization]]**.
