# Khan Academy

Khan Academy is a non-profit educational organization that provides free, world-class education for anyone, anywhere. It offers practice exercises, instructional videos, and a personalized learning dashboard across math, science, humanities, and more.

## Key Features
- Free educational content for all ages
- Covers math, science, economics, humanities, and test prep
- Personalized learning dashboard with progress tracking
- Instructional videos and practice exercises
- Available in multiple languages

## Relevance to Wiki
Khan Academy is a cornerstone resource for [[self-directed learning]], providing structured, free education across subjects. It complements [[Coursera]] and [[EdX]] for university-level content, [[Brilliant]] for interactive math and science, and [[PhET Simulations]] for science experiments.
