# Jane Margolis

Fictional character from the television series **Breaking Bad**, used in the collocation source as a case study for the addiction topic. Her storyline provides expressions such as *hold it together*, *get clean*, *slip back into old habits*, and *go down that road*.

## Role in the Source

The source uses Jane Margolis's narrative to create IELTS‑style Task 2 paragraphs and Part 3 Q&A sets arguing that addiction is a systemic failure, not purely personal. The expressions derived from her scenes are adapted for formal IELTS contexts through register transformation.
