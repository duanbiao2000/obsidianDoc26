# Boris Cherny

**Boris Cherny** is the Head of [[Claude Code]] at [[Anthropic]]. He is a prominent voice in the discussion surrounding the impact of AI agents on software engineering, advocating for the view that "coding is largely solved" for standard tasks and predicting a shift in professional identities from "Software Engineers" to "Builders."

## Role at Anthropic
As the head of the Claude Code team, Cherny oversees the development of Anthropic's AI coding agent. He emphasizes a mission-driven culture focused on safety and has reported significant productivity gains (200%) within his team.

## Key Insights
*   **AI Adoption:** Reports that 4% of GitHub commits are now AI-authored, with rapid growth projected.
*   **Professional Evolution:** Predicts the decline of specialized software engineering titles in favor of generalist "Builder" roles.
*   **Product Strategy:** Advocates for using "latent demand" (user misuse/abuse) as a primary signal for product iteration.

## Personal Workflow
Cherny states that 100% of his code is written by Claude Code, and he has not hand-edited code since late 2024. He maintains that while AI handles implementation details, the enjoyment of coding as an art form remains valid for those who choose it.
