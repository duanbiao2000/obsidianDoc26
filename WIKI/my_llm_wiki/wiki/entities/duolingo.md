# Duolingo

Duolingo is a free language-learning platform that uses gamification to teach vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation across dozens of languages. It offers structured lessons with speaking, listening, reading, and writing exercises.

## Key Features
- Free language learning for dozens of languages
- Gamified lessons with streaks, points, and levels
- Speaking, listening, reading, and writing practice
- Mobile and web access

## Relevance to Wiki
Duolingo is a primary tool for [[self-directed learning]] in languages, complementing [[Anki]] for spaced repetition and [[Quizlet]] for flashcard-based study. It connects to existing language learning resources like [[English is EZ with Connor]].
