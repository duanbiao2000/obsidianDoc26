# 100 Expressions from The New Yorker

A curated list of 100 expressions from *The New Yorker* magazine, selected for their ability to convey a "smart yet honest" temperament (既聪明又诚实的气质). The list is organized around this communicative quality rather than a specific topic domain, making it a unique resource for advanced English learners.

## Organizing Principle

Unlike topic-based vocabulary lists (e.g., food, technology, business), this list is organized by **communicative effect** — the impression the language creates in the listener or reader. The target effect is a balance of intellectual sophistication and genuine sincerity.
