# Numbeo

Numbeo is a crowd-sourced database of cost of living, quality of life, housing, healthcare, traffic, and crime statistics for cities and countries worldwide.

## Key Features
- Cost of living data for cities and countries worldwide
- Crowd-sourced data with regular updates
- Comparisons between cities and countries
- Covers housing, groceries, transportation, utilities, and more

## Relevance to Wiki
Numbeo is a practical resource for [[self-directed learning]] in economics and personal finance, complementing [[Trading Economics]] for economic data and [[NerdWallet]] for personal finance.
