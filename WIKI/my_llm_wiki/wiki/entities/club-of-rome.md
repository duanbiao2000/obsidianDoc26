# Club of Rome

The **Club of Rome** is an organization that collaborated with Jay W. Forrester in the early 1970s on the WORLD model series. This collaboration led to the publication of *The Limits to Growth*, a pioneering study in global sustainability modeling that used **System Dynamics** to simulate the interactions between population, industrialization, pollution, food production, and resource depletion.
