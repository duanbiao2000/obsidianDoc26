# Pablo (For Whom the Bell Tolls)

Pablo is the leader of the guerrilla band in [[Ernest Hemingway]]'s *For Whom the Bell Tolls*. He is a complex figure who serves as a primary source of internal conflict within the group.

## Characterization

Initially, Pablo is depicted as a former hero who has become cynical and fearful due to the prolonged hardships of war. He opposes Robert Jordan's mission to blow up the bridge, fearing it will bring retaliation upon his small group. His reluctance highlights the tension between individual survival and collective military objectives.

## Narrative Function

Pablo contrasts with the idealism and determination of [[Robert Jordan]]. While Pablo represents the corrosive effect of war on morale and leadership, his eventual partial redemption and participation in the retreat add nuance to the novel's portrayal of human behavior under extreme stress.
