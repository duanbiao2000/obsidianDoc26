# Viktor Navorski

Fictional protagonist of the film **The Terminal** (2004, directed by Steven Spielberg), used in the collocation source as a case study for immigration and bureaucracy topics.

## Role in the Source

The source uses Viktor Navorski's story to illustrate expressions such as *stand behind the yellow line*, *leave nothing to chance*, *we're your family now*, *make a run for it*, and *be stuck in no man's land*. These are adapted into IELTS Task 2 paragraphs balancing border control with human compassion, and into Part 3 Q&A sets on bureaucracy, culture shock, and cross‑cultural communication.
