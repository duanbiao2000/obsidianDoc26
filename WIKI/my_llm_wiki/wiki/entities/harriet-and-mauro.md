# Harriet and Mauro

The couple behind the YouTube channel [[Little Spanish Farmstead]]. They purchased an abandoned 5-acre smallholding in Castellón, Spain in February 2021 and are methodically restoring it to a productive food system.

## Homesteading Philosophy

Harriet frames their work as a shift from lifestyle choice to community necessity: "The world is going to need many, many more people working small plots of land growing food for themselves and their immediate community." This reflects the broader concept of [[Food Self-Sufficiency]] as resilience.

## Key Practices Documented

- Straw bale ground clearing (see [[Straw Bale Gardening]])
- Asparagus bed establishment with [[Intercropping]]
- Planting determinate potato varieties like Alouette (see [[Potato Varieties (Determinate vs. Indeterminate)]])
- Barter economy: exchanging vegetables for logs for raised bed construction
- Almond terrace restoration and livestock management
