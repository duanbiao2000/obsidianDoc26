# Statista

Statista is a statistics portal that provides access to data and reports on over 80,000 topics from more than 22,500 sources. It covers market research, consumer data, industry trends, and global statistics.

## Key Features
- Extensive database of statistics and reports
- Covers industries, markets, countries, and consumer behavior
- Data available in charts, tables, and downloadable formats
- Free tier with limited access; premium subscription for full access

## Relevance to Wiki
Statista is a valuable resource for [[self-directed learning]] in market research and data analysis, complementing [[Our World in Data]] for global statistics and [[Gapminder]] for development data.
