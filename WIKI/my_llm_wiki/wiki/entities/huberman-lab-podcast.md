# Huberman Lab Podcast

The Huberman Lab Podcast, hosted by Dr. Andrew Huberman, a neuroscientist and professor at Stanford University, discusses science-based tools and protocols for health, performance, and well-being.

## Key Features
- Science-based health and performance content
- Covers neuroscience, biology, psychology, and physiology
- Practical protocols and actionable advice
- Free access on major podcast platforms and YouTube

## Relevance to Wiki
The Huberman Lab Podcast is a valuable resource for [[self-directed learning]] in health and neuroscience, complementing [[TED]] for ideas and [[Psychology Today]] for psychology content.
