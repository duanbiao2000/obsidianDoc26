# OSSLT (Ontario Secondary School Literacy Test)

The Ontario Secondary School Literacy Test (OSSLT) is a mandatory literacy test administered in Grade 10 (≈高一) in Ontario, Canada. It is identified as the closest functional equivalent to the Chinese 会考 in Western education systems.

## Key Facts

- **Timing:** March of Grade 10 (age 15-16)
- **Status:** **Mandatory for graduation** — students cannot receive a high school diploma without passing
- **Purpose:** Tests **functional literacy** (practical reading and writing skills), not literary appreciation

## Question Types

1. **Reading comprehension** — informational and literary texts
2. **Short answer questions** — based on reading materials
3. **News report writing** — given a photo and factual information, write a news article
4. **Opinion essay** — take a position on an issue and argue it

## Significance

The OSSLT is notable because it is the only exam in the Western systems surveyed that is both:
- Administered at the Grade 10 (≈高一) level
- A mandatory graduation requirement

This makes it the most functionally similar exam to the Chinese 会考, despite the fundamental difference in what is tested (functional literacy vs. language knowledge).
