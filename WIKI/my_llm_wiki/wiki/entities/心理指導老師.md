# 心理指導老師 (Psychological Guidance Teacher)

**心理指導老師** (Psychological Guidance Teacher) is a professional role distinct from [[心理諮詢師]] (counselor) or [[心理治療師]] (therapist). The core distinction:

| Role | Focus | Target |
|------|-------|--------|
| **Psychological Guidance Teacher** | Add excellence | High-performers (athletes, astronauts) |
| **Counselor/Therapist** | Treat pathology | Patients |

## Key Work

- **Design** mental training programs for specific performance contexts.
- **Predict** psychological challenges in advance (pre-competition, pre-mission).
- **Train** mental skills through [[心理訓練]] methods (imagery, simulation, goal-setting).
- **Evaluate** psychological readiness and adjust training accordingly.

## Examples

- **[[航天員]] (Astronaut)**: [[Yang Liwei]]'s psychological training was delivered by guidance teachers, not therapists. His heart rate data (75-85 bpm during launch) demonstrates the effectiveness of this approach.
- **Elite athletes**: [[運動心理人員]] (Sport Psychologists) are the specialized version for sports.

## Connection to Existing Wiki

- **[[心理訓練]]**: The guidance teacher is the practitioner who delivers mental training.
- **[[心理技能訓練]]**: The PST framework (Education → Acquisition → Practice) is the methodology guidance teachers use.
- **[[斯多葛主義]]**: The "inner citadel" — controlling internal responses — is the philosophical goal that guidance teachers operationalize through structured training.
