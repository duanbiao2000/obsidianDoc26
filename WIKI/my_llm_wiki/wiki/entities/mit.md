# MIT

The **Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)** is the birthplace of **System Dynamics**. In the mid-1950s, Jay W. Forrester originated the field while transitioning from digital computing and servomechanisms research to management science. The **Sloan System Dynamics Group** at MIT has been central to the institutionalization and academic integration of the discipline worldwide.
