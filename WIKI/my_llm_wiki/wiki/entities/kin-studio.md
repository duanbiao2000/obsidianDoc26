# Kin Studio

**Kin Studio** was a creative agency co-founded by **Jordan Gilroy** and his brother. The agency aimed to combine web design and development services with videography and photography.

## History and Outcome
The studio was established to offer a multi-disciplinary creative suite. However, it eventually ceased operations due to a lack of overlap in client needs. Most web design clients already possessed visual assets or did not require video production, and vice versa. The partnership effectively functioned as two separate businesses sharing physical space rather than a unified service offering.

## Lessons Learned
The failure of Kin Studio illustrates the challenges of bundling disparate creative services without a clear, overlapping value proposition. It reinforced Gilroy's subsequent focus on the integrated **Hybrid Builder** model, where design and development are tightly coupled, rather than loosely associated disciplines.
