# Donella Meadows

**Donella Meadows**, along with her son Dennis Meadows, applied Jay Forrester’s WORLD model to co-author *The Limits to Growth* (1972). This work expanded the reach of **System Dynamics** into environmental and global sustainability discourse, demonstrating the methodology's capacity to model complex global interactions such as resource depletion and population dynamics.
