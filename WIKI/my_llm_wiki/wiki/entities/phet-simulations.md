# PhET Simulations

PhET (Physics Education Technology) Simulations, created by the University of Colorado Boulder, provides free interactive math and science simulations for learning through exploration.

## Key Features
- Free interactive simulations for physics, chemistry, biology, earth science, and math
- Research-based design for effective learning
- Available in multiple languages
- Works in browser and as downloadable app

## Relevance to Wiki
PhET Simulations is a key resource for [[self-directed learning]] in STEM, complementing [[Brilliant]] for interactive problem-solving and [[Desmos]] for graphing. It also connects to [[Khan Academy]] for structured learning.
