# GCSE English Language

GCSE English Language is a mandatory exam taken in Year 11 (age 15-16) in England and Wales. It is a graduation-required exam that tests analytical reading and creative writing skills.

## Key Facts

- **Timing:** Year 11 (≈高二 in Chinese system)
- **Status:** **Mandatory for graduation**
- **Format:** All handwritten long-form answers; **no multiple-choice tradition**

## Core Question Types

| Section | Content | Duration |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Section A: Reading | 1-2 unseen texts (fiction excerpt, news article, speech), 3-4 analysis questions | ~45 min |
| Section B: Writing | Choose one of two prompts: descriptive/narrative or persuasive/argumentative | ~45 min |

### Typical Reading Questions

- "How does the writer use language to convey [emotion/atmosphere]?" (requires textual evidence)
- "Compare how the two writers present [theme]."

### Typical Writing Prompts

- "Describe a place that has a strong emotional significance for you."
- "'Social media does more harm than good.' Write an article for a school magazine arguing for or against this statement."

## Significance

GCSE English Language is notable for its:
- **Emphasis on analysis** — not just understanding what is written, but analyzing *how* it is written
- **Creative writing component** — students must produce original narrative or descriptive text
- **No multiple-choice** — all answers are handwritten long-form, requiring sustained writing stamina
