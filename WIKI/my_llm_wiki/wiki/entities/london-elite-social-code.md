# London Elite Social Code

An archetypal social code governing behavior in elite London circles. The core principle is **social calibration** — knowing the room, your place in it, and the appropriate register.

## Core Values

- **Breeding:** Proper upbringing is signaled through subtle, ingrained behaviors.
- **Indirectness:** Class and status are implied, never stated or interrogated directly.
- **Composure:** Never lose your cool in public; once lost, it's lost forever.
- **Understatement:** Old money wears things with history; new money is too shiny, too fitted.

## Language Rules

| Rough Expression | Elite Alternative |
|---|---|
| "With all due respect..." | Avoid entirely — almost always followed by disrespect |
| "No offense, but..." | Avoid entirely |
| "Obviously..." | Avoid — implies the other person is slow |
| "To be honest with you..." | Avoid — implies you're usually not honest |

## Social Taboos

- Asking "How much do you earn?" — absolute taboo
- Asking "Where did you go to school?" directly — wait for them to volunteer it
- Being rude to waitstaff — reveals real class instantly
- Phone on the table at dinner
- Bluffing about wine — say "I defer to you completely on this"
- Over-familiarity on first meeting
- Being too direct, no softening
- Talking loudly about money or success
- Losing composure in public
- Agreeing too enthusiastically with everything

## Table Manners (Class X-Ray Machine)

- Wrong grip on cutlery (switching hands to eat is considered American and rough)
- Talking with food in mouth
- Tucking napkin into collar (goes on lap)
- Scooping soup toward yourself (scoop away)
- Cutlery left at random when finished (knife and fork together, diagonally)
- Elbows on the table
- Pouring your own drink first (pour for others first)
- Audible chewing or slurping

## Appearance Signals

| Rough Signal | Why |
|---|---|
| Shoes too new, too shiny | New money |
| Suit too fitted, too deliberate | Trying too hard |
| Tie clip and cufflinks simultaneously | Over-dressed = under-confident |
| Watch worn outside the shirt cuff | Displaying, not wearing |
| Right clothes, wrong occasion | Not knowing context |

## Conversation Taboos

- Agreeing with everything too eagerly
- Asking "Are you posh?" directly
- Skipping small talk (weather is London's social lubricant)
- Complimenting a house, then asking what it's worth
- Explaining why you don't drink — just say "I'll have a sparkling water, thanks"

## Hardest-to-Fake Tells

- Knowing which fork without looking
- Making someone feel included without ever saying so directly
- Delivering a devastating opinion so gently the target thanks you for it
