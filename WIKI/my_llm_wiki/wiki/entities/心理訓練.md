# 心理訓練 (Mental Training)

**心理訓練** (Mental Training) is a systematic discipline for purposefully changing an individual's psychological state to achieve optimal performance. It is fundamentally distinct from [[心理諮詢或心理治療]] (psychological counseling/psychotherapy): the former operates on a sports training model (enhancing normal function), while the latter operates on a medical model (treating pathology).

## Core Distinction

| Dimension | Mental Training | Psychotherapy/Counseling |
|-----------|----------------|--------------------------|
| Knowledge Base | Psychology | Medicine |
| Target Population | Normal/high-performers | Patients |
| Goal | Add excellence | Restore to normal |
| Method | Experiential, simulated | Talk therapy |
| Outcome | Requires continuous practice | May claim permanent effects |

## Principles

Mental training shares principles with physical training ([[超負荷原則]], [[循序漸進原則]], [[持續性原則]], [[針對性原則]], [[個性化原則]], [[全面性原則]]) and adds unique principles: [[特殊情境原則]] (context-specificity) and [[體驗原則]] (experiential learning).

## Methods

- **[[心理技能訓練]] (PST)**: Three-phase framework (Education → Acquisition → Practice).
- **[[意象訓練]]**: Internal vs. external imagery.
- **[[自我暗示和放鬆訓練]]**: Autogenic training (relaxation + activation).
- **[[模擬訓練]]**: Language-based and real-scenario simulation.
- **[[目標設定]]**: SMART with process/performance focus.
- **[[專注力訓練]]**: Five-type attention taxonomy.
- **[[正念練習]]**: Structured mindfulness practice.
- **[[腦波神經回饋訓練]]**: Non-invasive neurofeedback.
- **[[心理危機干預]]**: Immediate crisis support.
- **[[虛擬現實訓練]]**: VR-based phobia/simulation training.

## Applications

- **Sports**: Elite athletes (e.g., [[Iga Swiatek]]) use sport psychologists.
- **Aerospace**: [[Yang Liwei]]'s heart rate data demonstrates training effectiveness.
- **Military**: PRC's [[總參工程兵學院]] (2005) and global military mental training.
- **Education**: Children's attention training, ADHD interventions.
- **Public Security**: Industry-specific psychological training.

## Professional Roles

- **[[運動心理人員]]**: Sport psychologist.
- **[[心理指導老師]]**: Psychological guidance teacher — "adds excellence" not "treats pathology."

## Limitations

- **Transfer problem**: Classroom/simulated skills may not generalize to real contexts.
- **Individualization vs. comprehensiveness**: Tension between custom programs and systematic training.
- **No permanent effects**: "Use it or lose it" — requires continuous practice.
- **Theoretical gaps**: Autogenic training's basis is unproven; mental function isolation is unresolved.
