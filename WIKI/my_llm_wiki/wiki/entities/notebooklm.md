# NotebookLM

NotebookLM is a Google AI research tool designed to process uploaded sources and generate insights. It is described as the "thinking" component in a second brain system, complementing [[Obsidian]]'s "remembering" role.

## Key Features

- **Source Uploads:** Accepts PDFs, Google Docs, slides, YouTube videos, websites, and audio files.
- **Strategic Questioning:** Users can ask cross-source questions (disagreements, contradictions, evidence strength) rather than generic summaries.
- **Source Toggling:** Turn specific sources on/off to scope AI responses to selected documents.
- **Audio Overviews:** One-click generation of podcast-style conversations that walk through key findings and patterns.

## Free Tier Limits

- 50 sources per notebook
- 50 daily chats per notebook
- 3 audio overviews per notebook

## Role in the Second Brain System

In the [[NotebookLM-Obsidian Pipeline]], NotebookLM processes raw information into insight before it enters the permanent knowledge base. It is the "thinking" layer that transforms external sources and personal notes into actionable understanding.
