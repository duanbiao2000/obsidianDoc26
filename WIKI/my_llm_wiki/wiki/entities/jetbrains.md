# JetBrains

**JetBrains** is a software development company best known for creating the **IntelliJ IDEA** IDE and the **IntelliJ Platform**. The company is recognized for its strong engineering culture, focus on developer productivity, and sustainable commercial business model.

## Culture and Practices

*   **Dogfooding:** JetBrains engineers extensively use their own products to build them. This practice creates a tight feedback loop, allowing the company to identify and resolve usability issues rapidly.
*   **Community Engagement:** The company treats community feedback as crucial input, often adjusting business decisions (such as licensing models) in response to user backlash.
*   **Autonomy:** Developers at JetBrains have significant autonomy to propose and implement features, leading to many "small but life-changing" UX improvements.

## Strategic Positioning

JetBrains has positioned itself as a platform provider rather than just a tool vendor. By collaborating closely with language and framework teams (e.g., Java, Spring, Android), it ensures its tools co-evolve with the ecosystem. The adoption of the IntelliJ Platform by Google for **Android Studio** significantly expanded its influence.

## Approach to AI

JetBrains views AI as a complement to, not a replacement for, IDEs. The company focuses on integrating AI capabilities while maintaining the IDE’s role as a deterministic validator of code correctness. This strategy aims to keep the IDE relevant as a "judgment" layer in an AI-driven workflow.
