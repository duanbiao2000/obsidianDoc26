# 23andMe

23andMe is a personal genomics and biotechnology company that provides DNA testing services for ancestry and health insights. Users receive reports on genetic traits, health predispositions, and ancestry composition.

## Key Features
- DNA testing for ancestry and health insights
- Reports on genetic traits and health predispositions
- Ancestry composition and relative matching
- Paid service (not free)

## Relevance to Wiki
23andMe is a resource for [[self-directed learning]] in genetics and personal health, complementing [[PubMed]] for scientific research and [[Psychology Today]] for psychology content. Note that it requires a paid purchase.
