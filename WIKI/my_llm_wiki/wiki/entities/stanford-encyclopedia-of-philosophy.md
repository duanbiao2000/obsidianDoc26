# Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy

The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (SEP) is a freely accessible online encyclopedia of philosophy maintained by Stanford University. It contains peer-reviewed entries on philosophical topics, concepts, and thinkers.

## Key Features
- Free, peer-reviewed entries on philosophical topics
- Maintained and updated by academic experts
- Comprehensive coverage of philosophical traditions and concepts
- Extensive bibliographies and cross-references

## Relevance to Wiki
The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy is a premier resource for [[self-directed learning]] in philosophy, complementing [[Lesswrong]] for rationality and [[Psychology Today]] for psychology. It connects to existing content on [[批判性思維]] and Stoic philosophy.
