Here is a structured interpretation of the [[Project Overview]] page, organized by major thematic domains to clarify the wiki's expanding scope and core entities.

### 1. Philosophy & Mindset
This is the foundational core of the wiki, focusing on Stoic philosophy and personal development frameworks.
*   **Core Concepts:** [[Amor Fati]], [[Negative Visualization]], [[Inner Citadel]], [[Four Virtues]], [[Ego is the Enemy]], [[The Obstacle Is the Way]].
*   **Self-Correction Models:** Explores two distinct paths for handling mistakes: the futile "[[entanglement mindset]]" (characterized by [[retrospective rumination]]) versus "[[wu-wei letting go]]" (emphasizing causal logic and present-point starting). This section underscores the role of [[psychological resilience]] [1].

### 2. Education & Language Learning
The wiki heavily emphasizes language acquisition (specifically for [[IELTS]]) and educational theory.
*   **Enrichment Programs:** Defines their purpose and distinguishes them from remedial, regular, and AP/IB courses, analyzing their role in fostering [[critical thinking]] while addressing [[educational inequality]] [1].
*   **IELTS Preparation Strategies:** Covers advanced methodologies including [[Tension Thinking]], [[Structured Thinking]], [[Narrative Specificity in Language]], [[semantic difference cue]], and [[Deliberate Practice]]. It specifically contrasts Band 7 vs. Band 9 differentiators via concepts like [[lexical-resource-precision-vs-vagueness]], [[argumentation-density]], and [[register-switching]] [1].
*   **Language Learning Methods:** Documents techniques such as [[shadowing]], [[FSI Pattern Drill Methodology]] (including [[Substitution Drill]], [[Transformation Drill]], [[Response Drill]], and [[Expansion Drill]]), [[Progressive Sentence Expansion]], and the [[speak-less-ask-more]] philosophy utilizing the [[kwl-method]] [1].
*   **Vocabulary & Pragmatics:** Integrates resources like [[进阶英语词汇库]], [[100种非入门级日常高频果蔬食材]], [[100组雅思近义词替换实例]], [[BBC Learning English Thematic Synonym Replacement]], and [[Nominalization: Practical Guide]]. It also addresses cultural-pragmatic traps in "We don't use that very often," contrasting Chinese cultural logic with NYC elite logic [1].

### 3. Technology & Data Engineering
A significant technical domain covering data team workflows, infrastructure, and AI agents.
*   **End-to-End Data Scenarios:** Uses a "churn prediction system" to illustrate collaboration across roles ([[data architects]], [[data engineers]], [[data analysts]], [[BI developers]], [[data scientists]], [[ML engineers]]). Covers [[data layering design]], [[ETL pipelines]], [[dimensional modeling]], and introduces a [[data team maturity model]] [1].
*   **Data Infrastructure & Governance:** Details [[Chapter 11: Batch Processing]] (MapReduce, Spark, Flink, HDFS, S3) and defines data pipelines ([[automation]], [[reproducibility]], [[monitoring]]) using [[Airflow]], [[PythonOperator]], and [[DAGs]]. Also covers [[Data as a Toxic Asset]] (challenging the "new oil" metaphor) and [[Documentation Value Pyramid]] (a 5-level L7 model from onboarding to strategic decisions) [1].
*   **AI Agents & Development:** Explores the [[AI coding agent core loop]], [[agent teams]] collaboration, and [[worktree-task-isolation]] mechanisms using [[Git Worktree]] for isolated execution environments. Also touches on [[IDE Cognitive Theory]] for optimizing developer environments (minimizing cognitive load via [[stack-pointer-metaphor]], [[semantic-navigation]], and [[ast-based-refactoring]]) [1].

### 4. Productivity & Environment
Focuses on optimizing physical workspaces and psychological states for high performance.
*   **Home Office Setup:** Uses [[Nic Deville]]'s setup as a case study for [[productivity optimization]] and [[ergonomics]]. Key tools include the [[Elgato Streamdeck XL]], [[Kinesis Advantage2]], and [[Blackmagic Design ATEM Mini]] [1].
*   **Focus & Flow State:** Analyzes [[focus]] as "stimulus filtering" and [[flow state]] (pioneered by [[Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi]]) via neurological mechanisms like [[Transient Hypofrontality]]. Contrasts flow with [[Hyperfocus]], identifies the [[switching cost]] of [[multitasking]], and addresses [[ADHD]] impacts [1].
*   **Cognitive & Information Models:** Cross-disciplinary analysis comparing [[Maxwell's Demon]] (thermodynamics) with Countdown Thinking (cognitive psychology), grounded in [[Landauer's Principle]] [1].

### 5. Career & Interpersonal Dynamics
*   **Career Management:** Applies the Finnish [[Career Management Skills (CMS)]] framework (10 core competency domains including [[Self-Awareness]], [[Adaptability]], and [[Lifelong Learning Awareness]]) to [[IELTS]] Speaking and Writing preparation [1].
*   **Astrology & Relationships:** Analyzes [[zodiac personality traits]] (specifically [[Scorpio]], [[Capricorn]], [[Pisces]], [[Cancer]], [[Aquarius]]) and compatibility dynamics, noting its limitations as popular culture [1].
