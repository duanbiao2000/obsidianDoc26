# Key Concepts

*   **Attention & Energy Economics**
    *   **Definition:** The principle that peak cognitive output is limited to 2–4 hours daily and correlates with energy states rather than logged hours.
    *   **Why it matters:** Challenges traditional time management models that treat humans as continuous-output machines; emphasizes restorative activities as system maintenance.
    *   **Wiki Status:** New concept. Relates to but is distinct from `活著的時間 vs. 死去的時間` (Living Time vs. Dead Time).
*   **Focus Management**
    *   **Definition:** A superior metaphor to time management, emphasizing the guarding of cognitive bandwidth over fragmenting chronological slices.
    *   **Why it matters:** Addresses the limitations of over-optimization and metric blindness in traditional scheduling.
    *   **Wiki Status:** New concept.
