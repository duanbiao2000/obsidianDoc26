# OXFORD | 4K Narrated Walking Tour

A narrated walking tour of Oxford city centre by the YouTube channel *Let's Walk!*, published February 2022. The tour covers approximately 2.2 miles (3.5 km) over 52 minutes, visiting major landmarks including university colleges, historic churches, the Bodleian Library, and Oxford Castle.

The source provides a structured overview of Oxford's history: the origins of the university (c. 1096), the catalyst of Henry II's 1167 ban on English students in Paris, the development of the college system through Merton College's statutes (1264), and the town-and-gown conflicts that led to the founding of Cambridge. It also covers the English Civil War (Oxford as Royalist capital), the Oxford Martyrs (1555), the St. Scholastica Day Riot (1355), and multiple literary connections (Alice in Wonderland, Inspector Morse, and the alleged Harry Potter scar inspiration).

The source is formatted as a learning resource with objectives, exam-ready summaries, FAQ, and a rapid review checklist, suggesting pedagogical intent.
