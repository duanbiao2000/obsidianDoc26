# AI / Technology / Cognition Channel Naming + Slogan Brainstorm (100 Groups)

## Overview

This source document is a systematic brainstorming artifact for naming a content channel focused on **AI, technology, and cognition**. It generates 100 name-slogan pairs organized into 10 thematic directions, each representing a distinct positioning strategy. The document serves as a reusable framework for content strategy and brand positioning.

## The 10 Thematic Directions

1. **Signal/Noise** — Extends the "Signal" core metaphor, emphasizing filtering noise to capture meaningful information.
2. **Depth Series** — Emphasizes "digging one layer deeper" in thinking.
3. **Insight/Cognition** — Directly addresses cognitive upgrade and thinking methods.
4. **AI Frontier** — Technology-oriented, emphasizing AI itself and cutting-edge developments.
5. **Distillation/Refinement** — Emphasizes "removing water, keeping dry goods" content positioning.
6. **Companionship/Growth** — Emphasizes growing together with the audience.
7. **Clarity/Light** — Uses "light, perspective, lens" as metaphors for cognitive clarification.
8. **Future Outpost** — Emphasizes "knowing ahead, standing at the frontier."
9. **Curiosity/Questioning** — Centers on questioning, follow-up, and reverse thinking.
10. **Bilingual/Persona** — Lighter, brand-IP-oriented naming style.

## Key Insights

- The 10 directions are not mutually exclusive; many names could fit multiple categories, which is intentional for brainstorming but creates ambiguity for final selection.
- The document mixes English-dominant names (e.g., "SignalLab") and Chinese-dominant names (e.g., "信号Lab"), suggesting the author is undecided on language dominance.
- The "Signal/Noise" and "Depth" directions appear most congruent with the author's existing content on [[AI 編碼代理核心循環]] and [[上下文工程]].
