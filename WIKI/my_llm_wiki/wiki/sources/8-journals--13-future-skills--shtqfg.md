# Future Skills: The 20 Skills and Competencies Everyone Needs to Succeed in a Digital World

This source outlines the framework presented by [[Bernard Marr]] in his book *Future Skills*. It categorizes the essential competencies for success in a digital and AI-driven world into four domains: Technical, Personal, Interpersonal, and Career.

## Core Argument

While technical skills such as [[Digital Literacy]] and [[Data Literacy]] are foundational prerequisites, they are insufficient on their own. The primary differentiators for value in an AI-dominated landscape are "unique human skills"—specifically the abilities to think, feel, adapt, and connect. These include [[Critical Thinking]], [[Emotional Intelligence & Empathy]], [[Creativity]], and [[Collaboration]].

## The 20 Skills Taxonomy

Marr identifies 20 specific skills required for future success:

### Technical Skills (Foundational)
1. **[[Digital Literacy]]**: Understanding and using digital technologies.
2. **[[Data Literacy]]**: Understanding, analyzing, and applying data.
3. **[[Technical Skills]]**: Mastery of specific tools and platforms.
4. **[[Digital Threat Awareness]]**: Identifying cybersecurity risks.

### Personal Skills (Thinking & Adapting)
5. **[[Critical Thinking]]**: Finding reliable data and making decisions based on it.
6. **[[Judgment and Complex Decision Making]]**: Understanding the broader impact of decisions.
7. **[[Creativity]]**: Imagining, proposing new ideas, and dreaming.
8. **[[Adaptability and Flexibility]]**: Maintaining an open mindset.
9. **[[Ethical Awareness]]**: Understanding the ethical implications of technology.
10. **[[Time Management]]**: Improving work efficiency.
11. **[[Curiosity and Continuous Learning]]**: Maintaining a growth mindset.
12. **[[Embracing and Celebrating Change]]**: Accepting and managing change.
13. **[[Looking After Yourself]]**: Balancing work and life, focusing on physical and mental health.

### Interpersonal Skills (Feeling & Connecting)
14. **[[Emotional Intelligence & Empathy]]: Awareness and control of one's own and others' emotions.
15. **[[Collaboration & Working in Teams]]**: Integrating with others and cooperating well.
16. **[[Interpersonal Communication]]**: Listening, understanding, and expressing viewpoints.
17. **[[Cultural Intelligence and Diversity Consciousness]]**: Cross-cultural understanding and cooperation.

### Career Skills (Professional Orientation)
18. **[[Working in Gigs]]**: Adapting to flexible work environments.
19. **[[Leadership Skills]]**: Guiding and motivating others.
20. **[[Brand of "You" and Networking]]**: Building a personal brand and professional network.

## Significance

This framework provides a structured approach to self-assessment for professionals, particularly in technical fields like [[Data Scientist]] or [[ML Engineer]], where balancing hard technical proficiency with these human-centric skills is crucial for long-term career resilience against [[Automation]].
