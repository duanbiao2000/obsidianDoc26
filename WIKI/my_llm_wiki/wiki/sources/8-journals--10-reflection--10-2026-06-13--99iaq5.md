# Key Concepts

*   **Shadowing (影子跟读)**
    *   **Definition:** A language learning technique involving simultaneous listening and repeating.
    *   **Relevance:** Core method in the author's workflow, integrated with Mujing and recording for self-correction.
    *   **Wiki Status:** Exists as [[影子跟讀法 (Shadowing)]]. This source provides a practical implementation example using specific tools.
*   **Corpus Building / Data Collection Workflow**
    *   **Definition:** Systematic collection of large volumes of text/audio data (1238 items/hour, target 5000/day) for language learning.
    *   **Relevance:** Demonstrates a high-efficiency, industrial-scale approach to personal language acquisition.
    *   **Wiki Status:** Relates to [[自動化]] and [[生產力優化]]. Could extend the concept of [[資料工程師]] skills applied to personal knowledge management.
*   **Obsidian Knowledge Base Restructuring**
    *   **Definition:** Reorganizing notes via Link, Tag, and Folder division, utilizing AI for connectivity improvements.
    *   **Relevance:** Highlights the shift from simple note-taking to structured knowledge engineering ("Note Semiotics").
    *   **Wiki Status:** Relates to [[文檔價值金字塔]] and [[系統心智模型-文檔工程]].
*   **Note Semiotics (笔记符号学)**

    *   **Relevance:** Advanced conceptual framework for organizing digital notes.
