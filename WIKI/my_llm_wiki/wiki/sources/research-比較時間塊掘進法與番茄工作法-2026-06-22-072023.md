## Key Entities
- **Time Boxing / Time Blocking**: Central concepts. A schedule-centric method involving assigning tasks to predetermined calendar slots.
- **Pomodoro Technique**: Central concept. An attention-centric method using fixed 25-minute work sprints and 5-minute breaks, coined by Francesco Cirillo.
- **Francesco Cirillo**: Peripheral entity. The inventor of the Pomodoro Technique.
- **Cal Newport**: Peripheral entity. Author associated with "time-block planning" and "Deep Work," cited regarding treating personal blocks as non-negotiable.
- **Gloria Mark (UC Irvine)**: Peripheral entity. Researcher cited for findings on context-switching costs (23 minutes to reintegrate).
- **Albulescu et al. (2022)**: Peripheral entity. Researchers behind a PLOS ONE meta-analysis validating micro-break recovery.
- **Parkinson’s Law**: Conceptual entity. Principle that "work expands to fill the time available," supporting Time Boxing's hard-stop discipline.

## Key Concepts
- **Time Boxing/Blocking vs. Pomodoro Technique**: Core comparison. Time Boxing is strategic/planning-focused; Pomodoro is tactical/execution-focused.
- **Block-within-Block Architecture**: Hybrid protocol combining macro-level time blocking (60–180 min) with micro-level Pomodoro cycles for administrative tasks.
- **Implementation Intentions**: Psychological mechanism where specifying when/where/how increases execution rates by 20–40%. Supports Time Blocking.
- **Attention Residue & Switching Costs**: Cognitive drag caused by interruptions. Protected time blocks defend against this; fragmented intervals may hinder deep contextual loading.
- **Microbreak Recovery**: Structured short breaks (5–15 min) that yield consistent cognitive performance gains, grounding the Pomodoro break structure.
- **Flow State Interruption**: Risk of breaking deep work continuity with rigid timers, making shorter intervals counterproductive for complex problem-solving.
- **Productivity Theater**: Paradox where optimizing tools/systems consumes more time than executing work.
- **[[Habits]]**: Linked wiki concept. Sustainable systems require supportive habits.
- **[[Mindset]]**: Linked wiki concept. Iteration rather than rigid adherence is key.
- **[[心理弹性]] (Psychological Resilience)**: Linked wiki concept. Adapting to real-world friction in productivity systems.
- **[[Self-reflection]]**: Linked wiki concept. Used for periodic review and dynamic adjustment of interval lengths.
- **[[生產力優化]] (Productivity Optimization)**: Linked wiki concept. The broader workflow into which these techniques integrate.
