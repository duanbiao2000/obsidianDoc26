## Key Concepts

*   **"Chatterbox Drama" Selection Strategy (话痨剧选材)**
    *   **Definition:** Prioritizing media with high dialogue-to-action ratios over action-heavy content.
    *   **Why it matters:** Ensures exposure to natural conversational patterns and vocabulary relevant to Speaking Parts 1 & 3.
    *   **Wiki Existence:** No. Relates to [[沟通技巧]] but is a specific pedagogical tactic.
*   **Deliberate Output during Consumption (刻意输出)**
    *   **Definition:** Actively producing language (speaking/writing) immediately after consuming media, rather than passively watching.
    *   **Why it matters:** Transforms passive entertainment into active study, preventing "escapism."
    *   **Wiki Existence:** No. Relates to [[批判性思维]] or [[求知欲]] but is a distinct study method.
*   **Melodic Intonation/Rhythm (唱歌腔调)**
    *   **Definition:** Focusing on the prosody (stress, speed, intonation) of speech rather than isolated phoneme accuracy.
    *   **Why it matters:** Identified as the key to achieving a Band 7+ in speaking by mimicking natural flow.
    *   **Wiki Existence:** No. Contrasts with [[发音易错]], which focuses on individual word pronunciation.
*   **Active Recall Flashcards (主动回想自测闪卡)**
    *   **Definition:** Using Q&A formats to test understanding of why certain materials are effective/ineffective.
    *   **Why it matters:** Reinforces the strategic logic behind material selection.
    *   **Wiki Existence:** No.
