# Collocation固定搭配: Curated English Collocations for IELTS

A comprehensive compilation of English collocations, fixed expressions, and advanced phrases extracted from multiple YouTube videos and film scenes for IELTS study purposes. The document covers core verb phrases, high‑scoring alternatives, advanced sentence patterns, connected‑speech pronunciation rules, and demonstrates systematic **register transformation** from informal spoken English to formal IELTS‑appropriate language.

## Content Overview

The source is organized into seven thematic units:

1. **Core collocations and high‑score phrases from YouTube teachers** (e.g., Ben's "smart and honest" teaching meta‑discourse, open‑source workplace discussions, technology product videos)
2. **Technical standardisation and analytical reasoning collocations** (from Google DESIGN.md talk, intuition vs. reasoning video, problem‑solving programming interviews)
3. **CS50 game development lecture** (Lua + Love2D, Pong) — extracting vocabulary from technical domains (game loop, delta time, AABB collision detection, state machine)
4. **Career‑progression and language‑learning collocations** (open‑source reputation, network engineer to Distinguished Engineer story, informal vs. formal language learning)
5. **20‑phrase and 25‑phrase bilingual tables** of short spoken expressions with IELTS adaptation directions, "golden sentence" recommendations, and Task 2 model paragraphs
6. **Film‑derived expressions** from **Breaking Bad** (addiction topic, Jane Margolis) and **The Terminal** (immigration topic, Viktor Navorski)
7. **52‑item general expression list** organised by topic (city, happiness, sports, festivals, transitions, grammar, language‑learning)

## Key Methodologies and Findings

- **Register transformation**: Informal spoken phrases can be systematically adapted for formal IELTS contexts (e.g., "my bad" → "I admit that was a mistake on my part")
- **Golden sentence selection**: 12 specific phrases identified as having highest cross‑topic transferability (That's not the case, You have a point there, I'm dead serious, That's on you, Don't get me wrong, I'm not buying it, It is what it is, That's not how this works, Enough is enough, Fair enough, Out of nowhere, I'm blown away)
- **Emotional register classification**: Phrases categorised by attitude (refusal, anger, indifference, acceptance) for IELTS attitude expression
- **Connected speech patterns**: Glottal stop, -ing reduction, pronoun initial consonant dropping presented as pronunciation bonus points
- **Collocation extraction methodology**: Domain‑specific expressions from open‑source, engineering, film, and everyday video genres repurposed for IELTS
