# Boris Cherny: Programming Revolution and Industry Future

This source captures insights from **Boris Cherny**, Head of [[Claude Code]] at [[Anthropic]], regarding the fundamental shift in software engineering driven by AI agents. The discussion centers on the claim that "coding is largely solved" for standard tasks, leading to a redefinition of professional roles, productivity metrics, and product development strategies.

## Key Arguments

### Coding is Largely Solved
Cherny asserts that for the type of programming he performs, the act of translating logic into syntax is no longer a bottleneck because AI agents like [[Claude Code]] can handle it. He notes that he has not hand-edited a single line of code since November, with 100% of his code generated by the agent. This shifts the human role from implementation to oversight and intent definition.

### The Rise of the "Builder"
The traditional title of "Software Engineer" is predicted to disappear, replaced by the role of the **"Builder"**. In this new paradigm, everyone becomes a product manager who codes. Cherny highlights that within the Claude Code team, roles overlap significantly (~50%), with PMs, designers, and finance staff all contributing code. This convergence suggests a future where technical specialization gives way to generalist product creation.

### Productivity and Adoption Metrics
*   **Productivity:** Engineering productivity per individual has increased by **200%**.
*   **Adoption:** As of the recording, **4%** of all GitHub commits are authored by Claude Code, with predictions that this will rise to **20%** (one-fifth) by the end of the year.

### Latent Demand in Product Development
Cherny introduces the concept of **[[Latent Demand in AI Products]]**, where user "abuse" or non-intended usage of a tool serves as a strong signal for feature development. Observing how users push boundaries to solve real problems helps identify where the product should evolve.

### Historical Analogy: The Printing Press
The current transition is compared to the invention of the **printing press**. Just as literacy moved from a specialized skill (scribes) to a universal basic skill (70% global literacy over 200 years), coding ability is becoming ubiquitous. This transition is expected to be disruptive and painful, requiring societal adaptation.

## Personal Workflow and Philosophy
*   **Mission-Driven Work:** Cherny emphasizes that mission alignment (specifically safety at Anthropic) is crucial for personal happiness and retention.
*   **Enjoyment of Coding:** While AI handles minutiae, some individuals (e.g., colleague Lena) still enjoy hand-coding (e.g., C++) as an art form. AI removes the *necessity* of manual coding but not the *option* for hobbyist satisfaction.
*   **Generalist Advantage:** Future success favors cross-disciplinary generalists who are curious and fluent in AI, rather than narrow specialists.
