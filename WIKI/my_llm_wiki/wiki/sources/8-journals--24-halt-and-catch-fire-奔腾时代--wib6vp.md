## Key Concepts

*   **Displacement (潜台词位移)**
    *   **Definition:** A dialogue technique where core emotional conflicts are projected onto neutral technical objects or mundane tasks rather than being stated directly.
    *   **Importance:** The central thesis of the source; explains how the show achieves emotional depth without melodrama.
    *   **Wiki Status:** Related to [[Subtext]] or [[Dialogue Writing]], but this specific application to tech-themed drama is unique.
*   **Voiceprint (语言指纹)**
    *   **Definition:** The unique linguistic signature of a character, defined by syntax, vocabulary, and rhythm, which evolves over time while maintaining core dramatic function.
    *   **Importance:** Provides a framework for analyzing character consistency and growth through speech patterns.
