# Top 100 American Screenwriters (Film & TV) and Profile of Jamie Pachino

This source document presents a ranked list of the top 100 most influential American screenwriters, combining both film and television writers. The ranking is based on WGA script lists, historical screenwriter rankings, and industry consensus, though the methodology is opaque and the list is best treated as a curated opinion rather than a definitive authority. The document also includes a separate, detailed profile of screenwriter and playwright [[Jamie Pachino]].

## Content Summary

The document is divided into two distinct sections:

1.  **Top 100 Ranking (1-50 detailed, 51-100 brief):** A list of screenwriters ranked by "influence," with representative works. The top 10 includes [[Billy Wilder]], [[Woody Allen]], [[William Goldman]], [[Aaron Sorkin]], [[Nora Ephron]], [[Charlie Kaufman]], [[Robert Towne]], [[Oliver Stone]], [[Francis Ford Coppola]], and [[James L. Brooks]]. The list notably mixes film and television writers, reflecting the modern crossover between mediums.

2.  **Profile of Jamie Pachino:** A detailed overview of [[Jamie Pachino]]'s career as a television writer/producer, film writer, and playwright. It lists her TV credits (e.g., *Chicago P.D.*, *Halt and Catch Fire*), theater works (e.g., *Splitting Infinity*), and awards (e.g., Joseph Jefferson Award). She is presented as a case study of an "American senior screenwriter."

## Key Caveats

- The ranking methodology is not transparent. The term "influence" is not defined.
- Some entries in the 51-100 range contain factual errors (e.g., Horton Foote is incorrectly credited with *Brokeback Mountain*; Steven Knight is incorrectly credited with *Shutter Island*).
- The Jamie Pachino profile is thematically related but not part of the top 100 ranking.
