# A-level 全景指南：定义、学科体系与自学资源

A comprehensive guide to the UK's Advanced Level qualification system. The source covers the definition and structure of A-level, its differences from China's Gaokao, the subject system, significance for personal development, and curated self-study resources by subject.

## Key Claims

- **A-level differs fundamentally from Gaokao** — specialization in 3-4 subjects over 2 years vs. breadth in 6 subjects over 3 days.
- **A-level shapes identity and career trajectory** — subject choices declare intellectual direction by age 16.
- **Three layers of significance**: cognitive (deep thinking training), identity (early specialization), social screening (university admissions).
- **Students without A-levels face higher dropout risk** — the 10% of UK university entrants bypassing A-level have double the average dropout rate.
- **Self-study priority should be: past papers > textbooks > videos** — mark scheme logic must be internalized through practice.
- **Detailed resource recommendations** for mathematics, further mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, economics, psychology, history, and English literature.
