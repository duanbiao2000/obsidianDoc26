# One Exercise to 10X Your Speaking

A 20‑minute YouTube video by English speaking coach **Alex Air** presenting the **Retelling Stories Exercise** as a single, high‑impact method to improve speaking fluency, vocabulary retention, and storytelling ability. The video claims the exercise can 5&#215;–10&#215; speaking ability when performed consistently.

## Rationale

- **Combined input + output** – The exercise forces the learner to first comprehend a text (input) and then immediately produce it (output), bridging the gap between passive recognition and active use.
- **Passive → active vocabulary transfer** – Highlighting unknown items and deliberately using them in retelling moves vocabulary from understanding-only to recall-and-production.

## The Core Checklist

1. Pick a text slightly above your level (5–10 new elements: words, phrases, or grammar structures).
2. Read (or listen to) the text.
3. Highlight everything you do not know.
4. Retell the story using all highlighted items.
5. Assess your performance.
6. If not satisfied, repeat once with the same text, then move on.

## Variations

- **Narrative variations**: Change the time frame (present/past/future) or the audience (child, colleagues, scholars).
- **Performance variations**: Apply time constraints (10 s / 30 s / 60 s) or speed doubling (2&#215; faster on second attempt) to train concision, pacing, and filler‑reduction.
- **Similar story variation**: After retelling the original, produce a thematically‑linked personal story or example – closest to real‑conversation practice.

## Quantifiable Goals

The video emphasises consistency over perfection: set a measurable target (e.g., 30–50 hours or 50–100 texts retold) and stick to it. Practicing with an accountability partner is also recommended.
