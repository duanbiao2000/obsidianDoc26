# Developing a Small 5 Acre Homestead to Feed Us in Uncertain Times

YouTube video by the channel [[Little Spanish Farmstead]], published March 15, 2026. Hosted by Harriet and Mauro, a couple who purchased an abandoned 5-acre smallholding in Castellón, Spain in February 2021. The video documents a single work period in early spring, covering garden tasks, tree restoration work, and their philosophy around food self-sufficiency.

## Key Techniques

- **Straw bale ground clearing** — Passive method using straw bales to suppress vegetation without baking soil, superior to plastic mulch in hot, dry climates.
- **Intercropping / layered planting** — Planting fast annuals (radishes, lettuce, strawberries) in perennial beds (asparagus) during establishment phase.
- **Determinate vs. indeterminate potato varieties** — [[Alouette]] (determinate) does not require heavy hilling; tubers form in one layer.
- **Raised bed construction with bartered logs** — Exchange vegetables for materials.

## Philosophy

Food self-sufficiency framed as a response to global uncertainty: "The world is going to need many, many more people working small plots of land growing food for themselves and their immediate community."
