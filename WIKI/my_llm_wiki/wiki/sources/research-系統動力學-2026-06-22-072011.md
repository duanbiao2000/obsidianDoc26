# Research: System Dynamics

This source provides a comprehensive overview of **System Dynamics (SD)**, a computer-aided approach to strategy development, policy analysis, and design. It details the historical origins at MIT under Jay W. Forrester, core methodological concepts such as endogenous perspective, feedback loops, stocks, flows, and time delays, and key applications in urban planning, ecology, economics, and healthcare. The document also highlights modern developments including Group Model Building (GMB) and hybrid computational techniques integrating SD with AI and Agent-Based Modeling.
