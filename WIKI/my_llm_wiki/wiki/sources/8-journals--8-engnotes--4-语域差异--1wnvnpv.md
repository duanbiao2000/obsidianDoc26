# Register Differences in English: Phrasal Verbs vs. Single Verbs for IELTS

This source document provides a comprehensive reference for understanding and applying **register differences** in English vocabulary, specifically framed for [[雅思]] (IELTS) preparation. It argues that the distinction between phrasal verbs and single-word verbs is fundamentally a **register difference**, not a semantic one.

## Core Argument

The document's central claim is that phrasal verbs (Germanic origin) are informal/spoken, while single-word verbs (Latin/French origin) are formal/written. This is presented as a generative rule that learners can apply to choose appropriate vocabulary for different contexts.

## Key Content

- **Etymology Rule:** Germanic origin words → informal (phrasal verbs); Latin/French origin words → formal (single verbs)
- **Scenario Recommendations:** Academic papers → single verbs; business emails → single verbs; daily conversation → phrasal verbs; literature → mixed
- **Practical Advice for IELTS:** Speaking → prioritize phrasal verbs for naturalness; Writing (formal) → use single verbs for professionalism
- **Common Pitfall:** Chinese learners overuse phrasal verbs in writing (sounds informal) and overuse single verbs in speaking (sounds stiff)
