# FSI's Pattern Drill Methodology

This source outlines a high-intermediate self-study material based on the [[FSI Pattern Drill Methodology]]. It emphasizes "automatic language reflection" through structured repetition of sentence frames rather than rote memorization. The methodology is divided into four specific drill types: [[Substitution Drill]], [[Transformation Drill]], [[Response Drill]], and [[Expansion Drill]].

The document provides a concrete 15-30 minute daily training plan integrating these drills with AI tools like ChatGPT, Grammarly, and ElevenLabs to generate practice variations, check structural accuracy, and provide pronunciation models. It targets workplace and academic scenarios for high-intermediate learners.

## Key Components

*   **Warm-Up:** Activates target structures using complex clauses and collocations.
*   **Module 1 (Substitution Drill):** Focuses on mastering flexible use of complex sentence frames by replacing variables.
*   **Module 2 (Transformation Drill):** Involves converting sentences between advanced grammatical structures (e.g., active/passive, inversion, nominalization) while preserving meaning.
*   **Module 3 (Response Drill):** Builds instant conversational reflexes using pre-defined response frameworks in specific scenarios.
*   **Module 4 (Expansion Drill):** Iteratively adds logical layers (conditions, data, counterpoints) to simple core ideas to create coherent complex expressions. This directly parallels the concept of [[Progressive Sentence Expansion]].
