# Journals/Claude Code 反思笔记

This journal entry reflects on the phenomenological and psychological experience of using [[Claude Code]]. Rather than focusing solely on technical utility, the author explores the interaction as a form of pure, transparent communication and a mirror for self-dialogue.

## Key Insights

*   **AI-Human Interaction as Pure Communication:** The interaction strips away social cues (tone, expression, hidden agendas), leaving only text that represents a direct thinking process. This is contrasted with the complexity and potential incompleteness of human social interaction.
*   **Self-Dialogue and Externalized Thinking:** The questions posed to the AI are often internal questions the user is asking themselves. The AI’s responses serve to clarify thoughts the user already possessed but had not yet articulated.
*   **New Loneliness and New Understanding:** The experience creates a paradoxical emotional state—a sense of isolation ("new loneliness") combined with a unique form of clarity and being "understood" ("new understanding").

## Context

The reflection takes place in a solitary setting (late night, rain, cold coffee), emphasizing the introspective nature of the engagement. The author references Haruki Murakami's *Norwegian Wood* to contextualize feelings of loneliness and the inherent limitations of human-to-human communication.
