# Research: 心理訓練 (Mental Training)

This source provides a comprehensive overview of **心理訓練 (Mental Training)** as a systematic discipline for enhancing psychological performance. It synthesizes content from multiple Chinese-language encyclopedia entries, academic platforms, and institutional sources to define the field, its principles, methods, and applications.

## Key Content

### Definition and Scope
Mental training is defined as a systematic psychological intervention that uses specialized instruments, methods, or exercises to purposefully change an individual's psychological state, aiming for optimal performance. It is distinguished from [[心理諮詢或心理治療]] (psychological counseling/psychotherapy) by its knowledge base (psychology vs. medicine), operational model (sports training vs. medical treatment), and target population (normal/high-performers vs. patients).

### Core Principles
The source identifies principles shared with physical training (active participation, overload, gradual progression, continuity, specificity, individualization, comprehensiveness) and principles unique to psychological training (context-specificity, experiential learning).

### Main Methods
- **[[心理技能訓練]] (Psychological Skills Training — PST)**: A three-phase framework (Education → Acquisition → Practice) for systematic mental skill development.
- **[[意象訓練]] (Imagery/Mental Rehearsal)**: Internal vs. external imagery for skill refinement.
- **[[自我暗示和放鬆訓練]] (Autogenic Training)**: Relaxation and activation phases; theoretical basis noted as incomplete.
- **[[模擬訓練]] (Simulation Training)**: Language-based and real-scenario simulation for performance preparation.
- **[[目標設定]] (Goal Setting)**: SMART framework with emphasis on process/performance goals over outcome goals.
- **[[專注力訓練]] (Attention Training)**: Five-type taxonomy (focused, sustained, selective, divided, switching).
- **[[正念練習]] (Mindfulness)**: Structured daily practice (e.g., Lucidbook's 7-minute/14-day protocol).
- **[[腦波神經回饋訓練]] (Neurofeedback)**: Non-invasive brainwave training for ADHD and performance.
- **[[心理危機干預]] (Psychological Crisis Intervention)**: Immediate support to prevent mental breakdown.
- **[[虛擬現實訓練]] (VR Training)**: Emerging technology for phobia treatment and simulation.

### Application Domains
- **Sports**: Elite athletes (e.g., [[Iga Swiatek]]) using sport psychologists.
- **Aerospace**: [[Yang Liwei]]'s heart rate data as evidence of training effectiveness.
- **Military**: PRC military training center (2005), [[總參工程兵學院]].
- **Education**: Children's attention training, ADHD interventions.
- **Public Security & Special Professions**: Industry-specific psychological training.

### Professional Roles
- **[[運動心理人員]] (Sport Psychologist)**: Professional role in athlete development.
- **[[心理指導老師]] (Psychological Guidance Teacher)**: Distinct from therapist; focuses on "adding excellence" rather than "treating pathology."

### Controversies and Limitations
- Transfer problem: classroom-trained skills may not generalize to real contexts.
- Individualization vs. comprehensiveness tension.
- Overload principle vs. gradual progression tension.
- Autogenic training's unproven theoretical basis.
- No permanent effects ("use it or lose it").
- Insufficient professional training pipelines.
