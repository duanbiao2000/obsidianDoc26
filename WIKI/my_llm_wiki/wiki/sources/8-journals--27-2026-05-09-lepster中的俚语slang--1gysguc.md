# LEPster中的俚语Slang: Curated Idiomatic Expressions from Luke's English Podcast

This source is a curated list of 27 "quasi-slang / spoken expressions" extracted from an episode of [[Luke's English Podcast]]. The expressions were selected for their authenticity, usefulness, and transferability to the learner's own spoken English. Each entry includes the original sentence from the video transcript, a definition, and two example sentences demonstrating usage in relevant contexts (IELTS, language learning, daily conversation).

## Key Expressions

The list covers a range of idiomatic and colloquial expressions, including:

- **Comfort zone / Step out of your comfort zone** — Doing something that causes mild anxiety but enables growth.
- **Get to grips with** — Gradually understand or master something difficult.
- **Babbling away** — Talking continuously and fluently (informal, slightly playful).
- **Get the knack for / Have a knack for** — Learn the trick of something / be naturally good at it.
- **Resign oneself from the task** — Give up entirely, accept failure (more formal than "give up").
- **No-stress approach / Not rush into** — Learning without anxiety or internal pressure.
- **Cram / Cram study** — Last-minute memorization before a test.
- **Enjoy the ride** — Focus on the process, not just the destination.
- **Keep an open mind** — Avoid prejudging methods or viewpoints.
- **Soak up like a sponge** — Absorb language naturally through exposure.
- **Wading through the swamp** — Struggling through complex or dense material.
- **Concrete strategies / Tell me exactly what to do** — Specific, actionable methods vs. vague advice.
- **Keep an eye on** — Monitor progress informally.
- **Trip off your tongue** — Words come out naturally and effortlessly.
- **Take an active part** — Participate actively, not passively.
- **Brush up on** — Review or refresh a skill not used recently.
- **On the fly / On the go** — Improvise in real time / learn while moving.
- **Think outside the box** — Be creative, break conventional patterns.
- **In the pipeline** — In planning or production, not yet released.
- **Long gone** — Something that no longer exists or is far in the past.
- **Shout out some money / Shell out money** — Spend money (reluctantly).
- **It's not rocket science** — Something is not that difficult.
- **Who knew...?** — Ironic or sarcastic rhetorical device.
- **Nail something / Get it just right** — Do something perfectly.
- **Long haul** — Long-term process, not a quick fix.
- **Stakeholder** — Someone with a vested interest (used semi-jokingly).
- **Unique drama / Voyage of discovery** — Metaphor for learning as an unfolding, dramatic journey.
