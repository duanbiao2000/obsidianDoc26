# Mnemonics

Mnemonics are memory aids that use associations, acronyms, or imagery to improve recall. They are rated **C Tier** (Table 1) and **B Tier** (Table 2) in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List," with conflicting ratings.

## Types

- **Acronyms** — e.g., PEMDAS (order of operations).
- **Acrostics** — e.g., "My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Nine Pizzas" (planets).
- **Method of Loci** — associating items with locations.
- **Keyword method** — linking a foreign word to a familiar word via an image.
- **Rhymes and songs** — e.g., "I before E except after C."

## Limitations

- **Surface-level encoding** — Mnemonics help recall but may not deepen understanding.
- **Limited scope** — Best for lists and facts, less useful for complex concepts.
- **Extra cognitive load** — Learning the mnemonic itself requires effort.
