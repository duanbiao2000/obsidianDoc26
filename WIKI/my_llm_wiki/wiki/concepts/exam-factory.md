# Exam Factory

"Exam factory" is a criticism that UK schools prioritize measurable exam performance over genuine learning and creativity. This concept is closely linked to [[teaching-to-the-test]], where pedagogical approaches focus on maximizing exam scores rather than fostering deep understanding.

The exam factory model is driven by [[high-stakes-assessments]] (GCSEs at 16, A levels at 18) that determine university access, creating a [[results-driven-culture]] that encourages short-term memorization over long-term understanding. Critics argue this leads to burnout, narrow definitions of success, and the squeezing out of creative subjects.
