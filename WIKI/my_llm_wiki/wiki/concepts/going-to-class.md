# Going to Class

Going to class refers to attending lectures without active engagement strategies. It is rated **C Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Why It Is Low-Rated

- **Passive listening** — Without active engagement, information is poorly retained.
- **Illusion of understanding** — Hearing a lecture can feel like learning, but comprehension may be shallow.
- **Variable quality** — Lecture quality varies significantly by instructor.

## How to Improve

- **Prime before class** — Preview material to activate prior knowledge.
- **Take active notes** — Use Cornell or other structured note-taking methods.
- **Ask questions** — Engage with the material and instructor.
- **Review after class** — Consolidate notes and fill gaps.
