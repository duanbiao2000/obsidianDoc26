# Collocation Extraction Methodology

A systematic approach to extracting high‑frequency collocations from authentic video and film sources for IELTS preparation. The source demonstrates extraction from multiple genres:

- YouTube English teaching videos (e.g., Ben's meta‑discourse)
- Technical talks (Google DESIGN.md, programming interviews)
- Game development lectures (CS50 + Love2D)
- Career‑progression narratives (open‑source, engineering)
- Film dialogue (Breaking Bad, The Terminal)

## Procedure

1. Identify domain‑specific expressions in their natural context
2. Classify by rhetorical register (standardisation, analysis, intuition, technical, career‑progression, language‑learning, film‑derived)
3. Create bilingual tables with usage contexts
4. Adapt for IELTS via register transformation
5. Embed in model paragraphs and Q&A sets

## Output

The methodology produced 24 core collocations, 25 adapted spoken phrases, 22 film‑derived expressions, and a 52‑item general expression list, all with IELTS adaptation notes.
