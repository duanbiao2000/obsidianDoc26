# Varied Exposure / Intensive Reading-Listening Loop

This concept refers to a learning cycle where the learner encounters the same content through multiple modalities: reading the text, listening to the audio, and then reading while listening. This repetitive, varied exposure helps cement vocabulary and structure. It is discussed in the free voluntary reading video as a technique that leverages tools like [[lingq]].
