# Watching Videos and Lectures

Watching videos and lectures is a passive learning format where the learner consumes pre-recorded or live content. It is rated **D Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List" (Table 2).

## Why It Is Low-Rated

- **Passive consumption** — No active engagement or retrieval.
- **Illusion of learning** — Familiarity with content feels like understanding.
- **Variable quality** — Content quality varies widely.
- **Low retention** — Information is quickly forgotten without active processing.

## How to Improve

- **Take active notes** — Use [[cornell-note-taking]] or other structured methods.
- **Pause and recall** — Stop periodically to summarize what you've learned.
- **Ask questions** — Engage with the content critically.
- **Combine with active techniques** — Use the video as a source for [[active-recall]] or [[feynman-technique]].
