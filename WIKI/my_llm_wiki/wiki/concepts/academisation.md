# Academisation

Academisation refers to post-2008 reforms in the UK that moved schools away from local authority control toward greater autonomy. This policy aimed to raise standards by giving schools more freedom over curriculum, staffing, and budgets.

However, critics argue that academisation increased fragmentation and inequality, as schools with more resources and capacity could thrive while others struggled. Alongside [[free-schools]], academisation is seen as contributing to the [[two-tier-system]] and raising questions about whether these reforms genuinely improve quality or simply shift responsibility.
