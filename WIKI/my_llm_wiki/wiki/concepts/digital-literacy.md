# Digital Literacy

**Digital Literacy** is defined as the ability to understand, use, and apply digital technologies effectively. In the context of the [[Future Skills Framework]] by [[Bernard Marr]], it is identified as one of the four foundational technical skills required for success in a digital world.

While often considered a baseline requirement, digital literacy goes beyond basic computer usage. It involves a functional understanding of how digital tools operate, how to navigate digital environments safely, and how to leverage technology to solve problems. It is distinct from, but closely related to, [[Data Literacy]], which focuses specifically on the interpretation and analysis of data.

In an era of rapid technological change, digital literacy is a prerequisite for acquiring more specialized [[Technical Skills]] and maintaining [[Digital Threat Awareness]].
