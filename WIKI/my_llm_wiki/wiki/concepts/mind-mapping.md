# Mind Mapping

Mind mapping is a visual diagramming technique where concepts and their relationships are organized around a central idea. It is rated **A Tier** (Table 1) and **C Tier** (Table 2) in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List," with conflicting ratings.

## Structure

- **Central idea** — The main topic in the center.
- **Branches** — Major subtopics radiating outward.
- **Sub-branches** — Details and connections.
- **Keywords and images** — Concise labels and visual cues.

## Why the Conflicting Ratings?

The discrepancy may reflect different use cases:
- **A Tier** — For brainstorming, organizing ideas, and creative thinking.
- **C Tier** — For review and exam preparation, where it may be less effective than active recall.
