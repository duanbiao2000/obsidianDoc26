# Practice Problems / Practice Tests

Practice problems and practice tests are learning techniques that involve solving problems or taking practice exams to reinforce knowledge and identify gaps. They are rated **A Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Mechanism

Practice problems engage **active recall** and **retrieval practice**, forcing the learner to apply knowledge rather than passively review it. They also provide **feedback** on what has been mastered and what needs further study.

## Types

- **Closed-book practice** — solving problems without notes (strongest retrieval practice).
- **Open-book practice** — solving problems with reference materials (useful for application).
- **Timed practice** — simulating exam conditions.
- **Untimed practice** — focusing on understanding and problem-solving process.
