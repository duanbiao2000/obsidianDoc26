# Natural English Playbook

The Natural English Playbook is a pedagogical framework that defines natural spoken American English as a **system of 10 observable, learnable discourse and pragmatic behaviors**. It challenges the common assumption that ESL learners' primary bottleneck is vocabulary size, arguing instead that the missing element is an **operational standard for what "sounds like a real person."**

## Core Thesis

ESL learners often possess sufficient vocabulary but produce speech that sounds stiff, formal, or robotic because they apply written-language standards to spoken contexts. The playbook provides a systematic alternative: a set of behavioral rules that, when applied together, produce speech that native speakers perceive as natural.

## The 10 Rules

The framework organizes naturalness into 10 dimensions:

1. **[[cushioning-requests]]** — Adding modals or softeners before requests
2. **[[contractions-as-standard]]** — Using contractions always in casual speech
3. **[[filler-words-for-thinking-time]]** — Using fillers to buy processing time
4. **[[spoken-vocabulary-register]]** — Replacing formal words with everyday equivalents
5. **[[listener-check-ins]]** — Tag questions and hedges that loop in the listener
6. **[[verbal-reactivity]]** — Responding audibly and immediately to show engagement
7. **[[certainty-downgrading]]** — Softening claims with hedges and modals
8. **[[spoken-grammar]]** — Using sentence-final prepositions, starting with So/But/And
9. **[[narrative-structure-spoken]]** — Telling stories with discourse markers instead of enumerated lists
10. **[[energy-matching]]** — Calibrating delivery to the conversational context

## Key Principles

- **Systematicity:** The rules are not isolated tips but a coherent system covering pragmatics, syntax, discourse, and prosody
- **Contrastive learning:** Each rule is demonstrated via ❌/✅ pairs showing the transformation
- **Register awareness:** The framework distinguishes casual, conversational, and polished spoken registers
- **Practical implementability:** The playbook includes an AI prompt for automated practice

## Relationship to Other Approaches

| Approach | Focus | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Natural English Playbook | Pragmatic/discourse competence | Output-driven, rule-based |
| Shadowing (Connor) | Pronunciation, fluency | Input-driven, repetition-based |
| Traditional ESL | Grammar, vocabulary | Textbook-driven, written-language norms |

## Limitations

- Describes a specific **American** conversational style; generalizability to other English varieties is unclear
- Does not address when to deliberately avoid these patterns (formal presentations, academic writing)
- Lacks self-assessment rubrics or milestone structures
- The 30%/70% vocabulary/delivery split is a rhetorical simplification without empirical support
