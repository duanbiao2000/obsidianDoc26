# American Screenwriters

This concept page covers the domain of American screenwriting, particularly the ranking and recognition of influential screenwriters in both film and television. The source document "Top 100 American Screenwriters" provides a subjective ranking based on WGA lists, historical rankings, and industry consensus.

## The Ranking

The list ranks 100 screenwriters by "influence," mixing film and television writers. The top 10 includes [[Billy Wilder]], [[Woody Allen]], [[William Goldman]], [[Aaron Sorkin]], [[Nora Ephron]], [[Charlie Kaufman]], [[Robert Towne]], [[Oliver Stone]], [[Francis Ford Coppola]], and [[James L. Brooks]].

### Caveats

- The ranking methodology is opaque and subjective.
- Some entries in the 51-100 range contain factual errors (e.g., Horton Foote incorrectly credited with *Brokeback Mountain*; Steven Knight incorrectly credited with *Shutter Island*).
- The list should be treated as a reference point, not a definitive authority.

## Film vs. Television Crossover

A notable feature of the list is its inclusion of writers who have worked across both film and television, reflecting the modern blurring of these mediums. Examples include [[Aaron Sorkin]], [[Vince Gilligan]], and [[Joss Whedon]].
