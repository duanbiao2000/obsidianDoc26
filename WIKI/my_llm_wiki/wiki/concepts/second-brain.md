# Second Brain

A second brain is an external system that captures, organizes, and surfaces knowledge so your biological brain can focus on using information rather than storing it. The concept addresses the fundamental problem of retrieval: traditional note apps are "passive storage" that require you to remember to search, whereas a second brain actively surfaces relevant information when needed.

## Core Principles

1. **Capture is not the problem — retrieval is.** Most people save information everywhere but cannot find it later.
2. **The system must have two abilities:** to think about your information (processing) and to remember it permanently in a linked, searchable way (storage).
3. **Value is delayed but compounds.** The system feels like extra work at first but becomes increasingly powerful as the knowledge base grows.

## The NotebookLM + Obsidian Implementation

The source describes a specific implementation combining [[NotebookLM]] (thinking/processing) with [[Obsidian]] (remembering/storage). The workflow involves:

1. Research in NotebookLM with strategic questioning
2. Rewriting insights in your own words as linked notes in Obsidian
3. Feeding Obsidian notes back into NotebookLM for meta-analysis (pattern discovery)

This creates a three-layer system: thinking → remembering → meta-thinking.
