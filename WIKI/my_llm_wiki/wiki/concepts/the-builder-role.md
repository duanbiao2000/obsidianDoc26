# The Builder Role

The **Builder Role** is a proposed professional identity that replaces the traditional "Software Engineer" in an AI-augmented workflow. Coined by [[Boris Cherny]], Head of [[Claude Code]], this concept describes a shift where technical implementation is automated, and human value derives from product definition, oversight, and cross-disciplinary execution.

## Core Characteristics
*   **Convergence of Roles:** Builders combine skills traditionally separated into Product Management, Engineering, and Design. In AI-native teams, role overlap can reach ~50%.
*   **Generalist Focus:** Success favors cross-disciplinary curiosity and fluency with AI tools over narrow technical specialization.
*   **Product-Centric Coding:** Coding becomes a means to an end (product creation) rather than the primary craft. Everyone codes, but not everyone is a "software engineer" in the traditional sense.

## Implications
*   **Title Obsolescence:** The title "Software Engineer" may fade as the distinction between "those who code" and "those who build products" disappears.
*   **Skill Shift:** Value moves from syntax mastery to system architecture, prompt engineering, and understanding user needs ([[Latent Demand in AI Products]]).
*   **Education:** Training may shift from deep computer science fundamentals to broader product development and AI interaction skills.

## Context
This concept emerges from the observation that AI agents like [[Claude Code]] have solved the "minutia" of coding, allowing humans to focus on higher-level intent. It aligns with the broader trend of [[AI Judgment vs. Execution]], where AI handles execution and humans handle judgment.
