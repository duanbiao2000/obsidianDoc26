# Verifiable Capability

**Verifiable Capability** is a career strategy that shifts focus from credential-based hiring (degrees, certificates) to evidence-based hiring (GitHub repositories, live demos, public projects). It posits that the most effective way to enter the tech industry is to simulate the job immediately using AI tools, creating artifacts that demonstrate professional competence.

## Key Characteristics

*   **Simulation Advantage**: Unlike medicine or architecture, software engineering allows individuals to simulate professional work environments at home using AI agents and coding tools.
*   **Behavior → Identity**: Professional identity is formed through the act of doing the work, not just studying it.
*   **Public Proof**: Skills are demonstrated through public outputs ("Learn in Public"), allowing employers to assess actual capability rather than potential.

## Application

*   **Work as Learning**: Engaging in real-world projects as the primary method of learning, rather than completing courses before starting work.
*   **Weekly Builds**: Creating and sharing at least one running demo per week to build a portfolio of verifiable work.
*   **AI-Augmented Workflow**: Using tools like **Cursor** to accelerate the creation of complex prototypes, demonstrating system thinking and execution speed.
