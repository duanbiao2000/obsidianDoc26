# IELTS Vocabulary Reuse Strategy

The **IELTS Vocabulary Reuse Strategy** is a framework for efficiently building vocabulary across the four IELTS skills by prioritizing domains that transfer across multiple skills. It classifies vocabulary domains by their reusability level and provides a recommended preparation path.

## Reusability Classification

### High Reusability (Four Skills Universal)
These domains appear in all four skills and should receive the highest priority:

- Education
- Healthcare
- Environment
- Technology
- Social Culture

### Medium Reusability (Speaking + Writing Shared)
These domains are primarily relevant to Speaking Part 3 and Writing Task 2:

- Urbanization
- Globalization
- Mental Health
- Crime & Law
- Media

### Low Reusability (Skill-Specific)
These domains are exclusive to one skill and require dedicated preparation:

- **Listening only:** Life service scenarios (renting, shopping, dining, banking, community services)
- **Reading only:** Academic science topics (history, archaeology, astronomy, linguistics, evolution)
- **Speaking only:** Personal experience topics (Part 1/2: hometown, habits, preferences, childhood memories)

## Recommended Preparation Path

The most efficient path is to build a core vocabulary bank around Speaking+Writing social issue topics (high and medium reusability), then supplement:

1. **Primary investment:** High reusability domains (education, healthcare, environment, technology, social culture)
2. **Secondary investment:** Medium reusability domains (urbanization, globalization, mental health, crime, media)
3. **Skill-specific supplementation:** Add life service words for Listening, academic science words for Reading, personal experience words for Speaking Part 1/2

## Key Principle

Do not prepare vocabulary independently for each skill. The same vocabulary bank can be reused across skills, but depth requirements vary. For example, "environment" vocabulary appears in all four skills, but Listening requires recognition only, while Writing requires active production with precise collocations.
