# Hyperfocus

**Hyperfocus** describes a state of intense fixation on a specific task or stimulus. While often confused with [[flow-state]], hyperfocus is distinct in its nature and implications.

Unlike flow, which is adaptive, balanced, and enjoyable, hyperfocus can be rigid and maladaptive. It is commonly associated with neurodivergent conditions such as ADHD and autism. In hyperfocus, individuals may lose track of time and neglect other responsibilities, impairing broader task management and awareness. Recognizing this distinction is crucial for accurate application in productivity and educational contexts.
