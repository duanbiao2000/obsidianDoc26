# Cognitive Load Management

**Cognitive Load Management** refers to the strategies used to regulate the amount of information processed by the working memory to prevent overload and ensure effective learning. When cognitive load exceeds capacity, learners experience "mental clutter," "hitting a wall," or "brain shutdown," rendering further study ineffective.

## Strategies

### 1. Chunking
Breaking down complex information into smaller, manageable units ([[Chunking]]). The source [[8-journals--35-how-to-actually-make-new-info-stick--cxmvk1|How to Actually Make New Info Stick]] recommends limiting chunks to **2-3 concepts at a time** to avoid overwhelming the brain.

### 2. Shrinking Focus
When faced with a large volume of information (e.g., 10+ concepts), deliberately reduce the scope of attention. Focus on mastering small pieces before attempting to merge them into a bigger picture.

### 3. Modeling Mode
Switching to active visualization ([[Modeling Mode (Learning Strategy)]]) when passive absorption fails. This helps identify [[negative-transfer]] and ensures accurate schema construction.

### 4. Grounding Abstract Concepts
Translating academic jargon into plain English or real-world analogies reduces the decoding burden on working memory, allowing more resources for conceptual understanding.

## Signs of Overload

*   Inability to retain new information.
*   Feeling of "mental clutter" or confusion.
*   Physical or mental fatigue ("brain fried").
*   Forced or awkward analogies indicating [[negative-transfer]].

## Relation to Other Concepts

*   **[[Desirable Difficulty]]:** Cognitive load management seeks to maintain an optimal level of difficulty—challenging enough to promote learning but not so high as to cause shutdown.
*   **[[Zone of Proximal Development]]:** Effective load management keeps tasks within the ZPD, where guidance and scaffolding (like chunking) can facilitate success.
*   **[[Flow State]]:** Properly managed cognitive load is a prerequisite for entering [[Flow State]], where challenge and skill are balanced.
