# Two-Tier System (UK Education)

The UK education system is characterized by a two-tier system dividing elite private schools (independent schools) from state-funded schools. Private schools provide advantages beyond academics, including social networks, confidence, and access to opportunities. Private school fees start around £30,000 per year, creating a significant barrier to access.

This division raises questions about social mobility and whether the system is genuinely meritocratic or designed to benefit certain groups from the beginning. The two-tier system is a central theme in debates about education inequality in the UK.
