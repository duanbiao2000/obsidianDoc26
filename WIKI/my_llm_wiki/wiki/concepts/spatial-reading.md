# Spatial Reading

Spatial reading means taking in the entire structure of a book at a glance — a bird’s‑eye view — instead of sequential word‑by‑word processing. Visual summaries enable spatial reading, allowing the reader to see both the overall architecture and the individual ideas. This concept is introduced in the visual learning video and is a key benefit of [[chunking-with-visuals]].
