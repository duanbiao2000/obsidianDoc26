# Transition Adaptability

**Transition Adaptability** is the ability to cope with structural changes in one's educational or professional life, such as changing schools, switching jobs, experiencing unemployment, or undergoing reskiling. It is a core component of **Career Management Skills (CMS)**.

## Importance

In a rapidly changing labor market, the ability to adapt to transitions is as critical as technical skills. Transition adaptability involves:
*   **Resilience:** Maintaining psychological stability during uncertainty.
*   **Flexibility:** Adjusting goals and plans in response to new information or circumstances.
*   **Proactive Learning:** Identifying and acquiring new skills needed for the next phase.

## Connection to Other Concepts

*   **[[Psychological Resilience]]:** Transition adaptability relies heavily on emotional regulation and stress management.
*   **[[Migration Psychology]]:** For migrants, transition adaptability is crucial for navigating cultural and professional shifts in a new country.
*   **[[Career Management Skills]]:** It is one of the ten core domains in the Finnish CMS framework, highlighting that adaptability is a learnable skill, not just a personality trait.
