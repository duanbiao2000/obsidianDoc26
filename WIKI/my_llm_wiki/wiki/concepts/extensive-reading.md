# Extensive / Free Voluntary Reading

Extensive reading, also called free voluntary reading, is a language learning method where learners read large quantities of material for pleasure with limited dictionary lookups. The transition extraction document identifies vocabulary as the main obstacle to this approach. The concept is associated with linguist [[stephen-krashen]] and the [[tadoku-four-golden-rules]] method. The analysis notes that adults may handle 15–20% unknown words, that reading with audio support (e.g., [[lingq]]) aids acquisition, and that blending intensive and extensive reading may be more effective for beginners.
