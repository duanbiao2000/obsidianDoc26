# Summary Pages

Summary pages are condensed notes that capture key points from a topic or source. They are rated **C Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Why They Are Low-Rated

- **Passive creation** — Summarizing can become a mechanical transcription exercise.
- **No retrieval** — Creating summaries from notes rather than memory.
- **Shallow processing** — May focus on listing facts rather than understanding relationships.

## How to Improve

- **Write from memory** — Create summaries without looking at notes ([[blurting]]).
- **Synthesize** — Connect ideas across sources rather than listing facts.
- **Use as a review tool** — Test yourself against the summary.
- **Combine with active recall** — Use the summary as a cue sheet for retrieval practice.
