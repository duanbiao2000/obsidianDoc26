# Priming / Pre-study

Priming (also called pre-study) is a learning technique that involves previewing material before deep study to activate prior knowledge and prepare the brain for learning. It is rated **A Tier** (Table 1) and **B Tier** (Table 2) in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Mechanism

Priming activates relevant **schemas** and prior knowledge, making it easier to connect new information during subsequent study. It creates a mental framework that helps organize incoming information.

## Examples

- Skimming a chapter before reading it in depth.
- Reviewing key terms and concepts before a lecture.
- Looking at diagrams and headings before reading the text.
- Watching a short overview video before a detailed lesson.
