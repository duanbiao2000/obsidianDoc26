# It's Not Just X, It's Y (Sentence Structure)

The **"It's not just X, it's Y"** structure is a comparative/progressive sentence pattern that naturally provides contrast and depth in spoken and written English. It is identified as a core high-scoring structure for IELTS speaking, particularly in Part 3.

## Why It Works

The structure inherently contains **contrast and progression** — two elements that underpin high-scoring answers in both IELTS speaking and writing. It allows the speaker to acknowledge a surface-level point (X) while introducing a deeper insight (Y), demonstrating analytical thinking.

## Template

```
It's not just [surface level], it's [deeper level].
```

## IELTS Examples

**Question**: Do you think social media has changed the way people communicate?

> "Well, I think it's **not just** changed the tools we use to communicate — like, we went from phone calls to messages and videos. It's **actually changed** the way we *think* about communication itself. People now expect instant replies. Silence has become a kind of message."

**Question**: Is it important to learn a foreign language?

> "For me, learning English has been **not just** about memorizing vocabulary or grammar rules. It's **more about** seeing the world through a different lens. When you think in another language, you sometimes realize that certain feelings don't have a direct translation — and that tells you something about the limits of your own thinking."
