# Transformation Drill

A **Transformation Drill** is a type of [[FSI Pattern Drill Methodology]] that involves converting a sentence from one grammatical structure to another while preserving its original meaning.

## Objective

To target advanced grammatical manipulation and deepen understanding of syntactic relationships.

## Common Transformations

*   **Active to Passive Voice:** Shifting focus from the agent to the recipient of the action.
*   **Inversion:** Using negative adverbials or conditional structures to emphasize certain elements.
*   **Nominalization:** Converting verbs or adjectives into nouns to create more formal or concise expressions.

## Example

Original: *"Experts advocate that cities prioritize green infrastructure..."*
Passive: *"It is advocated by experts that green infrastructure be prioritized by cities..."*
