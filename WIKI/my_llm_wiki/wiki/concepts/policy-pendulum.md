# Policy Pendulum

The Policy Pendulum is a mental model describing the pattern of frequent education reforms swinging between opposing priorities. In the UK, this is evident in the shift from local authority control to [[academisation]] and [[free-schools]], and in ongoing debates about exam standards, curriculum breadth, and school accountability.

This pattern can create instability and fragmentation, as schools must constantly adapt to new policies. The Policy Pendulum reflects deeper societal tensions about the purpose of education and the kind of society people want to build.
