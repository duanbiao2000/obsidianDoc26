# Chunking with Visuals

Chunking with visuals is a technique that uses visual summaries (diagrams, mind maps, sketchnotes) to compress chapters, ideas, or entire books into manageable chunks that fit within the 4‑item limit of [[working-memory-limit|working memory]]. The visual learning video advocates this as a method to overcome information overwhelm and leverage the [[picture-superiority-effect]] and [[dual-coding]] for better retention.
