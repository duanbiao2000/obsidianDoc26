# Connected Speech Patterns for IELTS

A set of pronunciation features identified in the collocation source as IELTS "bonus points" for natural delivery:

- **Glottal stop**: Replacement of /t/ in certain environments (e.g., "button" → /ˈbʌʔən/)
- **-ing reduction**: Pronunciation of -ing as /ɪn/ (e.g., "going" → "goin'")
- **Pronoun initial consonant dropping**: e.g., "give him" → "give 'im"

## Relevance for IELTS

These patterns help test‑takers sound more natural in the Speaking test, demonstrating awareness of authentic connected speech rather than careful citation form pronunciation. The source notes that these are context‑dependent and should be used judiciously.
