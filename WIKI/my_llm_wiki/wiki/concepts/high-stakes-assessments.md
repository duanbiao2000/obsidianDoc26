# High-Stakes Assessments

High-stakes assessments are exams with significant consequences for students' futures. In the UK education system, GCSEs (taken at age 16) and A levels (taken at age 18) determine university access and career opportunities.

These assessments create a [[results-driven-culture]] that encourages [[teaching-to-the-test]] and short-term memorization over deep understanding. While they provide objective fairness and clarity, critics argue they create intense pressure, narrow definitions of success, and contribute to the [[exam-factory]] model. The impact of high-stakes exams on mental health is a significant concern.
