# C1 Advanced Proposal Writing

A structured approach to writing Proposals for the C1 Advanced Writing paper Part 2. The proposal genre focuses on arguing for a future course of action, with the primary temporal orientation being the future.

## Template Structure

### 1. Title
- Format: **Proposal to [action] + [object]**
- Example: *Proposal to Preserve the Old Central Library*
- Invent a specific name to make the scenario more realistic

### 2. Introduction
- Identify the subject (building/project/course) and its current status
- State the dual purpose: justify preservation/change and suggest how to modernize
- Template: *This proposal aims to explain why [subject] should be preserved and suggest ways in which it could be modernised and used.*

### 3. "Why It Is Worth Preserving" Paragraph
- Present 2–3 angles:
  - Historical or architectural value
  - Emotional significance or collective memory for local people
  - Strategic location advantages

### 4. "How to Modernize" Paragraph
- Specify a clear new use (youth club is more exam-appropriate than nightclub)
- Concrete renovation measures: change function, renovate interior, add technology, improve energy efficiency

### 5. "Who Benefits" Paragraph
- Directly answers "how it would benefit local people"
- Specific groups + specific benefits: local youth gain courses, sports, social space; nearby businesses see increased foot traffic

### 6. Conclusion
- Restate the position in formal language
- Template: *I strongly propose that [subject] should be preserved and converted into ...*
