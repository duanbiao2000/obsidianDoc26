# Deliberate Practice

**Deliberate Practice** is a concept popularized by psychologist [[Anders Ericsson]], referring to a specific type of focused, structured practice aimed at improving performance. It differs from simple repetition by involving immediate feedback, pushing beyond one's comfort zone, and high levels of concentration.

## Role in Skill Acquisition

Research suggests that while natural aptitude plays a role in early progress, sustained deliberate practice is the primary driver of mastery over the long term. This challenges the "natural talent" myth and emphasizes the importance of systematic effort.

## Application to Language Learning

For [[IELTS]] preparation, deliberate practice involves:

*   Targeted improvement of weak areas (e.g., specific phonemes or grammatical structures).
*   Seeking feedback from teachers or peers.
*   Analyzing model answers to understand high-band criteria.
*   Consistent, scheduled practice sessions rather than sporadic study.
