# Metrics vs Meaning

Metrics vs Meaning is a mental model describing the trade-off between measurable outcomes and deeper learning/creativity. It captures the tension in the UK education system between the [[results-driven-culture]] (metrics) and the broader goals of education such as critical thinking, creativity, and personal development (meaning).

This model is central to criticisms of the [[exam-factory]] model and [[teaching-to-the-test]], where the pursuit of measurable exam performance can come at the expense of genuine understanding and intellectual curiosity.
