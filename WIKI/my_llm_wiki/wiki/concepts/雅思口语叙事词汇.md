# 雅思口语叙事词汇

本概念聚焦于雅思口语Part 2叙事场景中使用的精准词汇与短语，旨在帮助考生用更生动、自然的方式讲述故事和描述经历。

## 情绪类（替代 very + 基础形容词）

| 词汇 | 替代平庸表达 | 使用场景 | 例句 |
|------|-------------|---------|------|
| mortified | very embarrassed | 讲糗事经历 | I was mortified when I called my teacher "mom". |
| ecstatic | very happy | 成就/好消息 | I was ecstatic when I got the offer. |
| infuriating | very annoying | 抱怨类话题 | The constant delays were absolutely infuriating. |
| exhausting | very tiring | 工作/学习压力 | Preparing for exams was mentally exhausting. |
| bewildering | very confusing | 复杂情况 | The instructions were utterly bewildering. |
| exhilarating | very exciting | 体验类 | Bungee jumping was an exhilarating experience. |
| daunting | very scary/difficult | 挑战类 | The project seemed daunting at first. |
| gutted | very disappointed | 失落感 | I was gutted when I missed the goal. |
| nerve-wracking | very stressful | 考试/面试前 | The interview was nerve-wracking. |
| surreal | very strange/unreal | 特殊经历 | It felt surreal to meet my idol. |

## 描述类（提升精准度）

- `compelling`（引人入胜的）→替代 interesting
- `underrated`（被低估的）→评价事物
- `mundane`（平凡的）→对比类话题
- `spontaneous`（自发的）→性格/旅行
- `meticulous`（一丝不苟的）→做事风格
- `versatile`（多才多艺的）→能力描述

## 状态/认知类（讲故事道具）

- `oblivious to`（未察觉）→描述人的状态
- `reluctant`（不情愿的）→心理状态
- `unbeknownst to me`（我不知道）→故事铺垫
- `it dawned on me`（我突然意识到）→转折叙述
- `dwelling on`（纠结于）→情绪描述
- `taken aback`（惊讶）→意外反应

## 叙事短语

- `it dawned on me that...`
- `I couldn't help but...`
- `I was caught off guard`
- `out of my depth`
- `on a whim`
- `put things into perspective`
- `come to terms with`

## 使用原则

- 口语中用一次精准词（mortified）比五次平庸词（very embarrassed）更有效。
- 注意语体：gig 为非正式，不适合正式场合。
- 叙事中使用插入语（unbeknownst to me）能增加故事层次感。
