# Flashcards

Flashcards are a study tool consisting of cards with a prompt on one side and the answer on the other. They are rated **C Tier** (Table 1) and **D Tier** (Table 2) in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List," with conflicting ratings.

## Mechanism

Flashcards support **active recall** — the learner must retrieve the answer from memory before checking. When combined with [[spaced-repetition]], they form a powerful learning system.

## Why the Low Rating?

The low rating may reflect **passive flashcard use** — simply flipping through cards without active retrieval, or using poorly designed cards that test recognition rather than recall. The rating may also reflect the tool rather than the technique: physical flashcards lack the scheduling optimization of digital tools like [[Anki]].

## Effective Use

- **Active retrieval** — Always try to recall the answer before flipping.
- **Spaced scheduling** — Use a spaced repetition algorithm (e.g., Anki).
- **Well-designed cards** — Focus on one concept per card; use images and mnemonics.
- **Elaborative encoding** — Add context and connections, not just definitions.

## Relationship to Other Techniques

- [[spaced-repetition]] — Flashcards are the most common implementation.

- [[anki]] — The most popular digital flashcard platform.
- [[remnote]] — A note-taking tool with integrated flashcards.
