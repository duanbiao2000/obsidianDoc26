# Pre-study

Pre-study is a learning technique that involves previewing material before deep study to activate prior knowledge. It is rated **B Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List" (Table 2). This is closely related to [[priming]], which is rated A in Table 1.

## Mechanism

Pre-study activates relevant schemas and creates a mental framework for organizing incoming information. It reduces cognitive load during subsequent study by establishing context.

## Examples

- Skimming a chapter before reading it in depth.
- Reviewing key terms and concepts before a lecture.
- Looking at diagrams and headings before reading the text.
- Watching a short overview video before a detailed lesson.

## Relationship to Other Techniques

- [[priming]] — Essentially the same concept.
- [[active-recall]] — Pre-study prepares the brain for effective retrieval practice.
- [[study-planning]] — Pre-study sessions can be scheduled as part of a study plan.
- [[chunking]] — Pre-study helps identify the main chunks before detailed study.
