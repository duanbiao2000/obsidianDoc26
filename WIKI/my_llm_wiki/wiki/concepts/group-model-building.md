# Group Model Building

**Group Model Building (GMB)** is a participatory process used in modern **System Dynamics** practice to align stakeholder mental models. By engaging diverse participants in the creation of **Causal Loop Diagrams** and other system maps, GMB facilitates shared understanding, resolves conflicts, and builds consensus on complex issues. This approach enhances the legitimacy and applicability of system dynamics models in policy and organizational contexts.
