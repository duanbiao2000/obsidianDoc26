# Picture Superiority Effect

The picture superiority effect is the phenomenon where pictures are remembered significantly better than words alone. [[lionel-standing]]'s 1970s experiment demonstrated that people could remember thousands of images with high accuracy. The visual learning video claims this effect yields a 6.5× improvement in retention when visuals are added to text. The effect is explained by [[dual-coding]] theory.
