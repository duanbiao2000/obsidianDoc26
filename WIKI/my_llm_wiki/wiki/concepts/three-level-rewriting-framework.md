# Three-Level Rewriting Framework

The **Three-Level Rewriting Framework** is an original methodology for using rewriting as cognitive training. It defines three progressive levels of rewriting, each targeting different cognitive skills.

## Level 1: Vocabulary Substitution (Low)

Replace words with synonyms or more precise terms.

**Example**: "The problem is very important" → "The issue is extremely crucial."

**Trains**: Memory retrieval, lexical flexibility, vocabulary activation.

**Does not train**: Precision of thought, depth of argument.

## Level 2: Concretization (Mid)

Transform abstract statements into specific, evidence-backed claims.

**Example**: "Communication is important" → "Daily writing, though taking up only 10-15% of communication, carries 90% of cognitive value."

**Trains**: Abstract-to-specific conversion, use of data and details to support claims.

## Level 3: Thought Restructuring (High)

Deepen arguments, reveal contradictions, build causal connections.

**Example**: "Writing helps you learn" → "Writing doesn't just record what you already know — it forces you to confront what you don't. The act of articulation reveals gaps in your understanding that reading alone never exposes."

**Trains**: Causal reasoning, contradiction detection, networked thinking, argument depth.

## Application

- Use Level 1 for vocabulary activation and fluency.
- Use Level 2 for developing evidence-based reasoning.
- Use Level 3 for critical thinking and argument construction.
- Progress through levels as skill develops; all three are valuable at different stages.

## Relationship to AI

AI can assist with Level 1 (vocabulary substitution) after the writer's thinking is clear. Levels 2 and 3 require human judgment and should not be delegated to AI.
