# Autotelic Experience

An **autotelic experience** is an activity that is intrinsically rewarding, where the process itself serves as the reward, independent of external outcomes. This concept is central to [[flow-state]] theory, distinguishing flow from extrinsic motivation-driven tasks.

Individuals with an autotelic personality tend to seek out challenges and engage deeply in activities for their own sake, fostering continuous personal growth, resilience against stress, and enhanced well-being.
