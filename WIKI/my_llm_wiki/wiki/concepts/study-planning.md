# Study Planning

Study planning is the metacognitive strategy of organizing study sessions, setting goals, and scheduling review. It is rated **S Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Components

- **Goal setting** — defining what you want to achieve in each session.
- **Scheduling** — allocating time for different subjects or techniques.
- **Prioritization** — identifying high-impact topics or weak areas.
- **Review planning** — scheduling spaced repetition and interleaving sessions.
- **Progress tracking** — monitoring what has been covered and what remains.

## Why It Is Effective

- Provides **structure** and **direction** to learning.
- Reduces **decision fatigue** — you don't have to decide what to study each moment.
- Enables **deliberate practice** by focusing on specific weaknesses.
- Supports **metacognition** — thinking about your own learning process.

## Relationship to Other Techniques

- [[interleaving]] and [[spaced-repetition]] — Planning is required to implement these techniques effectively.
- [[pomodoro-technique]] — A planning tool for executing focused work sessions.
- [[creating-content]] — Planning helps allocate time for content creation as a learning method.
