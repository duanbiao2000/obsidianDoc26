# Expansion Drill

An **Expansion Drill** is a type of [[FSI Pattern Drill Methodology]] that involves iteratively adding logical layers—such as conditions, data, examples, and counterpoints—to a simple core idea to create coherent complex expressions.

## Objective

To develop the ability to construct nuanced, multi-layered arguments and descriptions, moving from basic statements to sophisticated discourse.

## Process

1.  **Basic Statement:** Start with a simple core idea.
2.  **Add Condition:** Introduce constraints or contexts.
3.  **Add Data/Example:** Support the statement with evidence or specifics.
4.  **Add Counterpoint & Rebuttal:** Address opposing views and reinforce the main argument.

## Connection to Progressive Sentence Expansion

This drill is a direct practical application of the concept of [[Progressive Sentence Expansion]], providing a step-by-step framework for implementing it in self-study.
