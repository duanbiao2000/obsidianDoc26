# Feature Creep Prevention

**Feature Creep Prevention** is the practice of preventing uncontrolled expansion of product features beyond the original scope. The [[what-not-document]] is a tool specifically designed for this purpose.

## Mechanism

A What Not document prevents feature creep by:

1. **Pre-committing to exclusions:** The team agrees in advance what they will not build.
2. **Providing authoritative answers:** When a new feature is proposed, the team can quickly check the What Not document for a pre-agreed decision.
3. **Recording rationale:** The document includes reasons for exclusions, making it harder to override decisions without addressing the underlying logic.

## Claimed Benefit

The source claims that a What Not document can "save 30+ hours of meeting time" by eliminating repetitive discussions about rejected features. This claim is unsubstantiated and should be treated as a rhetorical estimate.
