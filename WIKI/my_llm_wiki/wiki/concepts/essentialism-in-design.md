# Essentialism in Design

**Essentialism in Design** is the principle that long-lasting design, whether in industrial products or software, strips away non-essential elements to reveal the underlying structure and function. It argues that good design is not about decoration, but about clarity and structural integrity.

## Principles

*   **Simplification to Essence**: Removing visual noise to expose the core concepts of a product (e.g., Notion's blocks, TikTok's video list).
*   **Structural Integrity**: Ensuring that the design reflects the true nature of the system, much like the **Eames Chair** achieves longevity through simplification and manufacturability.
*   **Tension with Character**: While essentialism drives efficiency, excessive simplification can strip a product of "character." Non-functional elements like animation and sound are crucial for emotional connection and must be balanced with functional minimalism.

## Application in Software

*   **Conceptual Clarity**: Software interfaces should evolve to expose the true conceptual model of the system, moving away from skeuomorphic metaphors.
*   **Long-Term Value**: Designs that focus on essence are more resilient to trends and technological changes.
