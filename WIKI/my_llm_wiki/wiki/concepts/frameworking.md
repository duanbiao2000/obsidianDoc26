# Frameworking

Frameworking is a learning technique that involves creating mental models or frameworks to organize knowledge. It is rated **B Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Mechanism

Frameworking helps learners build **mental models** — simplified representations of how things work. These models provide a structure for organizing new information, making it easier to understand, remember, and apply.

## Examples

- Creating a **taxonomy** or classification system for a domain.
- Building a **cause-and-effect diagram** for a process.
- Developing a **decision tree** for problem-solving.
- Constructing a **comparison matrix** for evaluating options.
