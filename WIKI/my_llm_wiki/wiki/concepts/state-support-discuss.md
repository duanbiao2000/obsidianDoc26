# State, Support, Discuss

**State, Support, Discuss** is a logical structure central to **UK academic admissions** essays and formal coursework. It prioritizes clear thesis statements, evidence-based support, and rigorous discussion over emotional resonance or narrative flair.

## Components

1.  **State**: Clearly articulate the main argument or thesis.
2.  **Support**: Provide evidence, data, expert quotes, or textual analysis to back the claim.
3.  **Discuss**: Analyze the implications, address counterarguments, and synthesize the findings to reinforce the thesis.

## Application in Admissions

In the context of elite UK university applications (e.g., Oxford, Cambridge, LSE), this structure demonstrates the applicant's ability to engage with complex ideas critically. It proves readiness for specialized study by showcasing analytical rigor and subject mastery. Unlike the US "Show, Don't Tell" approach, this method relies on objective clarity and logical coherence.
