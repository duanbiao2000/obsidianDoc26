# Time Delays in Systems

**Time Delays** are inherent lags in physical processes, information transmission, or cognitive recognition within a system. In **System Dynamics**, delays are critical because they frequently cause overshooting, oscillation, and policy resistance.

When a balancing loop includes a significant delay, the system may continue to adjust in the original direction even after the desired state has been reached, leading to instability. Understanding and mapping these delays is essential for designing effective policies that account for the time it takes for interventions to manifest their effects.
