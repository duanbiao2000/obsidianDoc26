# Substantive Verbs

**Substantive Verbs** are precise, high-information verbs that clearly convey the manner, intensity, or nature of an action. They are the preferred alternative to **Generic Verbs** in academic, professional, and formal writing.

## Role in Communication

Substantive verbs increase **information density** by encoding specific details about the action directly into the verb itself. This reduces ambiguity and enhances the clarity and professionalism of the text.

## Examples of Substitution

| Generic Verb | Substantive Alternative | Contextual Nuance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *get* | **obtain / retrieve / acquire** | Specifies method of acquisition |
| *make* | **formulate / devise / construct** | Emphasizes cognitive depth or planning |
| *do* | **conduct / execute / perform** | Indicates formal procedure or task completion |
| *have* | **convene / hold / encounter** | Highlights active agency or experience |
| *take* | **require / demand / occupy** | Clarifies resource allocation or necessity |

## Benefits

*   **Enhanced Precision**: Readers understand exactly what happened without guessing.
*   **Improved Flow**: Sentences become more dynamic and less reliant on auxiliary nouns.
*   **Professional Credibility**: Demonstrates a higher level of language proficiency and attention to detail.

## Connection to Other Concepts
