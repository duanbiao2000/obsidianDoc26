# Judgment Depth

**Judgment Depth** is a proposed metric for evaluating career paths and human value in an AI-augmented workplace. It shifts the focus from task completion and throughput to the quality of contextual wisdom, domain expertise, and relational equity.

## Definition

Judgment Depth refers to the capacity to make nuanced decisions that rely on:
*   **Domain Expertise**: Deep understanding of specific industry or technical contexts.
*   **Relationship Equity**: Trust and history built with stakeholders, clients, or team members.
*   **Contextual Wisdom**: The ability to interpret ambiguous situations and apply ethical or strategic considerations that cannot be derived solely from training data.

## Contrast with Task Completion

In traditional models, value is often measured by the volume of tasks completed. In an AI workplace, execution tasks are increasingly automated. Therefore, human value compounds in areas where AI struggles:
*   Navigating complex social or ethical dilemmas.
*   Synthesizing disparate pieces of information with high uncertainty.
*   Providing accountability and moral reasoning.

## Career Implications

Careers designed around Judgment Depth are more resilient to automation. They require continuous learning and engagement with the "bottom layer" of work (ethical judgment and accountability). Organizations should redesign promotion criteria to reward these deeper forms of contribution rather than just efficiency gains.
