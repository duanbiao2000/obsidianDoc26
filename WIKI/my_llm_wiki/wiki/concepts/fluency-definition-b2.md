# Fluency Definition (B2)

An operational definition of fluency as CEFR B2 level — the ability to converse on a wide range of topics (politics, history, etc.) despite making grammatical errors. The source "How to Get Fluent" states: "Fluency on the European scale... to me is B2. I make mistakes... but I can still get in a conversation and talk about everything from politics to history."

## Context

This is a personal, subjective definition. CEFR B2 is officially described as "independent user" — can understand main ideas of complex text, interact with a degree of fluency and spontaneity. The source's definition emphasizes communicative competence over error-free speech.

## Relevance

- Provides a realistic, attainable target for learners.
- Shifts focus from perfection to functional ability.
- Aligns with the [[Vocabulary Over Grammar]] principle — errors are acceptable if communication is effective.
