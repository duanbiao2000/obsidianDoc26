# Teaching to the Test

"Teaching to the test" is a pedagogical approach focused on maximizing exam scores rather than fostering deep understanding. It is a key criticism of the UK education system's [[results-driven-culture]] and is closely associated with the [[exam-factory]] model.

This approach is driven by [[high-stakes-assessments]] where exam performance determines university access and future opportunities. Critics argue it encourages short-term memorization, stifles creativity, and narrows the curriculum at the expense of broader learning and critical thinking.
