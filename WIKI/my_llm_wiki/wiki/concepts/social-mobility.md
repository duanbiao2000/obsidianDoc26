# Social Mobility

Social mobility refers to the ability of individuals to move between social classes. The UK education system is often criticized for struggling to facilitate social mobility, with the [[two-tier-system]] between private and state schools seen as a key barrier.

Private schools provide advantages beyond academics—including social networks, confidence, and access—that can perpetuate privilege across generations. The post-2008 reforms of [[academisation]] and [[free-schools]] have been questioned for potentially increasing fragmentation and inequality rather than improving social mobility.
