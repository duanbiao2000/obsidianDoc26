# Handoff Friction

**Handoff Friction** refers to the loss of information, efficiency, and intent that occurs when design work is transferred to development. It is a primary pain point in traditional software and web development workflows.

## Causes
- **Interpretation Errors:** Developers may misinterpret complex Figma files, animations, or interaction states.
- **Incomplete Specifications:** Designers may fail to document edge cases, responsive behaviors, or micro-interactions.
- **Technical Constraints:** Designs may be technically infeasible or inefficient to implement, requiring rework after the handoff.
- **Communication Gaps:** Lack of direct dialogue leads to assumptions and corrections.

## Consequences
- **Rework Loops:** Multiple rounds of corrections between design and dev teams.
- **Delayed Timelines:** Extended project durations due to back-and-forth communication.
- **Reduced Quality:** Final product may lack the polish or fidelity of the original design.
- **Creative Constraint:** Designers may limit their creativity due to uncertainty about implementation feasibility.

## Mitigation Strategies
1.  **Hybrid Builder Model:** One person handles both design and development, eliminating the handoff entirely.
2.  **Design Systems:** Shared component libraries and design tokens reduce ambiguity.
3.  **Prototyping:** High-fidelity prototypes (e.g., in Figma) clarify motion and interaction intent.
4.  **Lean Workflow:** Reducing upfront documentation in favor of rapid, iterative building.
