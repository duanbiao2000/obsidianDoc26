# Syntopical Reading

Syntopical reading is an advanced reading strategy that involves reading multiple books on the same subject to synthesize ideas and develop a comprehensive understanding. It is rated **B Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Origin

The term was coined by Mortimer Adler in "How to Read a Book" (1940). It is the fourth and highest level of reading in Adler's framework, following elementary, inspectional, and analytical reading.

## Process

1. **Identify the subject** and select multiple relevant books.
2. **Inspectional reading** — quickly survey each book to identify relevant passages.
3. **Create a common vocabulary** — identify key terms and concepts across authors.
4. **Synthesize** — integrate ideas from different sources into a coherent framework.
5. **Critique** — evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of different perspectives.
