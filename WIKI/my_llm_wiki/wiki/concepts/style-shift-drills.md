# Style-shift drills

Style-shift drills are a practice technique for C1→C2 learners. The exercise involves taking the same content and saying it in different styles or registers, noticing how language changes.

## Example from the Source

Take the phrase "Don't worry, everything will be okay" and practice saying it as if:
- Speaking to mates in the pub (informal, casual)
- In a business meeting (professional, measured)
- To a granddad (respectful, warm)

## What Changes

- **Register** — formal vs. informal vocabulary
- **Tone** — serious, lighthearted, empathetic
- **Lexical choice** — different words for the same meaning
- **Sentence structure** — shorter vs. longer, direct vs. indirect

## Why They Matter

Style-shift drills develop **appropriateness** — the ability to use the right language for the right context. This is a key component of [[effortless clarity]] at the C2 level.

## Relationship to Word Families

[[Word families]] support style-shift drills by providing the vocabulary flexibility needed to choose the right form (noun, verb, adjective, adverb) for each register.
