# Emotional Register Classification for IELTS

A classification system that categorises spoken phrases by the emotional attitude they convey (refusal, anger, indifference, acceptance) to help IELTS test‑takers express nuanced attitudes naturally in Speaking Part 3 and Writing Task 2.

## Categories

- **Refusal**: That's not the case, I'm not buying it
- **Anger/Frustration**: I'm dead serious, That's on you, Enough is enough
- **Indifference**: It is what it is, Fair enough
- **Acceptance/Agreement**: You have a point there, Fair enough (context‑dependent)

## Application

Used in conjunction with [[register-transformation-ielts]] to select appropriate expressions for different IELTS scenarios while maintaining natural delivery.
