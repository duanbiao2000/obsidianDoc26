# Cornell Note-Taking

Cornell Note-Taking is a structured note format with cues, notes, and summary sections. It is rated **B Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Structure

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Cue column** (left, ~2.5 inches) | Key questions, terms, or prompts |
| **Notes column** (right, ~6 inches) | Main notes from lecture or reading |
| **Summary** (bottom, ~2 inches) | Concise summary of the page |

## Process

1. **Record** — Take notes in the notes column during class or reading.
2. **Reduce** — After class, write cues/questions in the cue column.
3. **Recite** — Cover the notes column and use cues to recall the material.
4. **Reflect** — Review and connect to prior knowledge.
5. **Review** — Spaced review of cue columns.
