# Substitution Drill

A **Substitution Drill** is a type of [[FSI Pattern Drill Methodology]] where learners replace variables within a fixed complex sentence frame to master flexible usage.

## Objective

To achieve syntactic flexibility with high-intermediate structures by practicing different combinations of subjects, actions, and factors within a consistent grammatical skeleton.

## Example Structure

*"The extent to which [subject] can [action] hinges on [factor A] rather than [factor B], as [reason/clause]."*

By substituting different terms for each bracketed component, learners internalize the structure's logic and application across various contexts.
