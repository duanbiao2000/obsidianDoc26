# Focus Framing

**Focus Framing** is a communication technique used in [[what-not-document|What Not documents]] to avoid a negative tone. Instead of stating "We don't do X, Y, Z," the document reframes exclusions as positive statements of focus: "We focus on A and B, therefore we don't do C."

## Purpose

The technique addresses the common misconception that What Not documents are "too negative." By framing exclusions as consequences of focus, the document communicates strategic priorities rather than limitations.

## Example

- **Negative framing:** "We don't support offline mode."
- **Focus framing:** "We focus on real-time cloud collaboration, therefore we don't support offline mode."

## Limitation

The source notes that focus framing is recommended but not structurally enforced in the templates provided. The document's inherent structure (lists of "we don't do X") remains negative in form.
