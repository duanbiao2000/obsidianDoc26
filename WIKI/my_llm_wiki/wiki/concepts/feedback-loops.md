# Feedback Loops

**Feedback Loops** are fundamental mechanisms in **System Dynamics** for understanding dynamic behavior. They represent circular causality structures where the output of a process influences its own input.

## Types
*   **Reinforcing (Positive) Loops:** These loops drive exponential growth, collapse, or self-amplification. They occur when a change in a variable leads to further changes in the same direction.
*   **Balancing (Negative) Loops:** These loops pursue goals, seek equilibrium, and regulate deviations. They often produce oscillatory behavior, especially when combined with time delays.

## Loop Dominance
**Loop Dominance** refers to the shifting influence of different feedback structures over time. Due to nonlinearities, one loop may dominate the system's behavior in one regime, while another takes over as conditions change. This explains how systems transition between qualitatively different behavioral states.
