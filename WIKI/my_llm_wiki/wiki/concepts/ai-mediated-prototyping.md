# AI-Mediated Prototyping

**AI-Mediated Prototyping** is a technique that uses AI agents (such as those in **Cursor**) to generate runnable code prototypes directly, bypassing the need for static mockups. This approach significantly increases efficiency and allows for immediate iteration on real behavior.

## Process

1.  **Context Provision:** Provide the AI with context, components, and criteria.
2.  **Plan Mode:** Use AI to decompose complex tasks into manageable steps.
3.  **Iterative Collaboration:** Engage in a dialogue with the AI to refine the output, treating it as a collaborative partner rather than a simple tool.
4.  **Verification:** Test the runnable prototype to identify edge cases and system behaviors.

## Benefits

*   **Efficiency:** Reduces prototype development time from months (using traditional methods like **Figma**) to weeks or days.
*   **Realism:** Prototypes include real data, logic, and state management, providing more accurate feedback.
*   **Accessibility:** Lowers technical barriers for designers and non-engineers, enabling them to adopt **[[The Builder Role]]**.

This technique supports the **[[Learn in Public]]** methodology by enabling rapid creation of verifiable work output.
