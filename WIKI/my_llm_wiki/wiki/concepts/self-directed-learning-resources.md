## Critical Notes

The source's claim that these 52 websites are collectively "worth more than most college degrees" is an unsupported editorial assertion. The list is most useful as a categorized catalog of learning resources. Key limitations include:

- Several resources (Coursera, EdX, Futurelearn) offer university course content, undermining the anti-degree framing
- Some entries (Skillshare, Readwise, 23andMe) are not fully free
- The list does not address what college degrees provide beyond content knowledge: credentials, networking, structured progression, mentorship, and accreditation
