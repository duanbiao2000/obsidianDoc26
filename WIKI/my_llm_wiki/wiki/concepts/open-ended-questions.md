# Open-ended Questions

**Open-ended questions** are questions that require more than a one-word answer, such as "yes" or "no." They typically begin with words like "how," "what," "why," or "describe," and invite the respondent to elaborate, share opinions, or provide detailed explanations.

## Purpose

*   **Encourage Elaboration:** Prompts the speaker to provide more context and depth.
*   **Foster Dialogue:** Keeps the conversation flowing and prevents dead ends.
*   **Gain Insights:** Reveals underlying thoughts, feelings, and perspectives that closed questions might miss.

## Examples

*   "What was your experience like?"
*   "How did you arrive at that conclusion?"
*   "Why do you think this approach works?"

## Relation to Communication Styles

Using open-ended questions is a key tactic in the [[speak-less-ask-more]] framework, enabling listeners to guide conversations without dominating them.
