# Lock In (Idiom)

**Lock in** is an informal English idiom meaning to fully commit, focus intensely, and work without distraction. It is commonly used in contexts of study, work, or performance where sustained concentration is required.

## Usage in IELTS Context

In the source document, the speaker says: *"I'm going to lock in and I'll just get whatever I get."* This reflects a mindset of dedicated preparation without attachment to the outcome.

## Related Expressions

- **Nail it** — to perform excellently
- **Smash it** — to perform excellently (very informal)
- **Fight the same battle** — to face a recurring challenge

## Appropriateness for IELTS Speaking

- **Part 1 & 2:** Appropriate for describing study habits or preparation strategies
- **Part 3:** Use with caution; more formal alternatives may be preferred
- **Listening:** Essential for understanding native speakers in informal contexts
