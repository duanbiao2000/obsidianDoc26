# Free Schools

Free schools are schools established by parent/teacher groups or charities, operating outside local authority control. They are part of the post-2008 education reforms in the UK, alongside [[academisation]].

Proponents argue free schools increase choice and innovation in education. Critics contend they contribute to fragmentation and inequality, as they can draw resources and motivated families away from existing state schools, potentially deepening the [[two-tier-system]] and undermining [[social-mobility]].
