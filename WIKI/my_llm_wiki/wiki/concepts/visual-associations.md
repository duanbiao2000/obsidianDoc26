# Visual Associations

Visual associations is a memory technique that involves creating mental images to remember information. It is rated **A Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Mechanism

The brain processes visual information more efficiently than abstract information. Creating vivid, unusual, or emotionally charged mental images strengthens memory encoding and retrieval.

## Examples

- **Method of Loci** — associating items with locations in a familiar space.
- **Keyword method** — creating an image that links a foreign word to its meaning.
- **Diagramming** — drawing relationships between concepts.
- **Mind mapping** — creating visual diagrams of connected ideas.

## Relationship to Other Techniques

- [[mnemonics]] — Visual associations are a type of mnemonic.
- [[mind-mapping]] — A structured form of visual association.
- [[chunking]] — Visual images can serve as chunks that group information.
- [[active-recall]] — Recalling a visual image is a form of retrieval practice.
