# Stocks and Flows

**Stocks and Flows** are the three formal elements representing system structure in quantitative **System Dynamics** models, alongside time delays.

*   **Stocks (Levels):** These are accumulations or state variables that record the system's memory. Examples include inventory, population, and capital. Stocks integrate flows over time.
*   **Flows (Rates):** These are actions that increase or decrease stocks over time. Flows are often governed by decision rules and information processing.

Together, stocks and flows form the basis of **Stock-and-Flow Diagrams**, which provide the quantitative architecture for simulating system behavior using differential or integral equations.
