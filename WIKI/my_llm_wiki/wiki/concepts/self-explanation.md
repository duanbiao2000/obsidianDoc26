# Self-Explanation

Self-explanation is a learning technique where the learner explains a concept to themselves in their own words. It is rated **A Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List" (Table 2), as part of the Feynman Technique / Self-Explanation category.

## Mechanism

Self-explanation forces the learner to **elaborate** on information, connecting it to prior knowledge and identifying gaps in understanding. It is a form of **elaborative interrogation** — asking "why" and "how" questions about the material.

## Examples

- After reading a paragraph, explain it to yourself in your own words.
- When solving a problem, explain each step and why it works.
- When learning a new concept, ask yourself how it relates to what you already know.
