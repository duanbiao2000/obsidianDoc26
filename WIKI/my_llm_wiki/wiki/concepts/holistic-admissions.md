# Holistic Admissions

**Holistic Admissions** is the evaluation framework predominant in **US elite university** applications (e.g., Ivy League, Stanford, MIT). It assesses the applicant as a whole person, considering character, growth, values, and fit alongside academic achievements.

## Key Characteristics

*   **Character & Growth**: Essays must demonstrate resilience, maturity, and personal development.
*   **Values & Fit**: Applicants must align with the institution's culture and demonstrate core values through narrative.
*   **Narrative Focus**: The personal statement is a primary tool for revealing these qualitative traits, often using techniques like "Show, Don't Tell."
*   **Depth over Breadth**: Deep analysis of a single experience or interest is preferred over a superficial list of achievements.

## Contrast with UK Model

This contrasts sharply with the **UK Specialized Admissions** model, which prioritizes subject-specific academic potential and critical thinking skills over personal narrative or character assessment.
