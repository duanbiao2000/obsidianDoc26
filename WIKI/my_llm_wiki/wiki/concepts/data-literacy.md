# Data Literacy

**Data Literacy** is the ability to read, understand, analyze, and apply data. As part of the [[Future Skills Framework]] by [[Bernard Marr]], it is classified as a core technical skill, alongside [[Digital Literacy]].

For roles such as [[Data Scientist]] or [[ML Engineer]], data literacy is a fundamental competency. However, Marr argues that in the broader workforce, data literacy is becoming a universal requirement. It enables individuals to make evidence-based decisions, distinguish between reliable and unreliable information, and understand the implications of data-driven automation.

Data literacy is not just about statistical analysis; it includes the ethical awareness of how data is collected, used, and potentially misused, linking it directly to [[Ethical Awareness]] and [[Critical Thinking]].
