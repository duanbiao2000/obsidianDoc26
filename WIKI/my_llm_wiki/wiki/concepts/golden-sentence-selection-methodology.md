# Golden Sentence Selection Methodology

A cross‑video curation process that identifies the most transferable short spoken phrases for IELTS. The source identifies 12 "golden sentences" with highest cross‑topic transferability:

- That's not the case
- You have a point there
- I'm dead serious
- That's on you
- Don't get me wrong
- I'm not buying it
- It is what it is
- That's not how this works
- Enough is enough
- Fair enough
- Out of nowhere
- I'm blown away

## Criteria

Phrases are selected based on frequency of appearance across multiple video sources, adaptability to various IELTS topics, and potential for register transformation without losing communicative intent.
