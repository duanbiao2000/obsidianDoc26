# Pattern Detection Across Sources

Laying visual summaries of multiple books side‑by‑side allows the reader to detect recurring themes, connections, and contrasts — enabling cross‑source synthesis. This concept is introduced in the visual learning video as an advanced benefit of [[spatial-reading]] and [[chunking-with-visuals]].
