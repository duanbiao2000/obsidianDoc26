# Decision Rationale Recording

**Decision Rationale Recording** is the practice of documenting the reasoning behind technical and product decisions, especially rejections. The [[what-not-document]] is a form of decision rationale recording that focuses on *what was rejected* and *why*.

## Relationship to ADRs

[[architecture-decision-records-adr]] (ADRs) record the *why* behind a specific decision, including the context, alternatives considered, and the chosen option. What Not documents complement ADRs by aggregating multiple rejections into a single, accessible document for a broader audience. Together, they provide a complete record of design decision causality.

## Value

- **Institutional memory:** Prevents teams from repeating past debates.
- **Onboarding:** Helps new members understand why certain paths were not taken.
- **Consistency:** Ensures that future decisions align with past rationale.
