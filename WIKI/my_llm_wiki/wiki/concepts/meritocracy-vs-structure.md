# Meritocracy vs Structure

Meritocracy vs Structure is a mental model for checking structural advantages behind claims of meritocracy. It questions whether the UK education system is genuinely meritocratic—where talent and effort determine success—or whether structural factors like private school advantages, family wealth, and social networks play a decisive role.

This model is central to debates about the [[two-tier-system]] and [[social-mobility]], asking whether private education reflects wider inequality or actively reproduces and deepens it.
