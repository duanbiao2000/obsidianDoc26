# ATS-Compliant Resume

An ATS-compliant resume is formatted to pass through Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), the software used by Australian employers to screen job applications before human review. The structure and layout are optimized for machine parsing rather than visual appeal.

## Key Rules for Australian ATS Compliance

- **Simple Layout**: No graphics, images, columns, tables, charts, or text boxes — these can confuse ATS parsing
- **Standard Fonts**: Use universally readable fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman) at 10–12 point size
- **No Photos**: Australian resumes should not include photographs; ATS systems cannot process images
- **No Personal Details**: Age, date of birth, marital status, and nationality are not included (anti-discrimination norms)
- **Length**: 1–2 pages maximum, depending on experience level
- **File Format**: .docx or .pdf as specified in the job advertisement
- **Keywords**: Include relevant keywords from the job description and [[key-selection-criteria]] to pass automated screening

## Australian Resume Structure

1. **Contact Information**: Name, phone, email, LinkedIn profile, Australian address (city/suburb)
2. **Professional Summary**: 2–3 sentence overview of key qualifications and career objectives
3. **Key Skills**: Bulleted list of relevant competencies (match to job ad)
4. **Work Experience**: Reverse chronological with employer, role, dates, and bullet points describing achievements
5. **Education**: Qualifications, institution, and year
6. **Additional Sections**: Certifications, volunteering, professional development (if relevant)

## Relevance to Newcomers

For international candidates, an Australian address on the resume (even a temporary one) significantly increases response rates. The guide by [[Living Simply Australia]] recommends relocating before active job hunting or using a local contact's address. Candidates with [[education-first-resume-strategy|little experience]] should use an education-first format, placing education above work experience.
