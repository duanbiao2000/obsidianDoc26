# Neuroplasticity

**Neuroplasticity** (also known as brain plasticity or neural plasticity) is the ability of the nervous system to change its activity in response to intrinsic or extrinsic stimuli by reorganizing its structure, functions, or connections. This concept highlights the brain's capacity to adapt, learn, and recover from injury throughout life.

## Relevance to Learning

Understanding neuroplasticity underscores the importance of consistent practice and exposure to new challenges. It suggests that skills, including language proficiency and communication techniques, can be developed and refined through deliberate effort and repetition.
