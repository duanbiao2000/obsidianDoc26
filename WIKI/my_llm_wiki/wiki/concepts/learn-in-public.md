# Learn in Public

**Learn in Public** is a methodology and philosophy that advocates for acquiring skills by actively performing professional tasks and sharing the output publicly, rather than studying in isolation. It shifts individual identity from "student" to "practitioner."

## Core Principles

*   **Work as Learning:** The act of building real projects is the most effective form of learning. In software engineering, this allows individuals to simulate professional work conditions before formal employment.
*   **Verifiable Output:** Credentials and degrees are secondary to public, runnable portfolios. The primary asset in the AI era is demonstrable, verifiable work.
*   **Identity Shift:** By engaging in the work itself, individuals internalize the professional identity (e.g., "I am an engineer because I build software," not "I am studying to become an engineer").

## Application in the AI Era

AI tools like **Cursor** lower the barrier to entry, making it easier for non-engineers to build and share complex systems. This accelerates the **[[The Builder Role]]** transition, where designers and product managers can directly manipulate code and concepts. The practice complements **[[Agentic Learning System]]** by providing a framework for externalizing knowledge and receiving feedback through public engagement.
