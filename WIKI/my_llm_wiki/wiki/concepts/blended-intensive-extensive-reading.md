# Blended Intensive/Extensive Reading

Blended intensive/extensive reading is a language learning strategy that combines the benefits of both approaches using modern technology (e.g., [[lingq]]). The learner reads extensively for volume but uses digital tools to quickly look up and listen to unfamiliar words, thereby also engaging in intensive processing. The Luca Lampariello interview and the free voluntary reading video both advocate this blended approach as more effective than pure extensive reading for beginners.
