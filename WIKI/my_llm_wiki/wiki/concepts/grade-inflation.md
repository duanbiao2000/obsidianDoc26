# Grade Inflation

Grade inflation refers to the phenomenon of exam grade boundaries shifting over time, raising questions about whether standards have been maintained. In the UK education system, this is a recurring concern linked to the [[results-driven-culture]] and [[high-stakes-assessments]].

Critics argue that grade inflation can undermine the value of qualifications and create a "moving goalposts" effect where students must achieve ever-higher grades to secure the same opportunities. This concept is part of broader debates about the fairness and integrity of the UK's exam system.
