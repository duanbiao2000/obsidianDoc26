# C1 Communicative Achievement

One of the four C1 Advanced writing grading dimensions. Communicative Achievement assesses whether the response uses the appropriate register, tone, and format for the specified target reader. For Reports and Proposals, the reader is typically a higher-status individual (boss, teacher, organizer, committee) — referred to in the source as "big cheeses."

## Key Requirements

### 1. Formal Register
- Use formal, objective language
- Avoid contractions and colloquialisms
- Maintain a professional distance

### 2. Distancing Techniques
- Prefer passive voice over active: "It was noted that..." instead of "I noticed that..."
- Use third person references where appropriate
- Avoid direct address ("you should")

### 3. Indirect Suggestions
Instead of "You should change X," use:
- "Should these suggestions be implemented, ..."
- "It would be advisable to consider..."
- "One possible course of action would be..."

### 4. Polite First-Person
Contrary to the general rule of avoiding "I," polite first-person structures are acceptable for softening suggestions:
- "I would like to offer some feedback..."
- "I would suggest that..."

## Why Reader Awareness Matters

The C1 Advanced Writing rubric explicitly assesses whether the candidate has understood the communicative purpose and chosen an appropriate register. A technically accurate response that uses the wrong register (e.g., casual language when formal is required) loses marks on this dimension regardless of grammatical correctness.
