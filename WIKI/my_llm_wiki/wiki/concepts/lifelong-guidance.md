# Lifelong Guidance

**Lifelong Guidance** is a systemic approach to career and educational support that integrates guidance services across schools, vocational training institutions, employment services, and workplaces. It ensures that individuals have access to career management resources throughout their lives, not just during formal education.

## Key Characteristics

*   **Cross-Sectoral Integration:** Guidance is not confined to school counselors but is embedded in various societal institutions.
*   **Continuous Support:** Recognizes that career decisions and transitions occur at all life stages.
*   **Empowerment:** Aims to develop individuals' **Career Management Skills (CMS)** so they can navigate their own paths effectively.

## Context in Finland

In Finland, Lifelong Guidance is a national policy framework that supports the development of CMS. It emphasizes that while individuals are responsible for managing their careers, this responsibility is supported by a robust infrastructure of accessible information and professional guidance.

## Relation to Career Management Skills

Lifelong Guidance provides the *systemic context* for **Career Management Skills**. While CMS focuses on the individual's competencies, Lifelong Guidance focuses on the availability and quality of support systems that help individuals develop and apply those competencies.
