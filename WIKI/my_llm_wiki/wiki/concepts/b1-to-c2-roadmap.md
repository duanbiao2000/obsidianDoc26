# B1 to C2 Roadmap

The B1 to C2 Roadmap is a level-by-level progression framework for English speaking, organized around the [[Speaking Success System]]. It specifies what to focus on and what to avoid at each CEFR level transition.

## Level Transitions

### B1 → B2: Activation
- **Challenge**: Can understand but cannot speak fluently
- **Goal**: Activate existing knowledge; build spoken fluency
- **Primary focus**: [[High-frequency chunks]], thinking in English, speaking more
- **Mindset shift**: "Speak more, not learn more"

### B2 → C1: Naturalness (The Plateau)
- **Challenge**: Sounds okay but not natural; progress feels flat
- **Goal**: Sound natural and flexible with native speakers
- **Primary focus**: [[Collocations for natural English]], discourse control, [[fossilised errors]] correction
- **Mindset shift**: From "good enough" to wanting natural communication

### C1 → C2: Automaticity
- **Challenge**: Small but noticeable mistakes in nuance and style
- **Goal**: [[Effortless clarity]] — near-native automaticity and appropriateness
- **Primary focus**: Rhetoric, [[repair strategies]], [[word families]], [[style-shift drills]]
- **Mindset shift**: Language as a tool for connection and persuasion

## Key Principles Across Levels

- The same three-step system (discover, practice, review) applies at all levels
- **Noticing** (active) is more effective than **memorizing** (passive)
- Activation of language is more important than accumulation
- Authentic native input becomes increasingly important at higher levels
