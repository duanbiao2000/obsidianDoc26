# Results-Driven Culture

A results-driven culture in education prioritizes measurable exam performance over genuine learning and creativity. This culture is driven by [[high-stakes-assessments]] and manifests as [[teaching-to-the-test]] and the [[exam-factory]] model.

Critics argue this culture squeezes out creative subjects, encourages short-term memorization, and creates intense pressure on students. Since the 2008 financial crisis, austerity and a focus on measurable outcomes have further entrenched this approach in UK schools.
