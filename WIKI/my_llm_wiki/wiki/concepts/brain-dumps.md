# Brain Dumps

Brain dumps (also called blurting) is a learning technique where the learner writes down everything they know about a topic from memory. It is rated **C Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List" (Table 2). This is the same concept as [[blurting]].

## Mechanism

Brain dumps are a form of **free recall** — they force the brain to retrieve information without prompts. They reveal gaps in knowledge and provide a baseline for further study.

## Process

1. **Set a timer** (e.g., 5-10 minutes).
2. **Write everything** you can remember about a topic.
3. **Review** — compare your dump against notes or source material.
4. **Identify gaps** — note what was missing or incorrect.
5. **Repeat** — do another dump after studying to track progress.
