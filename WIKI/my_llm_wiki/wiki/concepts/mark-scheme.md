# Mark Scheme

A mark scheme is the official scoring rubric for an exam. In the [[A-level]] system, mark schemes specify exactly what points, arguments, or calculations earn marks, and in what format answers should be presented.

## Importance for Exam Preparation

A-level mark schemes have very specific answer logic. Internalizing this logic requires extensive practice with past papers — not just understanding the subject matter. The recommended priority is: **past papers > textbooks > supplementary videos**.

## Relationship to Self-Study

The source emphasizes that many students waste time watching videos without understanding the exam format and mark scheme logic. Mark scheme practice is the most efficient use of study time for exam-based qualifications.
