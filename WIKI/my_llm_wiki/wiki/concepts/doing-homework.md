# Doing Homework

Doing homework refers to completing assigned tasks outside of class. It is rated **C Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Why It Is Low-Rated

- **Variable quality** — Homework quality and relevance vary widely.
- **Passive completion** — Many students complete homework mechanically without active engagement.
- **No feedback** — Without timely feedback, errors go uncorrected.
- **Compliance over learning** — The goal is often completion rather than understanding.

## How to Improve

- **Treat homework as practice** — Engage actively with each problem.
- **Self-check** — Verify answers and identify errors.
- **Connect to learning goals** — Understand how each assignment relates to broader concepts.
- **Seek feedback** — Review corrected work and learn from mistakes.
