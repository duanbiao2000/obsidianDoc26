# IELTS Writing Collocations

**IELTS Writing Collocations** are fixed word pairings that are essential for achieving a high Lexical Resource score in the IELTS Writing test. Collocation errors (e.g., "~~make a conclusion~~" instead of "draw a conclusion") are more damaging to the score than using simple words correctly.

## Core Principle

The scoring criterion for Lexical Resource emphasizes **collocation accuracy** over vocabulary size. Incorrect pairings signal a lack of natural language command and can reduce the score even if the individual words are sophisticated.

## Usage Principle

In Writing, collocation accuracy is prioritized over idioms. Error-free collocations (e.g., "play a crucial role") are more valuable than rare words used with incorrect pairings. The document "[[8-journals--8-engnotes--18-雅思口语-vs-写作替换词-100组--1xhvyju]]" provides the full list with error examples.
