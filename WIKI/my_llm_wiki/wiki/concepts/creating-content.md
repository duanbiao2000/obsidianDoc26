# Creating Content

Creating content as a learning technique involves producing original material (e.g., blog posts, videos, diagrams, presentations) to deepen understanding of a topic. It is rated **S Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Mechanism

Content creation forces the learner to:

- **Synthesize** information from multiple sources.
- **Organize** knowledge into a coherent structure.
- **Explain** concepts in your own words (similar to the [[feynman-technique]]).
- **Identify gaps** in understanding that become apparent during creation.

## Examples

- Writing a blog post or tutorial.
- Recording a video explanation.
- Creating a mind map or diagram.
- Building a presentation or slide deck.
- Developing a practice problem set.

## Relationship to Other Techniques

- [[feynman-technique]] — Both involve teaching/explaining to deepen understanding.
- [[study-planning]] — Content creation requires planning and time allocation.
- [[active-recall]] — Creating content from memory is a form of active recall.
- [[group-discussions]] — Sharing created content invites feedback and discussion.
