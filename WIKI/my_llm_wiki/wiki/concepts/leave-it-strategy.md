# Leave It Strategy

The **Leave It Strategy** is a cognitive load reduction technique used in language learning. When encountering an unknown phrase or word, the learner deliberately skips it rather than stopping to investigate. The rationale is based on two scenarios:

1. **Common phrases:** If a phrase is frequently used, it will reappear naturally in other contexts, providing a second example from which the meaning can be inferred.
2. **Rare phrases:** If a phrase is uncommon, it is unlikely to be encountered again, so the effort of investigating it is not justified.

This strategy reduces perfectionism and the cognitive burden of trying to understand every single element of input, allowing the learner to maintain flow and long-term engagement.
