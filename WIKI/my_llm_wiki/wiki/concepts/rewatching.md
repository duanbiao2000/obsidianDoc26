# Rewatching Videos

Rewatching videos is a passive review method that involves watching video content again without active engagement. It is rated **D Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Why It Is Low-Rated

- **Passive processing** — Watching is a passive activity that does not engage retrieval.
- **Illusion of learning** — Familiarity with the content feels like understanding.
- **Low cognitive effort** — No active recall or elaboration.
- **Poor retention** — Information is quickly forgotten without active engagement.

## How to Improve

- **Take notes** — Write down key points while watching.
- **Pause and recall** — Stop the video periodically and summarize what you've learned.
- **Apply** — Try to use the information immediately after watching.
- **Combine with active techniques** — Use the video as a source for [[active-recall]] or [[feynman-technique]].
