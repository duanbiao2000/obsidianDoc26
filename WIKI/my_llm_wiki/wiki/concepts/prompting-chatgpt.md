# Prompting / ChatGPT for Learning

Using AI tools like ChatGPT for learning involves generating explanations, practice questions, or summaries through prompts. It is rated **B Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Use Cases

- **Generate explanations** — Ask ChatGPT to explain a concept in simple terms.
- **Create practice questions** — Generate quiz questions on a topic.
- **Simulate a tutor** — Ask follow-up questions to deepen understanding.
- **Summarize** — Get concise summaries of complex material.
- **Compare and contrast** — Ask for comparisons between related concepts.

## Limitations

- **Illusion of competence** — Reading AI-generated explanations can feel like learning but may not engage active recall.
- **Accuracy** — AI can produce incorrect or misleading information.
- **Over-reliance** — Using AI as a crutch instead of developing independent understanding.
