# Formulaic Sequence

A **Formulaic Sequence** (or **Formulaic Language**) is a pre-assembled linguistic string that is stored and retrieved as a whole, requiring minimal cognitive processing during production. These sequences are "pre-fabricated" and do not require real-time grammatical construction.

## Characteristics

*   **Fixedness**: They are often fixed or semi-fixed expressions.
*   **Immediate Retrieval**: Native speakers use them automatically without conscious thought.
*   **Naturalness**: They contribute to the natural flow of speech.

## Examples

*   `to be honest`
*   `anyway`
*   `long story short`
*   `pinky promise`

## Relationship to Lexical Chunks

While all lexical chunks are formulaic sequences, the term "formulaic sequence" is often used in linguistic research to emphasize the structural fixedness and storage mechanism. In pedagogical contexts, **[[Lexical Chunk]]** is the more common term for units aimed at improving fluency.
