# Transition Sentence/Phrase Categorization by Function

This is a methodological concept for analyzing spoken (and potentially written) discourse by grouping connecting language into four functional categories:

- **Opening / Introducing Ideas** — phrases that begin a segment or introduce a new concept.
- **Step-Progression / Procedural Transitions** — phrases that indicate a sequence of steps or logical development.
- **Topic-Shifting / Contrast / Qualification** — phrases that introduce a change of topic, a contrasting point, or a qualification.
- **Summarizing / Emphasis / Closing** — phrases that recap key points, emphasize a claim, or signal the end of a segment.

The concept was applied across seven YouTube video transcripts, demonstrating that these categories fit instructional, interview, and memory‑advice genres. Specific, reusable transition patterns were extracted per category.
