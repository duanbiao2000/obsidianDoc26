# Causal Loop Diagrams

**Causal Loop Diagrams (CLD)** are qualitative graphical representations used in **System Dynamics** to map causal relationships within a system. They show variables and the polarities of their feedback connections (positive or negative). CLDs are used in the early stages of modeling to scope problems, identify key feedback structures, and align stakeholder mental models before moving to quantitative **Stock-and-Flow Diagrams**.
