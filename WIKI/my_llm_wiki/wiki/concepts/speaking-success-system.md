# Speaking Success System

The Speaking Success System is a three-step framework for progressing from B1 to C2 in English speaking, developed by [[Keith Speaking Academy]]. The system consists of: **discover language**, **practice it**, and **review it**.

## Core Principle

The system is applied **differently at each CEFR level**. The same three steps are used, but the focus, materials, and techniques shift depending on whether the learner is at B1→B2 (activation), B2→C1 (naturalness), or C1→C2 (automaticity).

## The Three Steps

1. **Discover language** — Actively notice new chunks, collocations, patterns, and discourse phrases in authentic input
2. **Practice it** — Use the discovered language in speaking, discussions, debates, and oral summaries
3. **Review it** — Reflect on usage, identify errors (including [[fossilised errors]]), and refine

## Level-Specific Application

| Level | Discover Focus | Practice Focus | Review Focus |
|-------|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| B1→B2 | High-frequency chunks as sound units | Speaking more, thinking in English | Accepting mistakes, building fluency |
| B2→C1 | Collocations, lexical/grammatical patterns, discourse phrases | Discussions, debates, oral summaries | Identifying and correcting fossilised errors |
| C1→C2 | Nuance, rhetoric, word families, style shifts | Adapting language to contexts, repair strategies | Fine-tuning lexical choice and appropriateness |

## Relationship to Other Methods

The system is complementary to [[影子跟讀法 (Shadowing)]] and [[漸進式句子擴展 (Progressive Sentence Expansion)]], which provide specific practice techniques that can be integrated into the "practice" step. It also aligns with the [[口語高分底層邏輯]] framework for IELTS preparation.
