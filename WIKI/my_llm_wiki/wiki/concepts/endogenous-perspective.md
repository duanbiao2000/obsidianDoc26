# Endogenous Perspective

The **Endogenous Perspective** is a core philosophical tenet of **System Dynamics**. It posits that system behavior should be explained through internal structures and interactions rather than external shocks.

In this view, systems are treated as closed causal boundaries where behavior emerges from within the system's own architecture. This contrasts with conventional linear analysis, which often attributes changes to exogenous factors. By focusing on endogenous causes, System Dynamics reveals how circular causality and feedback loops generate complex dynamics, such as oscillations and exponential growth, independent of external triggers.
