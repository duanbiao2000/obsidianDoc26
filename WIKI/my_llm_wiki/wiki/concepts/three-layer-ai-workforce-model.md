# Three-Layer AI Workforce Model

The **Three-Layer AI Workforce Model** is a framework for organizing work in AI-integrated environments. It categorizes tasks into three layers based on their relationship to human agency, moral accountability, and skill development. The model aims to preserve human relevance and prevent skill atrophy while maximizing AI productivity.

## Layers of the Model

### 1. Bottom Layer: Ethical Judgment & Accountability
This layer represents the irreducible core of human responsibility. It involves:
*   **Moral Weight**: Bearing the ethical consequences of decisions.
*   **Relationship**: Explaining decisions to affected parties in a human context.
*   **Outcome Ownership**: Taking final responsibility for results.

Because this layer is defined as a *relationship* rather than a discrete task, it cannot be delegated to AI. AI can inform the decision-making process, but it cannot act as the accountable agent.

### 2. Middle Layer: Curation & Contextual Pushback
This layer operates on the principle of "AI generates, humans curates." It requires:
*   **Contextual Awareness**: Humans must have sufficient background information to critically evaluate AI outputs.
*   **Override Authority**: The genuine power to reject or modify AI suggestions.
*   **Organizational Discipline**: Protecting humans from becoming "review machines" who rubber-stamp outputs due to high volume or lack of time.

The failure mode of this layer is superficial human-in-the-loop systems where humans are technically present but functionally powerless.

### 3. Top Layer: Automation & Skill Atrophy Trap
This layer handles high-volume, repetitive execution tasks. While it drives productivity, it introduces a significant risk:
*   **Loss of Practice Reps**: Junior workers miss out on the repetitive tasks necessary to build foundational expertise.
*   **Skill Atrophy**: Without hands-on experience (e.g., processing raw data), workers may lose the intuition needed to detect errors or anomalies.

Deliberate design is required to ensure skill development continues even in highly automated zones.

## Application

Implementing this model requires shifting metrics from pure efficiency to intentional design. Organizations must ensure that humans retain **genuine override authority**, career paths reward **judgment depth**, and the **meaning gap** created by automation is actively managed through continuous articulation of human value.
