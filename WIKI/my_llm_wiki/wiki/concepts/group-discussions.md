# Group Discussions

Group discussions are a collaborative learning technique where learners discuss topics together to deepen understanding. They are rated **B Tier** in the source "27 Learning Techniques and Productivity Tools Tier List."

## Mechanisms

- **Explanation** — Articulating your understanding to others (similar to [[feynman-technique]]).
- **Exposure to different perspectives** — Seeing how others interpret the same material.
- **Active engagement** — Discussions require active recall and critical thinking.
- **Feedback** — Immediate correction of misconceptions.

## Effective Formats

- **Study groups** — Regular meetings to discuss course material.
- **Socratic seminars** — Question-driven discussions.
- **Peer teaching** — Each member teaches a topic to the group.
- **Debate** — Arguing different sides of an issue.

## Relationship to Other Techniques

- [[feynman-technique]] — Teaching and explaining to others.
