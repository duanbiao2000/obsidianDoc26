# IELTS Scenario System Unification

The **IELTS Scenario System Unification** is a framework for understanding how the four IELTS skills (Listening, Speaking, Writing, Reading) share overlapping scenario and topic systems. The central insight is that the four skills are not independent in terms of content — they draw from a common pool of topics, but each skill emphasizes different aspects and requires different depths of engagement.

## Core Principle

The four IELTS skills share significant scenario overlap, but differ in emphasis:

- **Listening** focuses on "life service scenarios" (renting, shopping, dining, banking, community services)
- **Speaking, Writing, and Reading** focus on "social issue topics" (education, healthcare, environment, technology, social inequality)
- The same vocabulary bank can be reused across skills, but depth requirements vary significantly

## Implications for Test Preparation

The unification framework enables a **cross-skill reuse strategy** that is more efficient than independent preparation for each skill. The recommended path is to build a core vocabulary bank around Speaking+Writing social issue topics, then supplement with life service words for Listening and academic words for Reading.

## Limitations

- The framework is based on qualitative observation of IELTS test patterns, not quantitative frequency analysis
- Each skill has exclusive scenarios that require dedicated preparation and cannot be cross-trained
- The optimal time allocation between cross-skill vocabulary building and skill-specific preparation is not quantified
