# IELTS Preparation Resource Recommendations

This page catalogs recommended video channels for IELTS preparation, organized by the skill domains they serve. These recommendations are based on qualitative assessment of content fit with IELTS test patterns.

## Universal Resources (Four Skills)

These channels cover topics that appear across all four IELTS skills:

- **TED-Ed** — Animated short videos (5-10 min) covering education, healthcare, environment, technology. Accurate subtitles, standard pacing. Recommended as a universal material library.
- **TED Talks** — Speech-style presentations covering the same universal topics. Language style is close to IELTS Speaking Part 3 and Writing Task 2 argumentation.
- **Kurzgesagt – In a Nutshell** — Science animation covering technology, healthcare, environment, space. Precise, concise language with high overlap between Listening and Reading vocabulary.

## Speaking + Writing Resources (Social Issues)

These channels cover social issue topics that appear in Speaking Part 3 and Writing Task 2:

- **DW News / DW Documentary** — Covers globalization, urbanization, social inequality, cultural conflict. Formal language style close to IELTS Writing Task 2.
- **BBC Ideas** — Short videos (3-5 min) on social, psychological, and cultural topics. High information density, directly usable for Part 3 responses.
- **Vox** — In-depth analysis of social phenomena (crime, urbanization, wealth gap). Combines narrative and argumentation with modern, fluent language.
- **The School of Life** — Mental health, relationships, social issues. Elegant language suitable for supplementing Part 3 viewpoint expression and advanced vocabulary.

## Listening Resources (Life Service Scenarios)

These channels cover the life service scenarios unique to Listening:

- **English in Action (BBC Learning English)** — Daily life scenarios (shopping, renting, banking, dining). Pacing close to IELTS Listening Part 1/2. Includes transcripts.
- **6 Minute English (BBC Learning English)** — 6-minute dialogues highly simulating IELTS Listening Part 1 conversations. Complete transcripts available. Suitable for intensive listening practice.
- **Rachel's English** — Focuses on American English daily speech, covering connected speech, reductions, and colloquial expressions in life service scenarios.

## Reading Resources (Academic Science Topics)

These channels cover the academic science topics unique to Reading:

- **SciShow** — Biological evolution, neuroscience, astronomy, geography. High language density with vocabulary overlapping IELTS Academic Reading.
- **PBS Eons** — Prehistoric history, biological evolution, archaeology. Covers high-frequency IELTS Reading academic topics. Standard pronunciation with moderate pacing.
- **National Geographic** — History, archaeology, astronomy, geography, natural sciences. Language style closest to IELTS Reading passages (long sentences, passive voice, dense academic vocabulary).
- **Crash Course** — Systematic subject explanations (history, biology, astronomy). 15-minute episodes suitable for building background knowledge frameworks for Reading topics.

## Usage Priority

- **Daily:** TED-Ed + 6 Minute English (highest ROI — universal coverage + Listening scenarios)
- **Weekly:** Vox / DW Documentary (Speaking+Writing topic material, select by topic)
- **Exam-specific:** Crash Course + PBS Eons (build academic background knowledge, reduce Reading comprehension load)
