# Marginalia vs. Formal Notes

This concept contrasts two note‑taking strategies: marginalia (informal annotations in the margins of a book) and formal notes (structured, chapter‑by‑chapter notes). The memory/reading retention video (“Smartest People Read Books Like This”) discusses when and how to use each for maximum retention.
