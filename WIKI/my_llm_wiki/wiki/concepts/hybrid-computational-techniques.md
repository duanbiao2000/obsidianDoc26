# Hybrid Computational Techniques

**Hybrid Computational Techniques** refer to the integration of **System Dynamics (SD)** with other computational modeling approaches, such as **Agent-Based Modeling (ABM)**, Dynamic Neural Networks, fuzzy logic, and machine learning.

These hybrids address specific limitations of pure SD, such as parametric uncertainty and the inability to easily model micro-level decision rules and heterogeneity. By coupling macro-level feedback structures with micro-level agent behaviors or data-driven predictive models, hybrid techniques offer more robust solutions for complex socio-technical systems. This area represents an emerging frontier in system dynamics research, linking traditional simulation with modern AI-driven context engineering.
