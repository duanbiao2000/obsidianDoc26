# Vocabulary as Main Obstacle

In the context of [[extensive-reading]], unknown vocabulary is identified as the primary barrier, more so than teacher resistance or motivation. The free voluntary reading video argues that adults can tolerate 15–20% unknown words, but beyond that, comprehension and enjoyment drop. Staying in limited content areas and using technology ([[lingq]]) are strategies to mitigate this obstacle.
