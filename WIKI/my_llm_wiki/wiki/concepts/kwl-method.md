# KWL Method

The **KWL Method** is an educational strategy used to activate prior knowledge, set learning goals, and reflect on new information. It involves three stages:

1.  **K (Know):** What do I already know about this topic?
2.  **W (Want):** What do I want to know or learn?
3.  **L (Learned):** What have I learned after engaging with the material?

## Application

The KWL method encourages learners to take an active role in their education by framing their curiosity and tracking their progress. It is particularly useful for reading comprehension, research projects, and structured learning sessions.

## Connection to Inquiry

By explicitly identifying what one "wants to know," the KWL method aligns with the principle of [[speak-less-ask-more]], promoting a mindset of continuous inquiry and self-directed learning.
