# "Table Stakes" Concept in IELTS Context

A term borrowed from gambling and business, defined in the source as the minimum qualification required to be considered for a position — not a differentiator. In IELTS contexts, it is used to express the idea that basic qualifications (e.g., a university degree) are necessary but not sufficient for career advancement.

## Example IELTS‑Style Usage

> "In today's competitive job market, a university degree has become table stakes — it no longer distinguishes a candidate but merely enables them to enter the competition."
