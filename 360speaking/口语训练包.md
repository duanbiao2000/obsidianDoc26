## Set 1: Online Content & Language Acquisition

## Part 1: Introduction and Interview

- Do you often listen to podcasts or watch YouTube videos in your free time?
- Do you prefer learning things through videos or by reading books?
- Have you ever used online content to help you learn a new language?
- What kind of voices or sounds help you feel relaxed when you are studying?

## Part 2: Individual Long Turn (Cue Card)

**Describe an online channel, podcast, or video series that you find educational.**
You should say:

- what it is and who the creator is
- how often you watch or listen to it
- what kind of knowledge or information you get from it
    and explain why you think it is an effective way to learn.

## Part 3: Two-way Discussion

- How has the internet changed the traditional ways of learning a language?
- Do you think artificial intelligence and algorithms control too much of what we consume online?
- Why do some people prefer self-study over attending traditional classroom courses?
- In the future, do you believe traditional teachers will be completely replaced by online media?

## 💡 Video-specific Lexical Chunks & Application

**shoot the breeze / chew the fat**
随性闲聊，轻松交谈。
I really enjoy listening to podcasts where the hosts just shoot the breeze about their daily lives, as it sounds much more natural.

**dumbed down**
过度简化，失去深度的。
A lot of language learning materials are too dumbed down, which makes it hard for advanced learners to improve.

**method in one's madness**
看似疯狂或毫无章法，实则有其道理或目的。
My study routine might look chaotic with lots of random YouTube videos, but there is actually method in my madness.

**stop being spoon-fed**
停止被“填鸭式”喂养，转向主动获取。
At the advanced stage of learning, you have to stop being spoon-fed and start researching topics on your own.

**bedrock**
基石，基础。
Extensive listening provides a very important bedrock for creating a natural instinct about how a language works.

## Set 2: Dreams, Psychology & Responsibilities

## Part 1: Introduction and Interview

- Do you usually remember your dreams when you wake up?
- Have you ever shared a weird dream with your friends or family?
- Do you think dreams have any special meanings?
- What do you usually do to relax when you are experiencing stress?

## Part 2: Individual Long Turn (Cue Card)

**Describe a time when you felt a strong sense of responsibility.**
You should say:

- what the situation was
- what exactly you were responsible for
- how you handled the challenges you faced
    and explain how you felt about taking on this responsibility.

## Part 3: Two-way Discussion

- Why do you think people in modern society often suffer from anxiety or nightmares?
- How do responsibilities change as an individual transitions from childhood to adulthood?
- Do you agree that parents today face more pressure than parents in the past?
- Is it important to seek professional psychological help (like therapy) when dealing with deep stress?

## 💡 Video-specific Lexical Chunks & Application

**mental bandwidth**
心智带宽，处理复杂问题的脑力或精力。
Modern life requires us to juggle so many roles that it consumes a lot of our mental bandwidth.

**juggle different roles**
在不同角色（如工作、家庭、个人）之间努力平衡。
Being an adult essentially means learning how to juggle different roles without completely losing your mind.

**background anxiety**
潜伏在背景中的焦虑感，隐性焦虑。
Many young professionals suffer from a constant background anxiety about failing to meet societal expectations.

**wish fulfillment**
愿望的满足（心理学概念，指梦境或幻想中实现潜意识的渴望）。
Sometimes our dreams are not just random images, but a psychological wish fulfillment of things we cannot control in reality.

**slip away**
悄悄溜走，不知不觉中失去。
If you don't spend enough time communicating with your family, the emotional connection might just slip away.

## Set 3: Humor, Misunderstandings & Culture

## Part 1: Introduction and Interview

- Do you enjoy telling jokes to your friends?
- What kind of comedy or TV shows make you laugh?
- Are you good at making other people smile when they are sad?
- Have you ever misunderstood what someone else was saying?

## Part 2: Individual Long Turn (Cue Card)

**Describe a time when you completely misunderstood a situation or a person.**
You should say:

- when and where it happened
- who was involved in this situation
- what was misunderstood
    and explain how the misunderstanding was finally resolved.

## Part 3: Two-way Discussion

- Why is humor considered an important part of human communication?
- Do you think a joke translated into another language can still be as funny as the original?
- In what kinds of situations is it inappropriate to use humor?
- How does the sense of humor differ between older generations and younger people?

## 💡 Video-specific Lexical Chunks & Application

**a kindred spirit**
志趣相投的人，灵魂伴侣。
When she mentioned her weird habit of collecting old bus tickets, I instantly knew I had found a kindred spirit.

**flawless delivery**
（演讲或讲笑话时）完美无瑕的表达与演绎。
Stand-up comedians rely heavily on flawless delivery, not just the content of the jokes themselves.

**change one's tune**
改变论调，改变态度（通常指因为情况变化而改口）。
He used to say online learning was useless, but he quickly changed his tune after passing the exam using podcasts.

**take someone by surprise**
出乎某人意料，使某人感到惊讶。
The cultural differences in non-verbal communication really took me by surprise when I first moved abroad.

**superficial**
表面的，肤浅的（文稿中用作双关语）。
Nowadays, many relationships built on social media tend to be relatively superficial compared to face-to-face interactions.
