### Part 2: Describe a time when you helped someone

I’d like to share an experience where I assisted an elderly neighbor who was struggling to **navigate** a new digital device.
我想分享一次帮助一位老邻居的故事，当时他正费劲地学习如何**操作/使用**（navigate）一个新电子设备。

He is a retired professor with a brilliant mind, but he found the **interface** of his first smartphone quite overwhelming.
他是一位退休教授，头脑非常聪明，但他觉得他第一部智能手机的**界面**（interface）让他无所适从。

I spent an afternoon showing him how to use video calls so he could **interact** with his grandchildren who live overseas.
我花了一个下午教他如何使用视频通话，这样他就能与住在海外的孙辈们**互动**（interact）了。

It was a fascinating **overlap** between his traditional world and the digital tools that define my generation.
那是他传统的世界与定义了我们这一代的数字工具之间一次迷人的**交汇/重叠**（overlap）。

Initially, I thought I was just performing a simple task, but I soon realized I was providing him with a vital **link** to his family.
起初，我以为我只是在完成一个简单的任务，但我很快意识到我是在为他提供一条与家人联系的重要**纽带/环节**（link）。

Seeing his relief and joy gave me a profound sense of **fulfillment** that I hadn't expected.
看到他的释然和喜悦，带给我一种出乎意料的深刻**成就感/满足感**（fulfillment）。

### Part 3: Discussion about Helping Others

**Question: Why do people help others?**

Essentially, helping others is a **fundamental** part of our social fabric that ensures a community can thrive.
本质上，帮助他人是我们社会结构中**基本的/根本的**（fundamental）一部分，它确保了一个社区能够繁荣。

It creates a sense of **reciprocity**, where a small act of kindness today builds trust for the future.
它创造了一种**互惠感**（reciprocity），即今日的小小善举能为未来建立信任。

**Question: Should children be taught to help others at an early age?**

I’m convinced that altruism should be encouraged early on to **cultivate** a genuine sense of empathy.
我深信应该在早期鼓励利他主义，以**培养**（cultivate）一种真正的同理心。

When children learn to support their peers, it becomes a **dominant** trait that shapes their adult personality.
当孩子学会支持同伴时，这会成为塑造他们成年后性格的**主导**（dominant）特质。

**Question: In what situations do people usually need help from others?**

People often require assistance when they encounter a major **transition**, such as moving to a new city or starting a career.
人们在遇到重大**转变**（transition）时往往需要帮助，比如搬到新城市或开启职业生涯。

In these moments, having a support system acts as a **catalyst** for a much smoother adjustment to the new environment.
在这些时刻，拥有支持系统会成为让你更快适应新环境的**催化剂**（catalyst）。

**Question: Do you think people today are less willing to help strangers than in the past?**

Actually, I think the way we help has undergone a significant **evolution** rather than a decline.
事实上，我认为我们提供帮助的方式经历了一场显著的**演变**（evolution），而不是在衰退。

There is a **trade-off**; while we might be more cautious in physical spaces, digital platforms allow us to support people globally.
这其中有一种**权衡/折中**（trade-off）；虽然我们在现实空间可能更谨慎，但数字平台让我们能够支持全球的人。

While urban life can make us seem **detached**, our ability to organize and help through technology is at an all-time high.
虽然城市生活可能让我们显得**冷淡/疏离**（detached），但我们通过技术进行组织和帮助的能力正处于历史最高点。
