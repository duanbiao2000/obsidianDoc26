# 🔷 10大题型 = 10个固定思维模型

---

# 1️⃣ Education 类（学习 / 学校 / 学生）

### 🧠 模型：“Motivation–System–Outcome”

- 动因：为什么学生会这样

- 系统：学校/考试制度如何影响

- 结果：学习质量变化

### 输出路径

> pressure → behavior → learning outcome

### 模板

> I think education systems strongly shape student behavior.  
> This is mainly because students are highly responsive to exam-driven incentives.  
> As a result, they tend to focus on performance rather than deep understanding.  
> For example, many students prioritize memorization over critical thinking.

---

# 2️⃣ Technology 类（AI / digital / internet）

### 🧠 模型：“Efficiency–Adoption–Dependency”

- 技术为什么被用

- 人如何依赖它

- 长期影响

### 模板

> I’d say technology is mainly adopted because it improves efficiency.  
> Over time, people become increasingly dependent on these tools.  
> What happens is that certain skills may gradually weaken as a result.  
> For example, people now rely heavily on GPS instead of navigation skills.

---

# 3️⃣ Government Policy 类（政策 / regulation）

### 🧠 模型：“Risk–Intervention–Tradeoff”

- 为什么需要政策（风险）

- 政府如何干预

- 自由 vs 控制

### 模板

> I think government intervention is necessary when risks become widespread.  
> This is because individual choices can sometimes create social costs.  
> However, policies often involve a trade-off between freedom and regulation.  
> For example, sugar taxes aim to reduce long-term health burdens.

---

# 4️⃣ Social Behavior 类（人 / 社会行为）

### 🧠 模型：“Norm–Pressure–Behavior”

- 社会规范

- 外部压力

- 行为变化

### 模板

> I think social behavior is largely influenced by societal norms.  
> This is because people tend to adjust their behavior to fit in.  
> As a result, group pressure often shapes individual decisions.  
> For example, people behave differently in public compared to private settings.

---

# 5️⃣ Work / Career 类（工作 / 职场）

### 🧠 模型：“Incentive–Competition–Performance”

> I think workplace behavior is driven by incentive structures.  
> This creates a competitive environment among employees.  
> As a result, performance improves, but sometimes at the cost of collaboration.  
> For example, bonus systems can encourage overworking.

---

# 6️⃣ Health / Lifestyle 类（健康）

### 🧠 模型：“Habit–Awareness–System Burden”

> I think health outcomes are mainly shaped by daily habits.  
> However, many people lack awareness of long-term risks.  
> This eventually increases pressure on public healthcare systems.  
> For example, poor diet contributes to chronic diseases like obesity.

---

# 7️⃣ Environment 类（环境）

### 🧠 模型：“Consumption–Externality–Regulation”

> I think environmental issues are closely linked to consumption patterns.  
> These behaviors often create negative externalities for society.  
> As a result, regulation becomes necessary to control damage.  
> For example, carbon taxes aim to reduce emissions.

---

# 8️⃣ Media / Internet 类（社交媒体）

### 🧠 模型：“Attention–Engagement–Amplification”

> I think social media is driven by attention-seeking mechanisms.  
> This leads to high user engagement and fast information spread.  
> However, it can also amplify misinformation.  
> For example, viral content often spreads without verification.

---

# 9️⃣ Crime / Safety 类

### 🧠 模型：“Opportunity–Control–Prevention”

> I think crime is often related to opportunity structures.  
> Weak control systems make certain behaviors easier.  
> As a result, prevention requires stronger monitoring.  
> For example, surveillance cameras reduce street crime.

---

# 🔟 Culture / Values 类（文化 / 价值观）

### 🧠 模型：“Tradition–Change–Conflict”

> I think cultural values are shaped by both tradition and modernization.  
> However, globalization often accelerates cultural change.  
> This can create tension between generations.  
> For example, younger people may adopt more individualistic values.

# 🧠 真正的8.5关键（比模板更重要）

## ✔ 1. 不是“背答案”，是“调用结构”

看到问题先分类：

> This is Technology / Policy / Social Behavior?

---

## ✔ 2. 每题只用 1 个模型

不要混模型（这是7.5 vs 8.5分水岭）

---

## ✔ 3. 每个句子只做一件事

- 句1：立场

- 句2：原因

- 句3：机制

- 句4：例子

---

## ✔ 4. 控制“口语真实感”

避免：

- systemic intervention

- metabolic illness crisis

改成：

- government action

- health problems
