**Have Stakeholders Been Considered?**
通过“利益相关者”视角快速大量生成高质量观点的方法

## 什么是 Stakeholder（利益相关者）

A stakeholder is any person or group who has an interest in an object, decision, or outcome.
利益相关者 = 对某个事物、决策或结果有利益关系（或会受影响）的个人或群体。

他们往往站在完全不同的立场，因此**主动切换视角**是打破思维定势、快速增加 idea 数量与深度的高效方法。

## 经典例子：企业是否应该深夜营业？

| Stakeholder       | 英文视角                              | 中文视角                          |
|-------------------|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Owners            | More profits, but need to handle more problems | 利润增加，但需更频繁处理问题     |
| Customers         | Greater shopping flexibility          | 购物时间更灵活                   |
| Employees         | More work/jobs, but may harm family life | 更多工作机会，但可能影响家庭生活 |
| Suppliers         | More sales                            | 销量增加                         |
| Government        | Higher tax revenue                    | 税收增加                         |
| Neighbours        | More vibrant area, but more traffic & noise | 社区更有活力，但交通与噪音增加   |

仅通过 6 个视角，idea 数量就从“1-2 个”迅速扩展到“10+ 个”，且天然具备对立与平衡，非常适合雅思 Discuss both views / Advantages & Disadvantages 题型。

## 高频写作话题的 Stakeholder 速查表（中英双语 · 可直接调用）

### 1. 教育类（Should university education be free?）

- **Students** – Lower financial burden, more equal access / 减轻经济负担，机会更平等
- **Parents** – Reduced family pressure / 家庭压力减小
- **Universities** – Possible drop in quality or funding shortage / 可能质量下降或资金不足
- **Taxpayers / Government** – Higher public spending, long-term economic benefit / 公共开支增加，但长期经济收益
- **Employers** – Better-educated workforce / 劳动力素质提升
- **Private colleges** – Increased competition / 竞争加剧

### 2. 科技类（Should AI replace human workers?）

- **Employees** – Job loss vs. new higher-skilled roles / 失业风险 vs. 新高技能岗位
- **Employers / Business owners** – Lower costs, higher efficiency / 成本降低、效率提升
- **Customers** – Cheaper products/services, but less human touch / 商品更便宜，但缺少人情味
- **Government** – Unemployment pressure vs. higher productivity & tax / 失业压力 vs. 生产力与税收上升
- **Tech companies** – Massive profits and market dominance / 巨额利润与市场主导
- **Society at large** – Faster progress vs. inequality & mental health risks / 进步加速 vs. 不平等与心理健康风险

### 3. 环境类（Should governments ban single-use plastics?）

- **Consumers** – Inconvenience, higher short-term cost / 不便，短期成本上升
- **Plastic manufacturers** – Revenue loss, need to innovate / 收入下降，需转型创新
- **Environmental groups** – Major win for oceans and wildlife / 海洋与野生动物重大胜利
- **Government** – Popular green policy, but enforcement costs / 受欢迎的绿色政策，但执法成本
- **Retailers / Shops** – Need new packaging solutions / 需寻找新包装方案
- **Future generations** – Cleaner planet / 更清洁的地球

### 4. 工作类（Should companies allow fully remote work?）

- **Employees** – Flexibility, better work-life balance, possible isolation / 灵活、平衡更好，但可能孤独
- **Employers** – Lower office costs, wider talent pool, harder supervision / 办公室成本降低、人才池扩大，但难监管
- **Local businesses** (cafés, restaurants near offices) – Fewer customers / 顾客减少
- **Cities / Government** – Less traffic & pollution, but less commercial tax / 交通污染减少，但商业税可能下降
- **Families** – More parent-child time / 亲子时间增加
- **IT / Cybersecurity teams** – Higher security risks / 安全风险上升

### 5. 社会类（Should the government increase taxes on unhealthy food?）

- **Consumers** (especially low-income) – Higher daily costs / 日常开支增加
- **Food companies** – Pressure to reformulate products / 被迫改良产品
- **Health system / Hospitals** – Lower long-term medical costs / 长期医疗成本下降
- **Government** – Extra revenue + healthier population / 额外税收 + 更健康的国民
- **Farmers** (sugar/meat) – Possible demand drop / 需求可能下降
- **Children / Future generations** – Better health habits / 更健康的生活习惯

## 2 分钟考试实操流程（Stakeholder 法）

1. **30 秒**：快速列出 4–6 个最相关的 stakeholders（用上面的速查表或自己想）。
2. **60 秒**：每个 stakeholder 写 1 个好处 + 1 个坏处（或支持/反对理由）。
3. **30 秒**：选出 2–3 个最有力、最好举例的观点，组成简单提纲（Thesis + Body 1 + Body 2）。

**结果**：idea 数量通常翻 3–5 倍，且天然包含平衡视角，文章更显 mature 与 critical。

## 与 Idea Bank 的结合建议

把 stakeholder 视角直接做成你的 Idea Bank 标签，例如：

- `#stakeholder-employees`
- `#stakeholder-government`
- `#stakeholder-future-generations`

以后看到任何题目，先问自己一句：
**“Who are the stakeholders, and what would each of them think?”**
就能在极短时间内从“没思路”变成“思路爆炸”。
