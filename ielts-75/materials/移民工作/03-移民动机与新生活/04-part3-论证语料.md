# 移民动机与新生活 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。本话题多涉及移民利弊、文化适应、全球化。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / In my view, … / It depends on …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：What are the main benefits of immigration?

**思路**：观点（好处多）→ 逐点（个人发展、文化、社会）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say immigration brings many benefits, for both individuals and societies. For individuals, it offers the chance to pursue better career opportunities and a higher standard of living. For societies, immigrants often bring fresh ideas, skills and cultural diversity. For example, many countries rely on immigrant workers in essential industries. So in my view, immigration is largely a positive force when it is well-managed.

## 问题 2：What difficulties do immigrants face?

**思路**：观点（困难真实存在）→ 逐点（语言、文化、身份）→ 举例 → 总结（可克服）。

**示范**：I'd say immigrants face several real difficulties. First of all, the language barrier, which affects everything from daily life to job applications. Then, cultural differences — understanding local norms and building a social network takes time. For example, many immigrants report feeling isolated in the first few years. That said, with support from communities and their own efforts, most people manage to overcome these challenges over time.

## 问题 3：Should countries encourage immigration?

**思路**：观点（有条件地鼓励）→ 原因（劳动力、多样性）→ 让步（资源与管理压力）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say countries should encourage immigration, but with sensible management. The main reason is that immigrants can fill labour shortages, bring new skills and contribute to economic growth, while also enriching the culture. That said, rapid immigration can put pressure on housing, healthcare and public services, which needs to be planned for. So I believe the right approach is to welcome immigration while investing in integration and public services.

## 问题 4：How can immigrants better adapt to a new culture?

**思路**：观点（主动融入）→ 方法（语言、社交、开放心态）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say the key is to be proactive. The main reason is that adaptation doesn't happen passively — immigrants need to learn the local language, take part in community activities, and be open to new experiences. For example, joining local clubs or volunteer groups is a great way to meet people and understand the culture. That said, it's also important to keep one's own cultural identity, so the goal is integration, not complete assimilation.

## 问题 5：Will globalisation lead to more migration in the future?

**思路**：观点（会）→ 原因（经济、信息、政策）→ 举例 → 让步（限制可能增多）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say globalisation will lead to more migration in the future. The main reason is that economic ties between countries are deepening, and information about opportunities abroad is more accessible than ever, which naturally encourages people to seek better lives elsewhere. For example, many young people now study or work across borders as a matter of course. That said, some countries may respond with stricter controls, so the trend may not be smooth. Still, I believe migration will continue to grow overall.
