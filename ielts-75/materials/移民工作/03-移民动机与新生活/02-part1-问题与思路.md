# 移民动机与新生活 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. Would you like to live in another country?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 具体动机 → E 举例。

**示范**：Yes, I would definitely like to. My main motivation would be career development and the chance to experience a different culture. For example, living abroad would give me the opportunity to improve my language skills in real situations, which I think would be very valuable.

## 2. What would be the hardest part of moving to a new country?

**思路**：A 点出困难 → R 原因 → E 应对方式。

**示范**：I'd say the hardest part would be adapting to a new culture and possibly the language barrier. The main reason is that everyday routines and social norms can be quite different, which takes time to get used to. What would help is keeping an open mind and being willing to ask questions, which makes the adaptation process smoother.

## 3. Do you think it's easy to make friends in a new place?

**思路**：A 表态（起初不易）→ R 原因 → E 应对 + 转折。

**示范**：I'd say it can be quite challenging at first. The main reason is that you start with no social network, and it takes time to build trust with new people. That said, joining local activities or community groups makes it much easier, and once you make a few good friends, the rest usually follows.

## 4. What are the main reasons people immigrate?

**思路**：A 列举原因 → R 逐点解释 → E 举例。

**示范**：I'd say there are several main reasons. First of all, better career opportunities and a higher standard of living, which is the most common motivation. Then, family reasons — many people move to be closer to family members. For example, some people relocate specifically to give their children better education and living conditions.

## 5. Would you miss your home country if you moved abroad?

**思路**：A 表态（会）→ R 原因 → E 如何面对（情感真实但积极）。

**示范**：Yes, I would definitely miss it, especially my family and the food. The main reason is that home is where your roots and memories are, so it's natural to feel homesick at first. That said, I think keeping in touch with family and staying connected to my culture would help, and the new experience would also bring new things to look forward to.

## 6. Is it better to move abroad young or later in life?

**思路**：A 表态（各有优劣）→ 年轻的好处 → 成熟后的好处 → 折中。

**示范**：I'd say both ages have their advantages. Moving young means you adapt more easily and have more time to build a life, but you may also lack experience and resources. Moving later in life means you're more prepared financially and professionally, though adaptation might take longer. So in my view, the right time depends on each person's situation.
