# 移民动机与新生活 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、动机与决定

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| immigrate / move abroad（移居） | decide to move abroad | make the move, settle abroad |
| motivation（动机） | my main motivation is … | the reason behind my decision |
| opportunity（机会） | career opportunities | chances, prospects |
| for the sake of（为了） | for the sake of my family | to give my family … |
| make the decision（做决定） | make the decision to move | decide to |
| weigh the pros and cons（权衡利弊） | weigh the pros and cons carefully | consider both sides |
| a fresh start（新的开始） | a fresh start in a new country | a new beginning |

## 二、适应与挑战

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| adapt / adjust（适应） | adapt to a new environment | adjust to the new life |
| culture shock（文化冲击） | experience culture shock | feel disoriented in a new culture |
| language barrier（语言障碍） | overcome the language barrier | get past the language difficulty |
| homesick（想家） | feel homesick at first | miss home |
| get used to（习惯） | get used to the new routine | settle into the new life |
| challenge（挑战） | face challenges | deal with difficulties |
| step out of my comfort zone（走出舒适区） | step out of my comfort zone | leave my comfort zone |

## 三、收获与评价

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| broaden one's horizons（开阔视野） | broaden my horizons | expand my outlook |
| gain independence（获得独立） | become more independent | grow more self-reliant |
| a sense of belonging（归属感） | build a sense of belonging | feel at home |
| rewarding（有收获的） | a rewarding experience | worthwhile, enriching |
| once-in-a-lifetime（难得的） | a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity | a rare chance |
| personal growth（个人成长） | significant personal growth | grow as a person |

## 四、高频句型

- My main reason for moving was …（我移居的主要原因是…）
- At first, I found it challenging because …（起初我觉得很有挑战，因为…）
- What helped me adapt was …（帮助我适应的是…）
- Looking back, I'm glad I made the decision, because …（回头看，我很高兴做了这个决定，因为…）
- Although it was difficult at first, …（虽然起初很困难，但…）

## 五、易错提醒

- "immigrate" 是动词，"immigration" 是名词，注意区分；
- 谈过去移居经历用过去时，谈现在状态用现在完成时/现在时；
- 避免只说 "good/bad"，用 challenging / rewarding / enriching 等更具体词汇。
