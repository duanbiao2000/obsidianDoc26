# 工作与职业 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。本话题多讨论职业选择、工作价值、就业趋势。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / In my view, … / It depends on …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：How important is job satisfaction?

**思路**：观点（非常重要）→ 原因（影响投入与健康）→ 举例 → 让步（收入也重要）。

**示范**：I'd say job satisfaction is very important. The main reason is that work takes up a large part of our lives, so doing something meaningful directly affects our motivation and well-being. For example, people who find their work rewarding are usually more willing to put in effort and stay in their jobs longer. That said, income and job security also matter, and for some people they are the priority. Still, long-term, satisfaction is what keeps people going.

## 问题 2：Should young people stay in one job for a long time?

**思路**：观点（看情况）→ 长期的好处（深度、稳定）→ 换工作的好处（视野、成长）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say it depends on the person and the career. Staying in one job for a long time allows people to build deep expertise and a strong professional network, which is valuable in some fields. On the other hand, changing jobs can broaden a person's experience and help them find a better fit. So I believe it's not about how long someone stays, but whether they are growing and learning.

## 问题 3：Will technology change the job market?

**思路**：观点（会，且已经发生）→ 具体变化（岗位消失与新增）→ 举例 → 总结（适应能力）。

**示范**：I'd say technology is already changing the job market, and will continue to do so. The main reason is that automation can take over repetitive tasks, while creating new roles that require digital skills. For example, jobs in data analysis and AI are growing rapidly, while some manual tasks are disappearing. That said, this doesn't mean job shortages overall, as new types of work emerge. What matters most, I think, is that people keep learning and adapting.

## 问题 4：Is it better to work for a big company or a small one?

**思路**：观点（各有优劣）→ 大公司优点（资源、体系）→ 小公司优点（成长、灵活）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say both have their strengths. Big companies usually offer more resources, clearer career paths and better training, which suits people who value stability. On the other hand, small companies often give employees more responsibility and a faster growth curve, which suits those who like to take initiative. So in my view, the better choice depends on what a person values most at that stage of their career.

## 问题 5：How can people achieve a good work-life balance?

**思路**：观点（需要主动管理）→ 方法（时间、边界、习惯）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say achieving a good work-life balance requires active management. The main reason is that work can easily expand into all available time unless people set clear boundaries. For example, switching off work notifications after hours and setting aside fixed time for family or hobbies can make a real difference. That said, it also depends on the job — some roles are inherently demanding. Still, being deliberate about time and limits is the key.
