# 工作与职业 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材：你的职业（或一份你想做的工作），可一材多用。

## 素材骨架（核心故事）

**主题**：你的工作——做什么、为什么选它、一个具体经历、收获与展望

- **是什么**：职位、工作领域、日常工作
- **为什么选择**：兴趣 + 价值
- **具体经历**：一次印象深刻的工作场景（项目、困难、成果）
- **收获与展望**：学到什么、未来目标

## 话题卡 1：Describe your job

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：a data analyst at a technology company
- 点 1：what I do — collect and interpret data, help teams make decisions
- 点 2：a specific experience — a project where my analysis changed the team's plan
- 收尾：rewards — sense of achievement, opportunities to learn

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about my job — I work as a data analyst at a technology company.
First of all, my daily work involves collecting and interpreting data, and turning it into clear information that helps the team make better decisions. It's a job that requires both technical skills and attention to detail.
Then, there's one project I remember clearly. The team was unsure about which product to focus on, and after I analysed user behaviour for a week, my findings helped them adjust the plan. Seeing that decision work out gave me a real sense of achievement.
In the end, what I find most rewarding about the job is that I keep learning new things, and it has clear opportunities for growth, which is exactly what I was looking for.

## 话题卡 2：Describe a career you would like to try（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：There's a career I'd really like to try — working as a data analyst.
- 重点：为什么想从事（兴趣、价值）+ 如果转行需要的准备
- 收尾：它符合我对职业的期待

## 话题卡 3：Describe a challenging experience at work（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about a challenging experience at work — the project I mentioned.
- 重点：困难是什么、如何克服、结果如何
- 收尾：这次经历让我成长，也让我更热爱这份工作

## 避免背稿感

- 工作话题要避免"简历式"罗列，用一个具体项目把故事串起来；
- 一材多用时，焦点按题目调整：描述工作 / 职业向往 / 挑战经历。
