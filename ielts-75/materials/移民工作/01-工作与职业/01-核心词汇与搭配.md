# 工作与职业 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、职业与工作内容

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| profession / career（职业） | my profession is … | my job is …, my career |
| job（工作） | do a job, take a job | work, position |
| be responsible for（负责） | be responsible for projects | handle, take charge of |
| specialise in（专攻） | specialise in data analysis | focus on, specialise in |
| work experience（工作经验） | have work experience in … | experience in the field |
| qualification（资质） | professional qualifications | skills, credentials |
| career path（职业路径） | plan my career path | my career plan |
| promotion（晋升） | get a promotion | move up, advance |

## 二、职业选择与规划

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| career goal（职业目标） | set a career goal | my goal is to … |
| job satisfaction（工作满足感） | high job satisfaction | a fulfilling job |
| work-life balance（工作生活平衡） | a healthy work-life balance | balance between work and life |
| job security（工作保障） | job security matters | stability of the job |
| challenges（挑战） | face challenges at work | deal with difficulties |
| opportunities（机会） | career opportunities | chances for growth |
| fulfil（实现/满足） | fulfil my potential | reach my potential |

## 三、评价与态度

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rewarding（有回报的） | a rewarding career | fulfilling, worthwhile |
| demanding（要求高的） | a demanding job | a challenging job |
| rewarding（有回报的） | find the work rewarding | feel the job is meaningful |
| gain experience（积累经验） | gain valuable experience | build up experience |
| make a difference（产生影响） | make a difference in the field | contribute to |
| long-term goal（长期目标） | my long-term goal is … | in the long run |

## 四、高频句型

- My job involves …, which means …（我的工作涉及…，这意味着…）
- I chose this career because …（我选择这个职业是因为…）
- What I find most rewarding about it is …（我觉得最有价值的是…）
- In the future, I plan to …, because …（未来我计划…，因为…）
- If I get the opportunity, I would …（如果有机会，我会…）

## 五、易错提醒

- "work" 作动词不接冠词（I work in…），作名词可加冠词（a job）；
- 描述日常工作用一般现在时，过去经历用过去时；
- 避免只用 "good job"，换用 rewarding / demanding / fulfilling 等具体评价。
