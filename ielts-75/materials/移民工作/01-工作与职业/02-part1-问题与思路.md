# 工作与职业 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. What do you do?

**思路**：A 直接说职业 → R 主要工作内容 → E 一个具体场景。

**示范**：I work as a data analyst. The main part of my job involves collecting and interpreting data to help the team make better decisions. What makes the work interesting is that I often deal with real business problems, so every project feels different.

## 2. Do you like your job?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 原因 → E 具体例子。

**示范**：Yes, I find it quite rewarding. The main reason is that I can see the impact of my work — for example, when my analysis helps the team adjust a plan and it works, I feel a real sense of achievement. The challenges keep it interesting too.

## 3. What is the most difficult part of your job?

**思路**：A 点出难点 → R 为什么难 → E 如何应对。

**示范**：I'd say handling tight deadlines is the toughest part. The main reason is that the work often requires accuracy as well as speed, so it can be stressful. What helps is that I plan ahead and prioritise tasks carefully, which keeps the pressure manageable.

## 4. What would you like to do in the future?

**思路**：A 直接计划 → R 原因 → E 条件句（语法加分）。

**示范**：I'd like to move into a management role in the future. The main reason is that I want to take on more responsibility and lead projects from start to finish. If I get the chance, I'd also like to gain more cross-department experience, which I believe will help me grow.

## 5. Is it difficult to find a job in your field?

**思路**：A 表态（看领域）→ R 原因 → E 举例 → 折中。

**示范**：I'd say it depends on the field. In my area, there are quite a few opportunities, but competition is also strong, so candidates need solid skills and experience. For those who keep learning and building their skills, I think it's manageable, though it does take effort.

## 6. What makes a good employee?

**思路**：A 列举品质 → R 逐点解释 → E 总结。

**示范**：I'd say a good employee needs a few key qualities. First of all, reliability — being able to deliver work on time and to a high standard. Then, the ability to cooperate, since most work involves teams. What I find most valuable, though, is a willingness to keep learning, because the working world keeps changing.
