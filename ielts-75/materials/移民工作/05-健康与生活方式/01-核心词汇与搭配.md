# 健康与生活方式 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、运动与健身

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| exercise（运动） | take exercise, do exercise | work out, stay active |
| keep fit（保持健康） | keep fit by running | stay in shape, stay healthy |
| regular exercise（规律运动） | do regular exercise | exercise regularly |
| aerobic exercise（有氧运动） | aerobic exercise like running | cardio |
| workout（锻炼） | a daily workout | exercise session |
| energy（精力） | full of energy | energetic |

## 二、饮食与作息

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a balanced diet（均衡饮食） | maintain a balanced diet | eat a healthy, balanced diet |
| fresh food（新鲜食物） | eat more fresh food | fresh produce |
| cut down on（减少） | cut down on sugar and salt | reduce, eat less of |
| a healthy diet（健康饮食） | stick to a healthy diet | eat healthily |
| sleep（睡眠） | get enough sleep | sleep well, have a good night's sleep |
| routine（作息） | a regular routine | a regular schedule |
| stay up late（熬夜） | avoid staying up late | not go to bed late |

## 三、健康与感受

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| maintain good health（保持健康） | maintain good health | stay healthy, keep well |
| relieve stress（缓解压力） | relieve stress through exercise | reduce stress |
| mental health（心理健康） | care about mental health | psychological well-being |
| energy levels（精力水平） | boost energy levels | improve energy |
| a healthy habit（健康习惯） | develop a healthy habit | build a good habit |
| pay attention to（注意） | pay attention to diet and rest | be mindful of |

## 四、高频句型

- I try to … by …, which helps me …（我尽量通过…，这帮助我…）
- The key to staying healthy, in my view, is …（在我看来，保持健康的关键是…）
- I've found that … makes a real difference to …（我发现…对…有实实在在的影响）
- It's important to …, because …（重要的是…，因为…）
- Compared with before, I now …（与以前相比，我现在…）

## 五、易错提醒

- "health" 是名词，"healthy" 是形容词，注意区分；
- 描述习惯用一般现在时，描述过去的改变用过去时或现在完成时；
- 避免只说 "good for health"，换用 maintain good health / relieve stress 等具体表达。
