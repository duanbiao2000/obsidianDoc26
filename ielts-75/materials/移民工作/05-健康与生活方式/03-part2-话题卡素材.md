# 健康与生活方式 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材：一个你养成的健康习惯（可一材多用）。

## 素材骨架（核心故事）

**主题**：一个你坚持的健康习惯——养成过程、怎么做、收获

- **是什么**：什么习惯（跑步、早睡、健康饮食）
- **养成过程**：之前的状态、为什么开始改变
- **做法**：具体怎么执行（频率、方式）
- **收获**：身体与精神上带来的变化

## 话题卡 1：Describe a healthy habit you have

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：running regularly, started about a year ago
- 点 1：why I started — felt tired and low on energy, wanted a change
- 点 2：how I do it — three times a week, in the park near my home
- 收尾：what it has brought — more energy, better sleep, a sense of achievement

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about a healthy habit I've developed — running regularly.
First of all, I started about a year ago, when I realised I felt tired all the time and had low energy. I decided that I needed a change, so I began going running in the park near my home.
Then, my routine is to run about three times a week, usually in the evening. At first it was quite hard — I could barely finish a short distance — but I kept going, and gradually it became easier.
In the end, the habit has brought me real benefits. Not only have I become more energetic and slept better, but I also feel a real sense of achievement every time I complete a run. It has become part of my life now.

## 话题卡 2：Describe a change you made in your lifestyle（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about a change I made in my lifestyle — taking up running.
- 重点：改变前的状态、改变的过程、遇到的困难与坚持
- 收尾：这次改变带来的整体影响

## 话题卡 3：Describe something that improved your health（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：There's something that has really improved my health — running regularly.
- 重点：具体改善（精力、睡眠、心情），用对比说明
- 收尾：我会继续坚持，并推荐给身边人

## 避免背稿感

- 健康话题要有"前后对比"，对比让故事真实且有说服力；
- 一材多用时，焦点按题目调整：健康习惯 / 生活改变 / 健康改善。
