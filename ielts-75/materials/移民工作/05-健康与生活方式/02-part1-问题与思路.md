# 健康与生活方式 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. Do you exercise regularly?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 具体运动 → E 频率与收获。

**示范**：Yes, I try to exercise regularly. I usually go running about three times a week, and I find it a great way to keep fit and relieve stress. It also gives me more energy for the rest of the day, which makes a real difference to my daily life.

## 2. What do you do to keep healthy?

**思路**：A 列举 → R 原因 → E 细节（运动+饮食+睡眠）。

**示范**：I try to keep healthy in a few ways. First of all, I do regular exercise, and I also pay attention to my diet, eating more fresh food and cutting down on sugar. What I find most important, though, is getting enough sleep — a regular routine really helps me stay energetic.

## 3. Is your diet healthy?

**思路**：A 表态（尽量）→ R 做法 → E 具体调整。

**示范**：I'd say my diet is fairly healthy. The main reason is that I try to maintain a balanced diet with plenty of vegetables and fresh food, and I've cut down on junk food. That said, I still allow myself the occasional treat, because I think balance matters more than strict rules.

## 4. How important is sleep to you?

**思路**：A 表态（非常重要）→ R 原因 → E 具体做法。

**示范**：I'd say sleep is extremely important. The main reason is that it directly affects my energy and concentration, and without enough sleep, I find it hard to stay focused during the day. So I try to avoid staying up late and keep a regular bedtime as much as possible.

## 5. Have you ever changed your lifestyle?

**思路**：A 回答（有）→ R 变化 → E 具体行动与结果。

**示范**：Yes, I have. I used to stay up late regularly and rarely exercised, but I changed that about a year ago. I started going running and adjusted my bedtime, and the result has been quite noticeable — I feel healthier and more energetic than before.

## 6. Do you think people are more health-conscious nowadays?

**思路**：A 表态（更注重了）→ R 原因 → E 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say people are generally more health-conscious now. The main reason is that information about healthy living is much more widely available, and people are more aware of the link between lifestyle and health. For example, more people pay attention to their diet and exercise than a decade ago, which I think is a positive trend.
