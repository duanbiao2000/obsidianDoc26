# 环境与社会议题 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材：一次你参与的环保/公益活动（可一材多用）。

## 素材骨架（核心故事）

**主题**：一次参与环保/公益活动的经历

- **背景**：什么活动、什么时候、谁组织的
- **过程**：做了什么、有什么具体细节
- **收获**：感受、认识、对态度的影响

## 话题卡 1：Describe an activity that helped the environment

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：a community clean-up activity I took part in last spring
- 点 1：why I joined — wanted to do something for the local environment
- 点 2：what we did — picked up litter in the park and along the river, sorted waste
- 收尾：what I gained — collective action matters, changed my habits

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about an activity that helped the environment — a community clean-up I took part in last spring.
First of all, I joined because I wanted to do something practical for the local environment, rather than just talking about environmental problems. The activity was organised by a local group, and it took place in the park and along the river near my home.
Then, what we did was quite hands-on. We spent the morning picking up litter, separating recyclables from general waste, and putting up notices to remind people to keep the area clean. It was tiring, but seeing the park become tidy again was really satisfying.
In the end, the experience taught me that collective action matters — small actions by individuals, when done together, can make a real difference. It also changed my daily habits; since then, I've been more careful about reducing waste and recycling.

## 话题卡 2：Describe a social activity you took part in（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about a social activity I took part in — the community clean-up.
- 重点：活动的社会意义（社区凝聚力、公众意识）
- 收尾：这类活动对社区的价值

## 话题卡 3：Describe an experience that changed your opinion（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about an experience that changed my opinion about environmental protection.
- 重点：之前的态度（觉得个人行动没用）→ 活动后的改变
- 收尾：这次经历让我明白每个人的行动都有意义

## 避免背稿感

- 环保话题容易"喊口号"，用具体行动与真实感受（累但满足）支撑；
- 一材多用时，焦点按题目调整：环保活动 / 社会活动 / 观念转变。
