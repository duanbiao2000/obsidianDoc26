# 环境与社会议题 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. Do you think environmental problems are serious?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 原因 → E 举例。

**示范**：I'd say they are very serious. The main reason is that issues like pollution and climate change already affect our daily lives, from the air we breathe to the weather we experience. For example, extreme weather events seem to happen more often than before, which shows the problem is real and urgent.

## 2. Do you do anything to protect the environment?

**思路**：A 列举行动 → R 动机 → E 细节。

**示范**：Yes, I try to do a few things. I recycle waste, reduce the use of plastic bags, and choose public transport whenever possible. The main reason is that I believe small individual actions add up, and if everyone does their part, it makes a real difference to the environment.

## 3. Is the environment a big concern in your country?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 原因 → E 具体问题与应对。

**示范**：I'd say it's a growing concern. The main reason is that rapid development has brought visible environmental problems, such as air pollution in big cities and waste management issues. That said, people and the government are paying more attention now, with more measures being taken to improve the situation.

## 4. What should individuals do to help the environment?

**思路**：A 列举 → R 原因 → E 举例。

**示范**：I'd say individuals can do a few practical things. First of all, reducing waste and recycling in daily life, and second, choosing greener transport, such as cycling or public transport. For example, even simple habits like saving water and electricity make a meaningful contribution over time.

## 5. Do you think it's the government's job to protect the environment?

**思路**：A 折中表态 → R 政府职责 → E 个人与企业的责任 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say protecting the environment is mainly the government's responsibility, but not exclusively. Governments can set rules, invest in green infrastructure and enforce environmental standards, which individuals cannot do alone. That said, companies and individuals also need to take responsibility, because environmental protection requires a joint effort from everyone.

## 6. Have you ever taken part in any environmental activity?

**思路**：A 回答 → R 动机 → E 具体经历。

**示范**：Yes, I once took part in a community clean-up activity. I joined because I wanted to contribute to my local environment, and it turned out to be quite rewarding — picking up litter in the park with other volunteers made me feel that collective action can really improve the place we live in.
