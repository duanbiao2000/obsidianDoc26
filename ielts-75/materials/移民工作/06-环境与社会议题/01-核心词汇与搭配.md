# 环境与社会议题 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、环境与保护

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| environmental problems（环境问题） | serious environmental problems | environmental issues |
| pollution（污染） | air / water pollution | contamination |
| climate change（气候变化） | fight climate change | global warming |
| protect the environment（保护环境） | protect the environment | preserve the environment |
| recycle（回收） | recycle waste | reuse and recycle |
| reduce waste（减少浪费） | reduce waste in daily life | cut down on waste |
| sustainable（可持续的） | a sustainable lifestyle | environmentally friendly |
| green / eco-friendly（环保的） | eco-friendly habits | green, environmentally friendly |

## 二、社会议题

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| social issues（社会问题） | social issues such as … | social problems |
| poverty（贫困） | reduce poverty | the problem of poverty |
| inequality（不平等） | social inequality | the gap between rich and poor |
| traffic congestion（交通拥堵） | traffic congestion in cities | heavy traffic |
| housing shortage（住房短缺） | a housing shortage | lack of housing |
| public concern（公众关注） | raise public concern | make people concerned about |

## 三、责任与行动

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| take responsibility（承担责任） | take responsibility for the environment | shoulder responsibility |
| individual action（个人行动） | individual actions matter | what individuals do |
| government measures（政府措施） | government measures to solve … | steps taken by the government |
| raise awareness（提高意识） | raise awareness of environmental issues | make people aware of |
| make a difference（产生影响） | small actions make a difference | have an impact |
| joint effort（共同努力） | a joint effort is needed | collective effort |

## 四、高频句型

- Environmental problems have become …, which means …（环境问题已成为…，这意味着…）
- I believe that …, because …（我相信…，因为…）
- It's important for … to …, as …（…应该…，因为…）
- Small actions by individuals, combined with …, can make a real difference.（个人的小行动，加上…，能产生真正的影响）
- Although …, I still believe that …（虽然…，但我仍然相信…）

## 五、易错提醒

- 环境类词汇注意搭配：protect the environment / fight climate change，
  避免中式表达；
- 谈社会议题保持客观建设性，先指出问题再给建议；
- 避免空喊口号，用具体方案（回收、措施、责任）支撑观点。
