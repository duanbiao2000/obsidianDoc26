# 环境与社会议题 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。本话题重点练"责任分担"多角度论证。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / In my view, … / It depends on …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：Who should be responsible for protecting the environment?

**思路**：观点（三方共同负责，政府为主导）→ 政府/企业/个人各负其责 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say environmental protection is a shared responsibility, with governments taking the lead. Governments can set strict standards, invest in green energy and enforce regulations, which neither individuals nor companies can do alone. Companies, meanwhile, should reduce pollution and adopt sustainable production, and individuals can contribute through everyday actions like recycling. For example, a single person's effort is small, but when governments, companies and people act together, the impact is far greater.

## 问题 2：Is it too late to solve environmental problems?

**思路**：观点（不晚，但需立刻行动）→ 原因（问题严重但已有解决方案）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say it's not too late, but the window for action is closing, so we need to act now. The main reason is that environmental problems are serious, but we already have the technology and knowledge to address them — renewable energy, waste reduction and better planning all work. For example, many countries have successfully cut pollution in a few years with the right policies. That said, time is of the essence, which means every delay makes the task harder. So I believe it's still possible, provided we act decisively.

## 问题 3：Should people be forced to live more environmentally friendly lives?

**思路**：观点（引导为主，部分强制）→ 原因（强制的边界）→ 举例 → 折中。

**示范**：I'd say a balance is needed — some measures can be mandatory, while others should be encouraged. It's reasonable to enforce rules on issues that affect everyone, such as waste sorting or emissions standards, because individual choices can harm the shared environment. For example, banning harmful single-use products can produce results quickly. That said, most lifestyle changes are better encouraged through education and incentives, since forcing people too much can cause resistance. So I believe regulation and encouragement should work together.

## 问题 4：What can individuals do to help solve social problems?

**思路**：观点（个人行动有力量）→ 方法（参与、发声、改变习惯）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say individuals can play a meaningful role in solving social problems. The main reason is that social problems are shaped by millions of daily choices, so changing those choices can create real momentum. For example, volunteering, supporting fair products, and speaking out on issues all add up. That said, individual action alone is not enough — it works best when combined with policy change and collective action. Still, I believe every individual contribution is a necessary starting point.

## 问题 5：How can cities become more sustainable?

**思路**：观点（多管齐下）→ 方法（规划、交通、能源）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say cities can become more sustainable through careful planning and new technologies. First of all, by designing cities to reduce the need for long commutes — mixed-use neighbourhoods where people can live, work and shop nearby. Then, by promoting clean public transport and renewable energy for buildings. For example, cities with good cycling networks, efficient public transport and green buildings have significantly lower emissions. Beyond that, involving citizens in planning makes sustainability efforts more effective, because a city is shaped by its people, not just its government.
