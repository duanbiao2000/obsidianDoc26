# 职场与同事 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、工作环境与同事

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| workplace / office（工作场所） | a friendly workplace | the office, the working environment |
| colleagues / co-workers（同事） | my colleagues are … | the people I work with |
| teamwork（团队合作） | good teamwork | working as a team, collaboration |
| cooperate（合作） | cooperate with colleagues | work together, collaborate |
| team spirit（团队精神） | a strong team spirit | a sense of teamwork |
| communication（沟通） | clear communication | effective communication |
| trust（信任） | build trust with colleagues | earn trust |

## 二、领导与管理

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| leader / manager（领导） | an approachable leader | a supportive manager |
| leadership（领导力） | develop leadership skills | the ability to lead |
| delegate（授权/分派） | delegate tasks | assign tasks |
| motivate（激励） | motivate the team | encourage, inspire |
| decision-making（决策） | involved in decision-making | take part in decisions |
| organisational skills（组织能力） | strong organisational skills | the ability to plan and organise |

## 三、评价与感受

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| reliable（可靠的） | a reliable colleague | someone I can count on |
| helpful（乐于助人的） | always helpful | willing to help |
| work well together（配合默契） | we work well together | we get along well at work |
| a positive atmosphere（积极氛围） | a positive working atmosphere | a supportive environment |
| resolve conflicts（化解冲突） | resolve conflicts calmly | handle disagreements |
| rely on（依赖） | rely on my team | depend on the team |

## 四、高频句型

- My workplace is …, which means …（我的工作场所…，这意味着…）
- What I appreciate most about my colleagues is …（我最欣赏同事的是…）
- We work as a team, where each of us …（我们作为团队工作，各自负责…）
- It was … who helped me …（正是…帮助我…）
- Compared with working alone, I find teamwork …（与独自工作相比，我觉得团队合作…）

## 五、易错提醒

- "colleague" 指同事，不用于上下级；"manager/supervisor" 指上级；
- 描述团队用一般现在时，回忆具体事件用过去时；
- 避免只用 "good team player"，换用 cooperate / contribute / reliable 等具体表达。
