# 职场与同事 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材：一位与你合作默契的同事（人物类，可一材多用）。

## 素材骨架（核心故事）

**主题**：一位乐于助人、配合默契的同事

- **人物**：职位、性格、你们如何合作
- **事件**：一次具体的合作经历（项目、困难、如何配合）
- **影响**：他/她如何影响了你的工作方式与团队氛围

## 话题卡 1：Describe a colleague you like

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：a senior colleague in my team, who has always been supportive
- 点 1：what he/she is like — patient, knowledgeable, approachable
- 点 2：a specific event — helped me through a difficult project, shared experience
- 收尾：impact — learned a lot, team atmosphere

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about a colleague I like very much — a senior colleague in my team.
First of all, she is incredibly patient and knowledgeable, and what makes her special is that she is always approachable — no question is too small to ask. This makes her easy to work with and trusted by everyone in the team.
Then, there's one experience I remember clearly. When I joined a difficult project and felt out of my depth, she spent time explaining the background and sharing her own experience, which helped me get up to speed quickly.
In the end, working with her has taught me a lot, not just about the work itself, but about how to support others. She has made our team a much more positive place to work.

## 话题卡 2：Describe a person who helped you at work（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about a person who helped me a lot at work — my senior colleague.
- 重点：具体帮助过程（项目上的指导）、你当时的处境与变化
- 收尾：这份帮助带来的影响，以及你想把这份支持传递下去

## 话题卡 3：Describe a team you enjoyed working in（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about a team I really enjoyed working in — my current project team.
- 重点：团队氛围、分工合作、一次成功的项目经历
- 收尾：好团队的关键是什么，对你的影响

## 避免背稿感

- 人物类故事要有"场景感"，把帮助的过程讲具体（说了什么、做了什么）；
- 一材多用时，焦点按题目调整：描述同事 / 感谢帮助 / 团队经历。
