# 职场与同事 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. What is your workplace like?

**思路**：A 描述环境 → R 特点 → E 具体细节。

**示范**：My workplace is quite a friendly and open environment. The main reason is that the office is designed to encourage communication, and there's a shared area where people can discuss ideas. I find it a comfortable place to work, which helps everyone stay motivated.

## 2. Do you get along well with your colleagues?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 原因 → E 具体场景。

**示范**：Yes, I get along really well with my colleagues. The main reason is that we help each other out and communicate openly, so it's easy to resolve problems. For example, when I was new, my teammates spent time guiding me, which made a big difference.

## 3. Do you prefer working alone or in a team?

**思路**：A 表态（可折中）→ R 各自好处 → E 场景。

**示范**：I'd say I prefer working in a team. The main reason is that teamwork brings together different perspectives, and I learn a lot from my colleagues. That said, I also value working alone when I need deep focus, so the best situation is a mix of both.

## 4. What qualities make a good colleague?

**思路**：A 列举品质 → R 逐点解释 → E 总结。

**示范**：I'd say a good colleague needs a few qualities. First of all, reliability — you need someone you can count on to deliver their part. Then, communication skills, because misunderstandings can easily slow a team down. What I value most, though, is a cooperative attitude, since work is rarely done alone.

## 5. Has technology changed the way colleagues work together?

**思路**：A 表态（改变了）→ R 具体变化 → E 举例。

**示范**：Yes, it has changed collaboration a lot. The main reason is that communication tools and shared platforms allow people to work together even when they are not in the same place. For example, my team often shares documents and holds meetings online, which makes collaboration much more flexible.

## 6. Do you think a good manager is important for a team?

**思路**：A 表态（非常重要）→ R 原因 → E 举例。

**示范**：I'd say a good manager is extremely important. The main reason is that a manager sets the tone for the team — a supportive one motivates people and helps resolve conflicts, while a poor one can lower the whole team's morale. For example, my current manager listens to our ideas, and the whole team is more engaged because of that.
