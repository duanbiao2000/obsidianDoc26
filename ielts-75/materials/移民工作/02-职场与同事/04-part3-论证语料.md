# 职场与同事 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。本话题多涉及团队合作、领导、职场文化。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / In my view, … / It depends on …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：Is teamwork more important than individual work?

**思路**：观点（团队更重要，但个体也不可缺）→ 原因（互补、效率）→ 举例 → 折中。

**示范**：I'd say teamwork is generally more important in modern work, though individual effort is still essential. The main reason is that most projects require a range of skills, and a good team can combine different strengths to achieve more than any single person. For example, in my team, the final results always come from combining everyone's contributions. That said, individual work still matters, especially for tasks requiring deep focus. So the best situation is strong individuals working well together.

## 问题 2：What makes a good leader?

**思路**：观点（多品质组合）→ 逐点（以身作则、倾听、激励）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say a good leader needs several qualities. First of all, the ability to set a clear direction, so the team knows what it's working towards. Then, being a good listener — a leader who takes in team members' ideas earns trust and makes better decisions. What I find most important, though, is the ability to motivate, because a motivated team will go through difficulties without giving up. A leader who combines these qualities can truly bring a team together.

## 问题 3：Should companies encourage social activities among colleagues?

**思路**：观点（应该，适度）→ 原因（关系、协作）→ 举例 → 让步（占用个人时间）。

**示范**：I'd say companies should encourage social activities, but in moderation. The main reason is that socialising helps colleagues build trust and understand each other, which then improves cooperation at work. For example, teams that know each other well usually solve problems faster and communicate more smoothly. That said, these activities shouldn't take up too much personal time, or they may feel like an obligation. So I believe a light, voluntary approach works best.

## 问题 4：Do you think gender diversity in the workplace matters?

**思路**：观点（重要）→ 原因（不同视角、创新、公平）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say gender diversity in the workplace matters a lot. The main reason is that diverse teams bring different perspectives and approaches, which leads to better decision-making and more innovation. For example, teams with a mix of backgrounds are more likely to spot problems and opportunities that a homogeneous team might miss. Beyond that, diversity is also about fairness and giving everyone equal opportunities. So I believe a diverse workplace is a stronger and more positive one.

## 问题 5：Will remote work continue to be popular?

**思路**：观点（会保持一定比例）→ 原因（灵活性、成本）→ 让步（协作与归属感）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say remote work will remain popular, but probably not as the only option for most people. The main reason is that it offers real benefits — flexibility, no commute, and a better work-life balance for many. That said, remote work also has drawbacks, such as weaker team connection and communication difficulties, which matter for collaboration. So I believe the future will be a hybrid model, where people combine remote and office work according to their needs.
