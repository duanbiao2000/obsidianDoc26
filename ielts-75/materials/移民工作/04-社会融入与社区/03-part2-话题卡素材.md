# 社会融入与社区 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材：一次社区志愿活动/邻里帮助的经历（可一材多用）。

## 素材骨架（核心故事）

**主题**：一次社区志愿活动（或邻居帮助你的经历）

- **背景**：什么活动/事件、什么时候、为什么参与
- **过程**：做了什么、有什么具体细节（分工、互动）
- **收获**：感受到什么、带来了什么影响

## 话题卡 1：Describe a voluntary activity you took part in

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：a community food drive last winter
- 点 1：why I joined — to give back, wanted to help
- 点 2：what we did — collected and sorted supplies, handed them out
- 收尾：what I felt — rewarding, community spirit

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about a voluntary activity I took part in — a community food drive last winter.
First of all, I joined because I wanted to give back to the community, especially during the cold season when some families were struggling. It was organised by a local group, and we needed volunteers to collect and sort the supplies.
Then, the work itself was quite hands-on. My role was to sort the food into boxes and hand them out at the distribution point, and what struck me was how many people came to donate and help — neighbours, shop owners, even children.
In the end, it was a deeply rewarding experience. Seeing people receive the supplies with gratitude made me realise how much a community can achieve when everyone contributes, and it strengthened my sense of belonging here.

## 话题卡 2：Describe a neighbour who helped you（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about a neighbour who helped me when I moved in.
- 重点：具体帮助（介绍社区、帮忙搬东西、日常关照）
- 收尾：这份帮助让我感受到社区的温暖，也让我学会了帮助他人

## 话题卡 3：Describe an event you enjoyed（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about a community event I really enjoyed — the food drive.
- 重点：活动氛围、人们的反应、你参与的部分
- 收尾：为什么难忘——社区精神与温暖

## 避免背稿感

- 社区类故事要讲"人"，把互动细节（捐赠的人、收到物资的人的反应）讲出来；
- 一材多用时，焦点按题目调整：志愿活动 / 邻居帮助 / 难忘活动。
