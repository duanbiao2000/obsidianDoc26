# 社会融入与社区 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。本话题多涉及社区、志愿服务、社会凝聚。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / In my view, … / It depends on …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：How important is community to individuals?

**思路**：观点（非常重要）→ 原因（归属、支持、认同）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say community is extremely important to individuals. The main reason is that a strong community provides a sense of belonging and a support network, which people rely on in difficult times. For example, people who feel connected to their community are often happier and more resilient when facing problems. Beyond that, community life also gives people a sense of identity and purpose. So I believe community is a fundamental part of a good life.

## 问题 2：Should young people do more voluntary work?

**思路**：观点（应该）→ 原因（成长、社会）→ 举例 → 让步（学业压力）。

**示范**：I'd say young people should do more voluntary work. The main reason is that it builds valuable skills and character — by helping others, young people learn responsibility, empathy and cooperation. For example, volunteering at community events can also help them understand social issues firsthand. That said, it should not come at the cost of their studies, so a reasonable balance matters. Still, the benefits of voluntary work, both for individuals and society, are well worth the time.

## 问题 3：Is it harder to make friends in a big city than in a small town?

**思路**：观点（城市更难）→ 原因（流动性大、节奏快）→ 举例 → 让步（机会更多）。

**示范**：I'd say it is generally harder to make friends in a big city. The main reason is that people in cities are often busy and mobile, so relationships take more effort to build. For example, neighbours in a big apartment building may live close but barely know each other. That said, cities also offer more opportunities to meet people through work, hobbies and community groups. So while it takes more effort, it is certainly possible to build a social circle in a city.

## 问题 4：What makes a community a good place to live?

**思路**：观点（多因素）→ 逐点（安全、支持、活动）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say a good community needs a combination of things. First of all, safety — people need to feel secure in their daily life. Then, a supportive atmosphere, where neighbours are willing to help each other. What I find equally important is shared activities, because community events bring people together and strengthen social bonds. For example, a community with regular events and strong local groups usually feels much more alive and welcoming.

## 问题 5：Should governments invest more in local communities?

**思路**：观点（应该）→ 原因（民生、凝聚力、公平）→ 让步（预算有限）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say governments should invest more in local communities. The main reason is that community facilities — such as libraries, parks and community centres — directly affect people's quality of life and strengthen social cohesion. For example, funding local activities can bring people together and prevent isolation. That said, governments have limited budgets and many competing priorities, so the investment should be efficient and focused on what matters most to local people.
