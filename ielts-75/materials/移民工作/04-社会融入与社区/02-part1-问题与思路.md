# 社会融入与社区 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. What is your neighbourhood like?

**思路**：A 描述 → R 特点 → E 细节。

**示范**：My neighbourhood is a quiet and friendly area. The main reason is that most people here know each other and are always willing to help. For example, neighbours often look out for each other, which makes it feel like a safe and close-knit community.

## 2. Do you know your neighbours well?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 情况 → E 具体场景。

**示范**：Yes, I know most of my neighbours fairly well. The main reason is that we often meet in the shared areas and take part in local activities together. For example, we have a neighbourhood group that shares news and helps with small problems, which keeps us connected.

## 3. Do you take part in any community activities?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 原因 → E 具体活动。

**示范**：Yes, I do. I take part in community clean-up events and some local cultural activities. The main reason is that I believe community activities help build stronger social bonds, and it's a good way to get to know the people around me.

## 4. Do you think it's important to help the local community?

**思路**：A 表态（重要）→ R 原因 → E 举例。

**示范**：I'd say it's very important. The main reason is that a community only works well when its members contribute to it. For example, small acts like volunteering at local events or helping neighbours make the area a better place to live for everyone.

## 5. Have you ever done any voluntary work?

**思路**：A 回答 → R 动机 → E 具体经历。

**示范**：Yes, I have. I volunteered at a community food drive last winter. I decided to do it because I wanted to give back to the community, and it turned out to be a rewarding experience — seeing people receive the supplies made me realise how much small actions can mean.

## 6. Would you like to organise a community event?

**思路**：A 表态（愿意）→ R 原因 → E 想组织什么活动。

**示范**：Yes, I'd love to. The main reason is that organising events is a great way to bring people together and strengthen the sense of belonging in a community. If I had the chance, I'd organise a neighbourhood cultural festival, where people could share food and traditions from different backgrounds.
