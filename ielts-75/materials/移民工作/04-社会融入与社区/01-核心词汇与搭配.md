# 社会融入与社区 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、社区与邻里

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| neighbourhood / community（社区） | a friendly neighbourhood | a close-knit community |
| neighbours（邻居） | my neighbours are … | the people living nearby |
| local community（当地社区） | get involved in the local community | take part in community life |
| community centre（社区中心） | activities at the community centre | local community facilities |
| integration（融入） | social integration | fitting into a new society |
| sense of belonging（归属感） | a sense of belonging | feeling part of the community |

## 二、志愿与活动

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| volunteer（志愿者/做志愿） | volunteer at a community centre | do volunteer work |
| voluntary work（志愿工作） | take on voluntary work | unpaid work for the community |
| charity（慈善） | work for a charity | support charitable causes |
| raise awareness（提高意识） | raise awareness of … | make people aware of … |
| organise（组织） | organise a community event | run, arrange an event |
| give back（回馈） | give back to the community | contribute to the community |

## 三、关系与氛围

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| supportive（支持的） | a supportive community | a community that helps each other |
| close-knit（关系紧密的） | a close-knit neighbourhood | a tight community |
| helpful（乐于助人的） | everyone is helpful | people are willing to help |
| trust（信任） | build trust within the community | create trust |
| social bonds（社会纽带） | strengthen social bonds | bring people closer together |
| inclusive（包容的） | an inclusive community | a community that welcomes everyone |

## 四、高频句型

- My neighbourhood is …, which means …（我的社区…，这意味着…）
- What I enjoy most about the community is …（我最喜欢社区的是…）
- I decided to volunteer because …（我决定做志愿是因为…）
- It was … who helped me …（正是…帮助了我…）
- Community activities help …, because …（社区活动有助于…，因为…）

## 五、易错提醒

- "community" 可指社区或群体，根据语境选译；
- "volunteer" 可作动词（做志愿）与名词（志愿者），注意区分；
- 避免只用 "nice people"，换用 supportive / close-knit / inclusive 等具体词汇。
