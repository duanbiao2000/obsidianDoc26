# 人物与关系 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、人物描述

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| describe（描述） | describe someone as … | portray, talk about |
| admire（钦佩） | admire someone for … | respect, look up to |
| influence（影响） | have a great influence on me | shape, affect |
| support（支持） | support me unconditionally | back me up |
| reliable（可靠的） | a reliable friend | someone I can count on |
| trustworthy（可信赖的） | a trustworthy person | dependable |
| warm-hearted（热心的） | a warm-hearted person | kind, caring |
| hard-working（勤奋的） | a hard-working person | diligent, hardworking |

## 二、关系与互动

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| get along（相处） | get along well with … | have a good relationship |
| keep in touch（保持联系） | keep in touch with friends | stay connected |
| close friend（密友） | a close friend of mine | a good friend |
| bond（纽带） | a strong bond | a close connection |
| trust（信任） | build trust | earn trust |
| rely on（依赖） | rely on each other | count on each other |
| have a lot in common（有很多共同点） | have a lot in common | share many interests |

## 三、影响与感受

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| make a difference（产生影响） | make a real difference | have a real impact |
| change my life（改变我的人生） | change my life | transform my life |
| learn from（向…学习） | learn from him/her | take … as a role model |
| look up to（仰慕） | look up to someone | admire |
| inspiration（鼓舞） | an inspiration to me | a source of motivation |
| gratitude（感激） | feel grateful to … | thankful |

## 四、高频句型

- The person I'd like to talk about is …, who …（我想谈的人是…，他/她…）
- What I admire most about him/her is …（我最钦佩他/她的是…）
- He/She has had a great influence on me, because …（他/她对我影响很大，因为…）
- We get along well, as we …（我们相处得很好，因为我们…）
- It was … who taught me that …（正是…教会了我…）

## 五、易错提醒

- 描述人物避免形容词堆砌，用具体事件支撑（"他说的一句话/做的一件事"）；
- 谈关系用现在时，回忆过去事件用过去时；
- 避免只讲"他很好"，要说"他好在哪里、对我意味着什么"。
