# 人物与关系 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。本话题多涉及友谊、家庭、人际关系。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / In my view, … / It depends on …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：Is it better to have a few close friends or many acquaintances?

**思路**：观点（少数密友更好）→ 原因（深度、信任）→ 举例 → 让步（人脉也重要）。

**示范**：I'd say it's better to have a few close friends. The main reason is that close friendships are built on trust and deep understanding, which requires time and effort — something that can't be spread across too many people. For example, when you face a real problem, it's usually a close friend you turn to, not an acquaintance. That said, having a wider network of acquaintances also brings opportunities, so the ideal is a balance: a few deep friendships, plus broader connections.

## 问题 2：Do you think family relationships change as people grow up?

**思路**：观点（会变化）→ 原因（独立、距离、角色）→ 举例 → 总结（感情可以更深）。

**示范**：I'd say family relationships do change as people grow up. The main reason is that adults become more independent, and distance or different lifestyles can change the way families interact. For example, many young adults move away from home and only see their families during holidays, which is different from childhood. That said, this doesn't necessarily mean the bonds weaken — in many cases, once people mature, they appreciate their family more and the relationship becomes deeper, even if contact is less frequent.

## 问题 3：How important is family in a person's life?

**思路**：观点（极其重要）→ 原因（支持、塑造、归属）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say family is extremely important in a person's life. The main reason is that family provides the first and most enduring support system — it shapes our values, gives us a sense of belonging, and stands by us in difficult times. For example, many people credit their family for the confidence and strength they draw on throughout their lives. Beyond that, family relationships teach us how to care for others, which influences all our other relationships.

## 问题 4：Do you think technology has changed the way people make friends?

**思路**：观点（改变了，且更便利）→ 原因（渠道多、成本低）→ 举例 → 让步（深度可能受影响）。

**示范**：I'd say technology has definitely changed the way people make friends. The main reason is that social media and messaging apps allow people to connect with others anywhere, at any time, which was impossible before. For example, people now make friends through shared interests online, even across countries. That said, online friendships can be more superficial, and some people find that face-to-face interaction still matters most for deep relationships. So technology has expanded the possibilities, but not replaced the need for real connection.

## 问题 5：Should children spend more time with family or with friends?

**思路**：观点（两者都需要，重在平衡）→ 家庭的意义 → 朋友的意义 → 折中。

**示范**：I'd say children need a balanced amount of time with both. Spending time with family gives children a sense of security and teaches them values, while time with friends helps them develop social skills and independence. For example, through play and group activities with friends, children learn cooperation and how to handle disagreements. That said, the balance matters — family time provides the emotional foundation, while friendships help children grow into social individuals.
