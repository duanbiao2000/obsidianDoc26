# 人物与关系 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材：一位你钦佩、对你影响深的人（人物类经典素材，最适合练一材多用）。

## 素材骨架（核心故事）

**主题**：一位你钦佩的人（如一位老师/家人/前辈）——品质 + 事件 + 影响

- **人物**：是谁、什么身份、最突出的品质
- **事件**：一个具体事件（他/她做了什么、说了什么）
- **影响**：对你性格、选择、价值观的影响

## 话题卡 1：Describe a person you admire

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：my grandfather, a hard-working and honest man
- 点 1：what he is like — hard-working, honest, calm
- 点 2：a specific event — worked hard to support the family, taught me the value of effort
- 收尾：influence — role model, shaped my values

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about a person I deeply admire — my grandfather.
First of all, he is a hard-working and honest man. He spent his whole life working hard to support his family, and what I admire most is his calm attitude — no matter how difficult things were, he never complained.
Then, there's one thing I remember clearly. When I was a child and complained about a difficult task, he told me that effort always pays off, and he showed me by example how he had built his life step by step.
In the end, he has had a great influence on me. He is my role model, and the values he taught me — perseverance and honesty — have shaped the way I approach life to this day.

## 话题卡 2：Describe a person who influenced you（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：There's a person who influenced me deeply — my grandfather.
- 重点：从"钦佩的品质"转向"具体影响"：童年的一句话如何影响你现在的选择
- 收尾：如果遇到困难，会想起他的话

## 话题卡 3：Describe a family member you are close to（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about a family member I'm very close to — my grandfather.
- 重点：亲密的表现（陪伴、沟通、关心），用日常细节（一起下棋、聊天）
- 收尾：这份亲情的意义

## 避免背稿感

- 人物类故事最有温度，把那个"关键场景"讲具体（说了什么话、做了什么事）；
- 一材多用是本话题的核心训练：同一个人物，不同题目的开场与重点必须调整。
