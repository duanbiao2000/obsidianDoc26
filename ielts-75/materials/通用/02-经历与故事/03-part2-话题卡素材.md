# 经历与故事 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材 1：一次克服困难的经历（最通用，可套多张话题卡）。
> 核心素材 2：一次全新的体验。

## 素材骨架 1（克服困难）

**主题**：一次克服困难的经历

- **背景**：什么困难、什么时候、什么处境
- **过程**：如何应对（行动细节）、有没有人帮助
- **结果与收获**：结果如何、学到了什么

## 话题卡 1：Describe a time you overcame a challenge

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：the presentation I gave last year, which I was terrified of
- 点 1：the challenge — nervous, large audience, a lot of material
- 点 2：how I coped — practised repeatedly, asked for feedback, prepared thoroughly
- 收尾：result — it went well, learned confidence and preparation

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about a time I overcame a challenge — giving a presentation in front of a large audience last year.
First of all, the challenge was that I was extremely nervous about speaking in public, and the topic required a lot of technical detail, so I was worried about losing the audience's attention.
Then, what I did was prepare thoroughly. I practised my speech repeatedly, asked a teacher for feedback on both the content and my delivery, and even recorded myself to check my pace. With each round of practice, I felt more confident.
In the end, it went much better than I expected — the audience asked good questions, and I walked away feeling proud. That experience taught me that preparation is the best way to overcome fear, and I've applied that lesson ever since.

## 话题卡 2：Describe an important decision you made（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about an important decision — choosing to prepare seriously for that presentation.
- 重点：决定前的犹豫（想放弃）、决定后的坚持
- 收尾：这个决定带来的改变

## 素材骨架 2（新体验）

**主题**：一次第一次做某事的经历（如第一次独自旅行/第一次做志愿者）

- **背景**：是什么体验、为什么去尝试
- **过程**：做了什么、感受如何（新鲜、紧张、兴奋）
- **收获**：学到什么、对以后的影响

## 话题卡 3：Describe something you did for the first time（一材多用）

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：the first time I travelled alone last summer
- 点 1：why I did it — wanted to be more independent
- 点 2：what happened — planned the trip myself, navigated a new city
- 收尾：what I gained — confidence, independence

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about something I did for the first time — travelling alone last summer.
First of all, I had never travelled on my own before, and I decided to try it because I wanted to become more independent and see whether I could handle everything myself.
Then, the experience itself was both exciting and a bit daunting. I planned the whole trip myself, from booking tickets to choosing where to stay, and navigating a new city on my own gave me a real sense of freedom.
In the end, it was a rewarding experience. I learned to plan, to solve problems on my own, and I gained a lot of confidence — it has made me more independent ever since.

## 避免背稿感

- 故事要讲出"变化感"：之前的状态 → 过程 → 之后的自己；
- 两个素材可组合使用：新体验常与克服困难同时发生，灵活组合。
