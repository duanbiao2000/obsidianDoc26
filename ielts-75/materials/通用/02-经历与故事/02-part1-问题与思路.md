# 经历与故事 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. Have you had any memorable experiences lately?

**思路**：A 回答 → R 为什么难忘 → E 细节。

**示范**：Yes, I had one recently — I went on a short trip with friends to a mountain town. It was memorable because it was the first time in a long while that I completely switched off from daily pressure, and the scenery was beautiful. I still think about that relaxing weekend often.

## 2. Do you prefer trying new things or sticking to what you know?

**思路**：A 表态（喜欢新事物）→ R 原因 → E 举例 → 让步。

**示范**：I'd say I prefer trying new things. The main reason is that new experiences broaden my horizons and often teach me something useful. For example, I recently took up a new hobby and learned far more than I expected. That said, I also value the comfort of familiar routines, so it's a balance.

## 3. What's the most challenging thing you've done?

**思路**：A 点出 → R 为什么难 → E 如何克服。

**示范**：I'd say the most challenging thing I've done was giving a major presentation in front of a large audience. The main reason is that I was nervous and had to prepare a lot of material. What helped was that I practised repeatedly and asked for feedback, and in the end, it went much better than I expected.

## 4. Have you ever done something for the first time recently?

**思路**：A 回答 → R 感受 → E 细节。

**示范**：Yes, I tried cooking a traditional dish from another region for the first time last month. It was quite challenging because the recipe was complex, but when the dish finally turned out well, I felt a real sense of achievement — it was a rewarding experience.

## 5. Do you think experiences help people grow?

**思路**：A 表态（当然）→ R 原因 → E 举例。

**示范**：I'd say experiences are exactly what help people grow. The main reason is that it's through facing new situations — and sometimes difficulties — that people learn about themselves and develop new skills. For example, the challenging presentation I mentioned taught me confidence and preparation, which I've used since.

## 6. What kind of experiences do you enjoy?

**思路**：A 列举类型 → R 原因 → E 举例。

**示范**：I enjoy experiences that are both fun and meaningful — like travelling, trying new hobbies, or helping others. The main reason is that they give me more than just enjoyment; they leave me with something to learn and remember. For example, every trip I take gives me new perspectives on life.

## 练习任务

- 准备 2 个万能故事（一次克服困难、一次新体验），供 Part 2 使用。
