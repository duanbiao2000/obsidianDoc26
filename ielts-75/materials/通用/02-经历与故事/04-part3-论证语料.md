# 经历与故事 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。本话题多涉及经历的价值、成长、失败的意义。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / In my view, … / It depends on …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：Do difficult experiences help people grow?

**思路**：观点（是，但需正确面对）→ 原因（锻炼能力、塑造心态）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say difficult experiences do help people grow, provided people face them rather than avoid them. The main reason is that challenges force us to develop new skills and build resilience, which we wouldn't gain from easy situations. For example, people who have overcome setbacks often say they became stronger and more capable afterwards. That said, difficulty alone doesn't guarantee growth — it's the effort put into overcoming it that makes the difference.

## 问题 2：Is it better to learn from success or from failure?

**思路**：观点（都重要，但失败更深刻）→ 原因（失败暴露问题）→ 举例 → 折中。

**示范**：I'd say both are valuable, but failure often teaches more. The main reason is that success tends to confirm what we already know, while failure exposes our weaknesses and forces us to rethink. For example, many people recall a specific failure as the moment they learned the most. That said, learning from success matters too, as it builds confidence and shows what works. So the ideal is to learn from both, but with special attention to failures.

## 问题 3：Should young people be encouraged to try new things?

**思路**：观点（应该）→ 原因（试错成本低、成长快）→ 举例 → 让步（安全与引导）。

**示范**：I'd say young people should definitely be encouraged to try new things. The main reason is that youth is the best time for exploration — the cost of failure is lower, and the lessons learned carry forward for a lifetime. For example, trying new hobbies, subjects or activities often leads to hidden talents and lifelong passions. That said, trying new things doesn't mean taking unnecessary risks, so guidance and a safe environment matter too.

## 问题 4：How do people change as they experience more of life?

**思路**：观点（逐步成熟）→ 原因（阅历塑造认知）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say people gradually become more mature and open-minded as they experience more of life. The main reason is that each experience adds to a person's understanding of the world and of themselves, which changes their perspective. For example, someone who has travelled and worked in different places often sees situations from multiple angles rather than a single one. Beyond that, experience also tends to make people more patient and tolerant, because they've learned that not everything goes as expected.

## 问题 5：Are memorable experiences more valuable than everyday moments?

**思路**：观点（都重要，视角不同）→ 难忘经历的价值 → 日常时刻的价值 → 折中。

**示范**：I'd say both have value, but in different ways. Memorable experiences, such as big trips or important events, stand out and often shape who we are, providing long-lasting lessons. On the other hand, everyday moments — the small, ordinary interactions with family and friends — are what make up the fabric of daily happiness. For example, the warmth of a regular family dinner may not be "memorable" in the dramatic sense, but it contributes to well-being in a quiet, steady way. So I believe the two complement each other.
