# 物品与活动 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材 1：一件有意义的物品（礼物类经典素材，可一材多用）。
> 核心素材 2：一个你喜欢做的活动。

## 素材骨架 1（有意义的物品）

**主题**：一件对你有特殊意义的物品

- **是什么**：什么物品、谁给的/怎么来的
- **背后的故事**：一个相关事件或回忆
- **意义**：为什么珍视、带来什么

## 话题卡 1：Describe a gift you received that was important to you

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：a hand-made notebook from my best friend on my graduation
- 点 1：what it is — hand-made, with photos and messages inside
- 点 2：why it matters — took time and thought, reminds me of our friendship
- 收尾：meaning — I keep it and use it, a reminder of that time

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about a gift I received that was really important to me — a hand-made notebook from my best friend on my graduation.
First of all, the notebook is not an ordinary one. She had filled it with photos, messages and memories we shared over the years, all put together by herself.
Then, what makes it so special is the thought behind it. It took her a long time to make, and it shows how well she knows me. Every time I open it, I'm reminded of our friendship and all the good times we've had.
In the end, it means a lot to me because it's unique and irreplaceable. I still keep it on my desk and use it for notes, and it's a reminder of a meaningful moment in my life.

## 话题卡 2：Describe an object you use every day（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about an object I use every day — my notebook.
- 重点：怎么用、为什么依赖它（记录、整理）
- 收尾：小物品对生活的作用

## 话题卡 3：Describe a hobby or activity you enjoy（一材多用）

**素材骨架 2（喜欢的活动）**

- **是什么**：什么活动、怎么开始的
- **为什么喜欢**：两个原因
- **具体经历与收获**：一次印象深刻的经历、带来的变化

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：photography, started three years ago
- 点 1：why I enjoy it — capturing moments, seeing ordinary things carefully
- 点 2：a specific experience — a photo I was proud of
- 收尾：what it has brought — patience, relaxation, shared with friends

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about a hobby I really enjoy — photography.
First of all, I started it about three years ago, and what I enjoy most is that it makes me notice the world more carefully — a sunset, a street scene, even small details I'd normally ignore.
Then, there's one photo I still remember clearly: a sunset over the river, which took me several attempts to capture. When I finally got it, I felt a real sense of achievement.
In the end, photography has brought me more than just photos — it has taught me patience, given me a way to relax, and even become something I share with friends.

## 避免背稿感

- 物品话题重点在"故事与意义"，别花太多时间描述外观；
- 一材多用时，焦点按题目调整：礼物 / 日常物品 / 爱好活动。
