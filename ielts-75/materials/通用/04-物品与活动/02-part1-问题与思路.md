# 物品与活动 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. Do you like giving gifts to others?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 原因 → E 举例。

**示范**：Yes, I enjoy giving gifts to others. The main reason is that choosing a gift shows that you care about the person's tastes, and seeing them like it is rewarding. For example, I once picked a book for a friend based on her interests, and her reaction made the effort completely worthwhile.

## 2. What's the most meaningful gift you've received?

**思路**：A 点出 → R 为什么有意义 → E 细节。

**示范**：I'd say the most meaningful gift I've received was a hand-made notebook from my best friend. The main reason is that it took her time and thought to make, and it reminds me of our friendship every time I use it. It means a lot to me because it's unique — you can't buy that kind of thoughtfulness.

## 3. Do you prefer spending time on activities or on objects?

**思路**：A 表态（活动）→ R 原因 → E 对比。

**示范**：I'd say I prefer spending time on activities. The main reason is that activities create memories, while objects are just things. For example, a trip or an evening with friends stays with you, whereas a new object quickly feels ordinary. Experiences, in my view, are more valuable.

## 4. What do you usually do in your free time?

**思路**：A 列举 → R 原因 → E 细节。

**示范**：I usually do a few things in my free time — reading, listening to music and going for walks. The main reason is that they're simple, relaxing activities that help me recharge after a busy day. Whenever I feel stressed, one of them is usually enough to make me feel better.

## 5. Have you ever received a gift you didn't like?

**思路**：A 回答（坦诚）→ R 如何应对 → E 启示（避免尴尬、重视心意）。

**示范**：Honestly, yes, there has been a gift I didn't really like. The main reason was simply that it didn't match my taste, but I knew the giver had put thought into it. So I tried to appreciate the intention rather than focus on the gift itself, which is what matters, and it taught me to think about others' preferences when giving gifts.

## 6. Do you think it's important to try new activities?

**思路**：A 表态（重要）→ R 原因 → E 举例。

**示范**：I'd say it's quite important. The main reason is that trying new activities broadens your horizons and can lead to unexpected interests. For example, when I took up photography, I had no idea it would become one of my favourite hobbies — if I hadn't tried it, I would have missed out on a lot of fun.
