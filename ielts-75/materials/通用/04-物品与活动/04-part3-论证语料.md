# 物品与活动 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。本话题多涉及礼物、消费、活动价值。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / In my view, … / It depends on …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：Is it better to give a hand-made gift or a bought one?

**思路**：观点（手作更好，心意为重）→ 原因（独特、用心）→ 举例 → 让步（购买也实用）。

**示范**：I'd say a hand-made gift is generally better, because it shows more thought and effort. The main reason is that a hand-made gift is unique and carries the giver's personal touch, which a purchased item often lacks. For example, a notebook filled with personal messages means far more than an expensive but generic gift. That said, a well-chosen bought gift can also be meaningful, especially when it matches the receiver's needs. So the key is thoughtfulness, not the price.

## 问题 2：Do you think people buy too many things nowadays?

**思路**：观点（消费确实偏多）→ 原因（营销、便利）→ 举例 → 让步（合理消费）。

**示范**：I'd say people do buy quite a lot nowadays. The main reason is that shopping has become extremely convenient, and marketing constantly encourages people to buy more than they need. For example, many people accumulate far more clothes, gadgets and other items than they actually use. That said, buying things isn't inherently bad — the problem is excess. I believe mindful consumption, where people buy what they need and value, is the right approach.

## 问题 3：What makes an activity worth doing?

**思路**：观点（有意义+有收获）→ 原因 → 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say an activity is worth doing when it brings both enjoyment and meaning. The main reason is that an activity that's merely fun may quickly feel empty, while one that offers growth or connection leaves something lasting. For example, volunteering or learning a new skill may be challenging, but the sense of achievement and progress makes it truly worthwhile. So in my view, the best activities combine pleasure with value.

## 问题 4：Is it important for people to have hobbies?

**思路**：观点（重要）→ 原因（减压、成长、平衡）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say having hobbies is very important. The main reason is that hobbies provide a way to switch off from work and daily pressure, which protects both mental and physical health. Beyond that, they offer opportunities for learning and self-expression. For example, people who pursue hobbies like music, sport or crafts often report feeling more balanced and fulfilled. In a world full of responsibilities, a hobby is a necessary part of a healthy life.

## 问题 5：Should children be encouraged to play more, or to study more?

**思路**：观点（平衡，游戏与学习都重要）→ 游戏的好处 → 学习的必要性 → 折中。

**示范**：I'd say children should have a balance of play and study, with neither neglected. On the one hand, play is essential — it develops creativity, social skills and physical health, and it's how children learn about the world. On the other hand, study builds knowledge and discipline, which are also important. For example, children who have enough time to play tend to be more focused and creative in their studies. So I believe the best approach is structured study combined with ample time for play.
