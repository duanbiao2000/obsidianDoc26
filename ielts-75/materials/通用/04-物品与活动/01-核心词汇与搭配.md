# 物品与活动 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、物品

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| gift / present（礼物） | receive a gift | a present |
| meaningful（有意义的） | a meaningful object | something that matters |
| useful（有用的） | a very useful item | practical |
| valuable（珍贵的） | valuable to me | precious |
| keep（保存） | keep it for years | hold onto it |
| reminder（提醒物） | a reminder of … | something that reminds me of |
| hand-made（手工的） | a hand-made gift | homemade |

## 二、活动

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| activity（活动） | leisure activities | things to do |
| take up（开始） | take up a new activity | start doing |
| participate（参与） | participate in activities | take part in |
| enjoy（喜欢） | I really enjoy … | I'm fond of, I'm into |
| relaxing（放松的） | a relaxing activity | something to unwind |
| rewarding（有收获的） | find it rewarding | worthwhile |
| quality time（高质量时光） | spend quality time | make time for |

## 三、评价与态度

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mean a lot to（意义重大） | it means a lot to me | it's very important to me |
| cherish（珍惜） | cherish the memory | value |
| practical（实用的） | a practical choice | useful, functional |
| worthwhile（值得的） | a worthwhile activity | rewarding, valuable |
| meaningful（有意义的） | a meaningful activity | something with meaning |
| never get tired of（百看不厌） | I never get tired of it | I always enjoy it |

## 四、高频句型

- The object I'd like to talk about is …, which …（我想谈的物品是…，它…）
- It means a lot to me, because …（它对我意义重大，因为…）
- I've kept it for …, and it reminds me of …（我保存了…，它让我想起…）
- I enjoy this activity because …（我喜欢这个活动，因为…）
- What I find most special about it is …（我觉得它最特别的是…）

## 五、易错提醒

- 物品类话题避免只描述外观，要讲"背后的意义"；
- 描述物品用现在时，讲述获得经历用过去时；
- 避免通篇 "good/nice"，用 meaningful / practical / cherish 等更精准词汇。
