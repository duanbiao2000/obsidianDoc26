# 地点与空间 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. Do you like visiting new places?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 原因 → E 举例。

**示范**：Yes, I love visiting new places. The main reason is that getting to know unfamiliar places broadens my horizons and offers a break from routine. For example, even a new neighbourhood or park can feel refreshing, and travelling to a totally new city gives me a lot to discover.

## 2. What's your favourite place in your city?

**思路**：A 点出 → R 为什么喜欢 → E 细节。

**示范**：I'd say my favourite place is the large park in the east of the city. The main reason is that it's peaceful and full of greenery, so whenever I feel stressed, I go there to walk or just sit. It's a perfect place to relax and clear my mind.

## 3. Do you prefer busy places or quiet places?

**思路**：A 表态（可折中，分场景）→ R 各自好处 → E 场景。

**示范**：It depends on the occasion. For socialising or exploring, I enjoy busy, lively places — they're full of energy. But when I need to focus or unwind, I definitely prefer quiet places, like the park I mentioned, where I can concentrate and relax.

## 4. Is there a place you would like to visit in the future?

**思路**：A 点出 → R 动机 → E 期待。

**示范**：Yes, I'd love to visit a historical city in Europe. The main reason is that I'm fascinated by ancient architecture and history, and visiting in person would be far more meaningful than reading about it. I'm particularly looking forward to wandering through the old streets and visiting the museums there.

## 5. How have public spaces in cities changed?

**思路**：A 概括变化 → R 具体变化 → E 举例。

**示范**：I'd say public spaces have improved in many ways. The main reason is that cities have been paying more attention to quality of life, so more green spaces and pedestrian areas have been built. For example, many cities now have redesigned parks and walking paths, which make the city more pleasant to live in.

## 6. Do you think tourists should avoid visiting crowded places?

**思路**：A 表态（视情况）→ R 原因 → E 折中建议。

**示范**：I'd say it depends. Crowded places are often popular because they're worth seeing, so tourists shouldn't avoid them entirely, but they should plan ahead — for example, visiting at off-peak times to avoid the worst crowds. That way, they can still enjoy the place without the discomfort.
