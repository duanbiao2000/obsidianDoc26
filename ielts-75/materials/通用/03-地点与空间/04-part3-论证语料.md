# 地点与空间 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。本话题多涉及城市、旅行、公共空间。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / In my view, … / It depends on …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：Should cities preserve their historical places?

**思路**：观点（应该）→ 原因（文化、身份、旅游）→ 举例 → 让步（发展需求）。

**示范**：I'd say cities should definitely preserve their historical places. The main reason is that historical sites carry a city's culture and identity — they connect people to the past and make a city unique. For example, old streets and historic buildings are often the first thing visitors remember, and they bring economic benefits too. That said, preservation shouldn't block development, so the key is to find a balance between protecting heritage and growing the city.

## 问题 2：Is living in a big city better than living in a small town?

**思路**：观点（因人而异）→ 城市优点 → 小镇优点 → 折中。

**示范**：I'd say it depends on personal priorities. Big cities offer more opportunities in work, education and entertainment, and public services are usually more developed. On the other hand, small towns provide a calmer pace of life, closer communities and more space. For example, families may prefer the quiet and safety of a small town, while career-focused people may choose the city. So in my view, neither is universally better.

## 问题 3：What makes a city attractive to visitors?

**思路**：观点（多因素）→ 逐点（文化、便利、环境）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say an attractive city needs a combination of things. First of all, cultural richness — interesting places, history and local culture that visitors can experience. Then, convenience — good transport, clear information and friendly locals. What matters most, though, is a pleasant environment, because even a city with great sights can feel unwelcoming if the streets are dirty or crowded. For example, cities that balance culture, convenience and cleanliness tend to leave the best impression.

## 问题 4：Should public spaces be free for everyone?

**思路**：观点（核心公共空间应该免费）→ 原因（公平、福祉）→ 举例 → 让步（维护成本）。

**示范**：I'd say basic public spaces should be free for everyone. The main reason is that parks, squares and public libraries are public goods — charging for them would exclude people who can't afford it, which goes against the idea of equal access. For example, free parks allow families, students and elderly people to enjoy the city without cost. That said, some special facilities or events may need charges to cover maintenance, so I believe core spaces should be free while special services can be paid.

## 问题 5：How can cities become more environmentally friendly?

**思路**：观点（多管齐下）→ 方法（绿化、交通、规划）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say cities can become more environmentally friendly in several ways. First of all, by expanding green spaces, such as parks and tree-lined streets, which improve air quality. Then, by promoting public transport and non-motorised travel, such as cycling and walking, to cut down on emissions. For example, cities with good cycling networks and clean public transport have significantly reduced their environmental impact. Beyond that, careful planning that balances development with nature is essential for long-term sustainability.
