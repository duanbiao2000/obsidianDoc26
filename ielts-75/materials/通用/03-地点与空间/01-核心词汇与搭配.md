# 地点与空间 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、地点描述

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| located in（位于） | is located in the heart of the city | sits in, lies in |
| atmosphere / vibe（氛围） | a peaceful atmosphere | a calm vibe |
| scenery / landscape（风景） | stunning scenery | beautiful views |
| crowded（拥挤的） | a crowded place | packed, busy |
| peaceful（宁静的） | a peaceful place | a quiet, calm place |
| historical（历史的） | a historical city | a city with a long history |
| modern（现代的） | a modern city | a city of skyscrapers |

## 二、空间与场所

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| public space（公共空间） | public spaces like parks | open areas for everyone |
| landmark（地标） | a famous landmark | a well-known site |
| attraction（景点） | tourist attractions | places worth visiting |
| park（公园） | a large park | a green space |
| museum（博物馆） | visit a museum | a place of history |
| a quiet place（安静的地方） | a quiet corner | a peaceful spot |

## 三、感受与活动

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| recommend（推荐） | I highly recommend … | I'd suggest … |
| worth visiting（值得去） | a place worth visiting | a place you shouldn't miss |
| relax（放松） | a good place to relax | a great spot to unwind |
| explore（探索） | explore the city | discover, wander around |
| escape（逃离） | escape the noise | get away from the noise |
| have a strong impression（印象深刻） | left a strong impression on me | made a deep impression |

## 四、高频句型

- There's a place I'd like to talk about — …（我想谈一个地方——…）
- What makes it special is …（它特别的地方是…）
- I usually go there when …, because …（我通常在…时去那里，因为…）
- It's a perfect place for …, as …（它是…的完美去处，因为…）
- Whenever I visit, I always …（每次去，我都会…）

## 五、易错提醒

- 描述地点用现在时，回忆经历用过去时；
- 避免只堆形容词，用"五感细节"（能看到/听到/感受到什么）代替；
- "worth doing" 是固定搭配，不要写成 "worth to do"。
