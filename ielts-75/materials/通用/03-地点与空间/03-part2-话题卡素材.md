# 地点与空间 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材 1：一个你喜欢的安静地点（可一材多用）。
> 核心素材 2：一个你想去的旅行目的地。

## 素材骨架 1（喜欢的地点）

**主题**：一个你常去、喜欢的地点（如公园/图书馆/老街）

- **位置与外观**：在哪里、长什么样
- **氛围与活动**：氛围如何、人们在这里做什么
- **你的感受**：为什么喜欢、给你带来什么

## 话题卡 1：Describe a quiet place you like

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：the large park near my home, where I go to relax
- 点 1：what it's like — peaceful, green, with a lake
- 点 2：what I do there — walk, sit by the lake, watch people
- 收尾：why I like it — escape the noise, clear my mind

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about a quiet place I like — a large park near my home.
First of all, the park is quite peaceful, with plenty of green trees and a small lake in the centre. Unlike the busy streets around it, the moment I step into the park, the noise of the city seems to fade away.
Then, what I usually do there is walk around the lake or find a bench to sit and read. I also enjoy watching people — families with children, joggers, people walking their dogs — which gives the place a warm and relaxed atmosphere.
In the end, what I love about it is that it offers a perfect escape from daily pressure. Whenever I feel stressed, going there helps me clear my mind, and it has become my favourite place to unwind.

## 话题卡 2：Describe a place you would like to visit（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：There's a place I'd really like to visit — a historical city in Europe.
- 重点：为什么想去（建筑、历史、文化）、期待做什么
- 收尾：相信这会是一次难忘的经历

## 话题卡 3：Describe a place that is important to you（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：There's a place that's important to me — the park near my home.
- 重点：为什么重要（日常的慰藉、回忆、习惯）
- 收尾：它已成为我生活的一部分

## 素材骨架 2（想去的目的地，备用）

**主题**：一个向往的旅行目的地——动机、特色、期待

- 动机：为什么想去（文化/自然/历史）
- 特色：最吸引你的 2 个方面
- 期待：想体验什么、想象中的感受

## 避免背稿感

- 地点描述容易"说明书化"，加入个人活动与感受，让地点有温度；
- 一材多用时，焦点按题目调整：安静地点 / 想去的地方 / 重要的地方。
