# 住宿与家乡 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材：你的家乡（可一材多用，覆盖地点、经历、感受类话题卡）。

## 素材骨架（核心故事）

**主题**：你的家乡——位置、特点、一个地标/活动、你的个人情感

- **位置与规模**：位于哪里、规模、人口特点
- **特点**：最突出的 2 个特点（风景/美食/氛围/文化）
- **地标/活动**：一个具体的地点或活动（带细节：什么时候、做什么）
- **情感**：对家乡的感情、未来的期望

## 话题卡 1：Describe your hometown

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：a medium-sized city in the south, where I grew up
- 点 1：character — relaxed, friendly, famous for riverside scenery
- 点 2：landmark — the old town along the river, weekend market
- 收尾：feeling — home, proud of it, changes are welcome but memories stay

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about my hometown — a medium-sized city in the south where I grew up.
First of all, what makes it special is its relaxed and friendly atmosphere. It's not a busy metropolis, and people here are warm and easy-going, which gives the city a really comfortable vibe.
Then, the landmark I'd mention is the old town along the river. There's a weekend market there, where locals sell food and crafts, and I still remember going there with my grandparents when I was a child.
In the end, to me it's simply home. Even though the city is changing fast, those memories and the warmth of the place will always stay with me.

## 话题卡 2：Describe a place you would like to visit again（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：There's a place I'd love to visit again — my hometown's old town.
- 重点：重游的场景——周末市场、河边散步、童年回忆
- 收尾：为什么想再去——找回忆、感受那种氛围

## 话题卡 3：Describe a place that is important to you（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：There's a place that's really important to me — my hometown.
- 重点：为什么重要——成长、家人、熟悉的社区
- 收尾：无论走到哪里，它是我身份的一部分

## 避免背稿感

- 家乡话题容易"背稿感"重，讲的时候要像在向朋友介绍，用自然停顿；
- 三个话题卡共享素材，重点按题目调整，不要逐字照背。
