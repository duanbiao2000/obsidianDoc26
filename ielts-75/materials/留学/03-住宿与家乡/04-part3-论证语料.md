# 住宿与家乡 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。本话题多涉及居住选择、城乡对比、城市问题。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / From my point of view, … / It depends on …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：Is it better to live in a big city or a small town?

**思路**：观点（各有优劣，因需求而异）→ 大城市优点 → 小镇优点 → 折中总结。

**示范**：I'd say it depends on what people value. Big cities offer more opportunities — better jobs, education and entertainment — and everything is more convenient. On the other hand, small towns provide a calmer pace of life, stronger community bonds and a closer connection to nature. So in my view, the better choice depends on whether someone prioritizes opportunities or quality of life.

## 问题 2：What are the problems of living in big cities?

**思路**：观点（问题存在）→ 逐点列举（压力、成本、污染）→ 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say big cities face several problems. First of all, the cost of living is very high — housing and daily expenses are a real burden for many people. Then, city life can be stressful, with heavy traffic and constant noise, which takes a toll on people's well-being. For example, many young people I know work long hours and barely have time to relax. So while cities offer opportunities, they also come with real costs.

## 问题 3：Should the government build more housing in cities?

**思路**：观点（应该）→ 原因（人口增长、住房紧张）→ 让步（成本与规划难度）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say governments should indeed build more housing in cities. The main reason is that urban populations keep growing, and the shortage of affordable housing pushes up rents and puts pressure on ordinary people. That said, building more housing is not simple — cities need to balance it with green spaces and infrastructure, otherwise it can make cities worse to live in. So I believe it should be done carefully, along with better public services.

## 问题 4：How does the environment affect where people choose to live?

**思路**：观点（影响越来越大）→ 原因（健康、生活质量）→ 举例 → 让步（工作机会仍是主导）。

**示范**：I'd say the environment plays an increasingly important role. The main reason is that people are becoming more aware that clean air, green spaces and low pollution directly affect their health and quality of life. For example, many families now choose to live further from industrial areas, even if it means a longer commute. That said, work opportunities still tend to be the decisive factor for most people, so environment and livelihood often have to be balanced.

## 问题 5：Do you think people will move more to cities in the future?

**思路**：观点（会，但速度放缓/有分流）→ 原因（就业、资源）→ 让步（远程办公、乡村发展）→ 折中预测。

**示范**：I'd say people will keep moving to cities, but the trend may slow down. The main reason is that cities still concentrate the best jobs, education and medical resources, which naturally attract people. That said, the rise of remote work and the improving conditions in smaller towns mean some people can now live wherever they prefer. So in the future, I believe we'll see a more balanced pattern, with people moving between cities and smaller places.
