# 住宿与家乡 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. Where do you live now?

**思路**：A 直接回答 → R 位置特点 → E 具体细节（社区/交通）。

**示范**：I live in a flat in the city centre. The main reason I like it is that everything is convenient — public transport, shops and cafes are all within walking distance. My neighbourhood is quite quiet, which makes it a really comfortable place to live.

## 2. Do you like your hometown?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 喜欢的原因 → E 一个具体场景/地标。

**示范**：Yes, I'm very fond of my hometown. The main reason is that it has a relaxed atmosphere and beautiful scenery, especially along the river in the old town. Whenever I go back, I love walking there with my family, and it always feels like coming home.

## 3. What do you like about your hometown?

**思路**：A 列举 2 点 → R 原因 → E 细节。

**示范**：I'd say two things stand out. First, the food — my hometown is known for its street food, which I never get tired of. Second, the people — everyone is friendly and willing to help, which gives the place a warm and welcoming atmosphere.

## 4. Would you prefer to live in a city or in the countryside?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 原因 → E 让步（另一面的一点好处）→ 回观点。

**示范**：I'd definitely prefer living in a city. The main reason is that cities offer more opportunities — better jobs, education and entertainment, and everything is more convenient. That said, the countryside is calmer and closer to nature, which is appealing too. But for now, the opportunities in the city win for me.

## 5. What's your ideal home like?

**思路**：A 描述理想居所 → R 原因 → E 具体特征。

**示范**：My ideal home would be a bright flat with a big window, located in a quiet area but not too far from the centre. The main reason is that I need both peace for studying and convenience for daily life. If I had the chance, I'd also love a small balcony where I could read in the morning.

## 6. Has your hometown changed much since you were a child?

**思路**：A 表态（变化很大）→ R 具体变化 → E 感受（好坏并存）。

**示范**：Yes, it has changed a lot. The most noticeable change is that the city has expanded a lot, with new roads and shopping centres appearing everywhere. While this has made life more convenient, I do feel the old, quiet corners of my childhood have gradually disappeared, which makes me a bit nostalgic.

## 练习任务

- 重点练第 4 题的比较结构与第 6 题的"变化+感受"；
- 把家乡话题的标准化介绍（位置+特点+地标+感受）整理成一段背熟，可复用于多题。
