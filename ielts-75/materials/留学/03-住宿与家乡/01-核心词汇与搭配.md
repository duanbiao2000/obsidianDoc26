# 住宿与家乡 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、家乡与城市

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hometown（家乡） | my hometown is … | the place I'm from, where I grew up |
| located in / situated in（位于） | is located in the south of … | lies in, sits in |
| population（人口） | a city with a population of … | a city of … people |
| scenery（风景） | beautiful scenery | a beautiful landscape, views |
| landmark（地标） | a famous landmark | a well-known spot |
| atmosphere（氛围） | a friendly atmosphere | a warm vibe |
| convenient（便利的） | convenient transport | easy to get around |
| livable / a nice place to live（宜居的） | is a great place to live | is a wonderful place to settle down |

## 二、住宿

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| accommodation（住宿） | find accommodation | housing, a place to live |
| rent（租房） | pay rent, rent an apartment | lease |
| landlord（房东） | my landlord is … | the person who owns the flat |
| neighbourhood（社区） | a quiet neighbourhood | a community, an area |
| neighbourhood（社区） | a friendly neighbourhood | local area, local community |
| spacious（宽敞的） | a spacious flat | a roomy apartment |
| well-furnished（家具齐全的） | a well-furnished flat | fully equipped |
| facilities（设施） | basic facilities | amenities, amenities nearby |
| commute（通勤） | a long commute | the journey to work/school |

## 三、感受与评价

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| be used to（习惯于） | I'm used to city life | accustomed to |
| get used to（适应） | getting used to the new place | adjusting to, adapting to |
| feel at home（宾至如归） | feel at home here | feel comfortable and welcome |
| miss（想念） | I miss my hometown | I feel nostalgic about |
| in my opinion（在我看来） | in my opinion, … | as far as I'm concerned |

## 四、高频句型

- My hometown is …, which is known for …（我的家乡…，以…闻名）
- What I like most about it is …（我最喜欢的是…）
- I've lived there for …, so I'm quite familiar with …（我在那里住了…，所以很熟悉…）
- Compared with …, I find … more …（与…相比，我觉得…更…）
- If I had the chance, I would …（如果有机会，我会…）

## 五、易错提醒

- "hometown" 搭配动词 is / has / is known for，不要与 "home" 混用；
- 谈家乡用一般现在时（客观描述）+ 过去时（回忆），注意切换；
- 比较结构用 compared to / whereas / while，避免只用 "but"。
