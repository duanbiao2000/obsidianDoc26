# 科技与数字生活 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。科技话题重点练"双刃剑"论证。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / In my view, … / It depends on how …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：Is technology making people's lives easier?

**思路**：观点（总体更便利）→ 好处（效率、便利）→ 让步（压力、依赖）→ 回观点。

**示范**：I'd say technology has generally made life easier. The main reason is that it saves us enormous amounts of time — tasks that used to take hours, like booking tickets or doing research, can now be done in minutes. For example, online services allow people to handle almost everything from home. That said, technology also brings new pressures, such as information overload and a growing dependency on devices. Still, if people use it wisely, the convenience it offers is a real improvement.

## 问题 2：Do children spend too much time on screens?

**思路**：观点（普遍偏多）→ 原因（诱惑大、自控弱）→ 举例 → 让步（合理使用有价值）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say many children do spend too much time on screens. The main reason is that digital content is highly engaging by design, and children often lack the self-control to stop themselves. For example, it's common to see children glued to phones for hours, which affects both their health and their social skills. That said, screens can also be valuable learning tools when used properly, so I believe the answer is not to ban them, but to guide children to use them in a balanced way.

## 问题 3：Will artificial intelligence change the way people work?

**思路**：观点（会，且已开始）→ 具体变化（重复性工作自动化、新岗位）→ 让步（挑战与转型）→ 总结。

**示范**：I'd say artificial intelligence will definitely change the way people work, and it has already started to. The main reason is that AI can handle repetitive tasks more efficiently, freeing people to focus on creative and strategic work. For example, in fields like data analysis and translation, AI is already changing daily workflows. That said, this also means workers need to update their skills, and some jobs may disappear. Still, I believe AI will ultimately reshape work in a positive direction, provided people adapt.

## 问题 4：Is social media a positive or negative development?

**思路**：观点（双刃剑，取决于使用）→ 好处（连接、信息）→ 弊端（比较、隐私）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say social media is a double-edged sword — its impact depends on how people use it. On the one hand, it keeps people connected across distances and provides a fast way to share information. On the other hand, it can lead to unhealthy comparison, and privacy issues are a real concern. For example, many people feel anxious after scrolling through others' posts. So in my view, social media itself is neither good nor bad; what matters is how people manage their use of it.

## 问题 5：Should there be stricter rules on the use of technology?

**思路**：观点（特定领域需要）→ 原因（隐私、安全、健康）→ 让步（过度监管抑制创新）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say stricter rules are needed in certain areas. The main reason is that issues like data privacy and online safety have become serious, and without rules, ordinary people are left vulnerable. For example, clear regulations on how companies handle personal data would protect users effectively. That said, over-regulation could also stifle innovation and make useful technology harder to develop. So I believe rules should be targeted and balanced, focusing on protecting people without blocking progress.
