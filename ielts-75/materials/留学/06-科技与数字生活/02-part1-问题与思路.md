# 科技与数字生活 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. Do you use the internet a lot?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 用途 → E 具体场景。

**示范**：Yes, I rely on the internet quite heavily. The main reason is that it's how I study, keep in touch with friends and get information on a daily basis. For example, almost all my research for assignments is done online, and I also video-call my family regularly.

## 2. What do you usually do on your phone?

**思路**：A 列举用途 → R 原因 → E 细节。

**示范**：I use my phone for a few things — messaging, listening to music and checking news. The main reason is that it's convenient for everything in one place, so I don't need to carry other devices. Sometimes I also use navigation apps when I travel, which saves me a lot of trouble.

## 3. Do you think you spend too much time on your phone?

**思路**：A 坦诚表态 → R 原因（习惯）→ E 应对措施（展示自我管理）。

**示范**：Honestly, yes, I probably spend more time on my phone than I should. The main reason is that it's easy to get drawn into social media without noticing. What I've started to do is set screen-time limits, which helps me cut down and spend more time on offline activities.

## 4. Is the internet important in your life?

**思路**：A 表态（非常重要）→ R 原因（学习、生活、社交）→ E 举例。

**示范**：I'd say it's extremely important. The main reason is that it has become essential for almost everything — studying, working and keeping in touch with people. For example, during the times when I couldn't meet my friends in person, online communication was what kept us connected.

## 5. Do you prefer reading on a screen or on paper?

**思路**：A 表态（可折中）→ R 原因（场景不同）→ E 具体场景。

**示范**：It depends on what I'm reading. For quick information or articles, I prefer reading on a screen, because it's more convenient and searchable. But for long books or things I need to focus on, I still prefer paper, as it's easier on my eyes and helps me remember better.

## 6. How has technology changed the way you study?

**思路**：A 概括变化 → R 具体两点（资源、方式）→ E 举例。

**示范**：It has changed my study a lot. The main reason is that I now have easy access to a huge range of resources online, and I can study at my own pace. For example, I can watch lecture videos again whenever I need to review, which wasn't possible before.
