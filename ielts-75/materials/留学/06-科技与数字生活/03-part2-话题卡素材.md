# 科技与数字生活 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材：一次你借助科技解决实际问题/获得便利的经历（可一材多用）。

## 素材骨架（核心故事）

**主题**：一次借助科技（如在线课程/导航/某个 App）解决困难的经历

- **背景**：遇到什么困难/需求
- **过程**：用什么科技工具、怎么用、如何发挥作用
- **结果**：解决了什么问题、感受如何

## 话题卡 1：Describe a time you used technology to solve a problem

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：last semester, struggled with a difficult maths course
- 点 1：the problem — concepts too hard, couldn't keep up in class
- 点 2：how I used tech — online tutorial videos, an exercise app with instant feedback
- 收尾：result — grades improved, learned to use tech for learning

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about a time I used technology to solve a problem — last semester, when I struggled with a difficult maths course.
First of all, the problem was that the concepts were too abstract, and I couldn't keep up with the teacher's pace in class, so I was falling behind.
Then, I turned to technology for help. I found a series of online tutorial videos that explained each concept step by step, and I also used an exercise app that gave me instant feedback on my answers. This meant I could learn at my own pace and immediately see where I went wrong.
In the end, it worked — my grades improved steadily, and I realised that technology, when used properly, can be a powerful learning tool.

## 话题卡 2：Describe an app or software you like（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：There's an app I really like — an online learning platform I used last semester.
- 重点：它的功能、为什么好用、如何使用
- 收尾：它改变了我的学习方式

## 话题卡 3：Describe a change that has improved your life（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about a change that has improved my life — the spread of online learning tools.
- 重点：过去怎么学习、现在怎么学习、变化带来的具体好处
- 收尾：这个变化让我明白，善用科技能带来真正的进步

## 避免背稿感

- 科技类话题容易讲得"说明书化"，要加入个人感受与具体困难，让故事有温度；
- 一材多用时，焦点按题目调整：解决困难 / 推荐工具 / 生活变化。
