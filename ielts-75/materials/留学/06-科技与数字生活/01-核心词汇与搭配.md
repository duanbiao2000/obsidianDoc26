# 科技与数字生活 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、设备与使用

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| smartphone（智能手机） | be glued to my smartphone | be attached to my phone |
| device（设备） | digital devices | gadgets, equipment |
| keep in touch（保持联系） | keep in touch with friends | stay connected with |
| rely on（依赖） | rely heavily on the internet | depend on |
| access（获取/使用） | easy access to information | be able to get information |
| convenience（便利） | for convenience | for the sake of convenience |

## 二、网络与媒体

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| social media（社交媒体） | spend time on social media | browse social platforms |
| online（在线） | study/work online | remotely, on the internet |
| information overload（信息过载） | suffer from information overload | too much information to handle |
| privacy（隐私） | protect personal privacy | keep my data safe |
| cyber security（网络安全） | issues of cyber security | online safety concerns |
| virtual（虚拟的） | virtual communication | online, remote |

## 三、评价与影响

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| double-edged sword（双刃剑） | a double-edged sword | has both pros and cons |
| reshape（重塑） | reshape our daily life | change the way we live |
| efficiency（效率） | boost efficiency | make things faster |
| dependency（依赖性） | growing dependency on tech | relying more on technology |
| face-to-face interaction（面对面交流） | face-to-face interaction | meeting in person |
| keep up with the times（跟上时代） | keep up with the times | stay current |

## 四、高频句型

- Technology has become part of our daily life, which means …（科技已成为日常生活的一部分，这意味着…）
- I'd say that … is a double-edged sword.（我认为…是把双刃剑）
- On the one hand, … / On the other hand, …（一方面…，另一方面…）
- Although …, I still believe that …（虽然…，但我仍然认为…）
- It depends on how people use it.（这取决于人们如何使用它）

## 五、易错提醒

- "technology" 泛指科技，"device/gadget" 指具体设备，别混用；
- 谈科技影响用现在完成时（has changed / has brought）体现"已经发生并持续"；
- 避免通篇"good/bad"，用 double-edged / convenient / dependency 等更精确词汇。
