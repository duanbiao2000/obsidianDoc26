# 学业与专业 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。没观点时用折中表态或从个人经验切入。

## 语料句（通用，可套用于多个问题）

**表态**
- From my point of view, … / I'd say that … / It seems to me that …
- It depends on …（折中）/ It varies from person to person.

**解释**
- The main reason is that … / This is because …
- The advantage of … is that … / What matters most is …

**举例**
- For example, … / Take … for instance.
- A good case in point is …

**让步**
- That's true, but … / On the other hand, …
- Although …, I still believe that …

## 问题 1：Do you think school uniforms should be required?

**思路**：观点（可折中）→ 原因（统一、减少攀比）→ 让步（个性表达受限）→ 回观点。

**示范**：I'd say requiring school uniforms has both sides. On the one hand, uniforms create a sense of unity and save students the trouble of choosing clothes every day, which can reduce pressure and comparison. That's true, but on the other hand, they can limit students' freedom to express their personalities. Still, I believe the benefits outweigh the drawbacks, as long as the uniforms are comfortable and affordable.

## 问题 2：Should students be allowed to use phones in class?

**思路**：观点（不建议）→ 原因（分心、影响专注）→ 让步（应急/学习工具）→ 折中建议。

**示范**：I'd say phones should generally not be allowed in class. The main reason is that they are a huge source of distraction — even when they're on the desk, notifications pull students' attention away from the lesson. That said, there are cases where phones can be useful learning tools, so I'd suggest a compromise: phones stay turned off unless the teacher allows them for specific activities.

## 问题 3：Is online learning as effective as face-to-face learning?

**思路**：观点（各有优劣）→ 线上优点（灵活、资源多）→ 线下优点（互动、自律门槛低）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say online learning can be as effective as face-to-face learning, but it depends on the learner. Online courses offer flexibility and access to a wide range of resources, which suits self-disciplined students. On the other hand, face-to-face learning provides more interaction and immediate feedback, which helps those who struggle with motivation. So in my view, the best approach is a mix of both.

## 问题 4：How important is university education for a successful career?

**思路**：观点（重要但不是唯一）→ 原因（技能、证书、人脉）→ 让步（技能、经验同样重要）→ 回观点。

**示范**：I'd say university education is fairly important, but it's not the only path to a successful career. The main reason is that it builds both professional knowledge and soft skills like critical thinking and teamwork, which employers value. That's true, but many careers also value hands-on experience, and some people succeed through vocational training or entrepreneurship. Still, for most people, a degree lays a solid foundation.

## 问题 5：Do you think homework is necessary for students?

**思路**：观点（适量必要）→ 原因（巩固、自主学习）→ 让步（过量有害）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say homework is necessary, but only in moderation. The main reason is that it helps students consolidate what they've learned and develop self-discipline, which is essential for long-term progress. That said, too much homework can lead to burnout and take away time from rest and hobbies. So I believe the key is a reasonable amount of meaningful homework, rather than hours of repetitive tasks.
