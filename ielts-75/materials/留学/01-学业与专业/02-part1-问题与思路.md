# 学业与专业 · Part 1 问题与思路

> 每题按 A-R-E（回答 → 原因 → 例子/细节）展开 3–4 句。
> 示范回答为 7.5 档水平：有搭配、有从句、有具体细节。

## 1. What are you studying?

**思路**：A 直接说专业 → R 选它的原因 → E 一个具体课程或收获。

**示范**：I'm studying computer science, which focuses on programming and data analysis. I chose it because I've always been fascinated by how technology shapes our daily life, and what I enjoy most is that the courses are very hands-on — we build real projects instead of just reading textbooks.

## 2. Do you like your major?

**思路**：A 明确表态 → R 喜欢的原因 → E 一个具体例子。

**示范**：Yes, I'm really into it. The main reason is that it's both challenging and rewarding — although the coursework is demanding, I find it rewarding to see my projects actually work. For example, I recently built a small app for my assignment, which gave me a real sense of achievement.

## 3. What's the most difficult part of your studies?

**思路**：A 点出难点 → R 为什么难 → E 如何应对（展示解决问题能力）。

**示范**：I'd say keeping up with the coursework is the toughest part. It's demanding because the workload is heavy and the concepts get more complex as we go, so I have to manage my time carefully. What helps is that I make a study schedule and stick to it, which stops me from falling behind.

## 4. Do you prefer studying alone or with others?

**思路**：A 表态（可折中）→ R 原因 → E 具体场景。

**示范**：It depends on the task. When I need to focus on reading or writing, I prefer studying alone so I can concentrate, but for group projects I actually enjoy working with others, because discussing ideas with classmates often helps me see things from different angles.

## 5. What do you plan to do after graduation?

**思路**：A 直接计划 → R 原因 → E 条件假设（条件句加分）。

**示范**：I'm planning to work in the tech industry for a couple of years to gain practical experience. If I get the chance, I'd also like to do a master's degree, because I believe further study will lay a solid foundation for my long-term career.

## 6. Was there a subject you used to dislike but now enjoy?

**思路**：A 点出科目 → R 转变的原因 → E 细节。

**示范**：Yes, maths. I used to find it really boring at school because it felt abstract, but now I've come to enjoy it, since my major has shown me how it's actually applied in real problems. That shift in perspective made it much more interesting to me.

## 练习任务

- 每个问题录音回答，检查是否达到 3–4 句、有 A-R-E 层次；
- 把示范回答里的搭配挑 3 个加入 Anki。
