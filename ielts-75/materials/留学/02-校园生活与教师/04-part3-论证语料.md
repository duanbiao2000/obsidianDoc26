# 校园生活与教师 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。本话题多讨论"教育方式与师生关系"类抽象问题。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / From my perspective, … / It depends on …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：Should teachers be friends with students?

**思路**：观点（保持专业距离更合适）→ 原因（权威与公平）→ 让步（关系好利于沟通）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say teachers should keep a professional distance rather than being close friends. The main reason is that a certain level of authority helps maintain discipline and fairness in the classroom — if a teacher is too casual, students may struggle to take rules seriously. That said, being friendly and approachable is still important, because a good relationship encourages students to ask questions. So I believe the balance is: friendly in attitude, but professional in role.

## 问题 2：Is it better for students to learn in small classes or large classes?

**思路**：观点（小班更好）→ 原因（互动多、关注度高）→ 让步（大班也有资源与多样性优势）→ 回观点。

**示范**：I'd say small classes are generally better for learning. The main reason is that students get more individual attention and more opportunities to participate, which makes it easier for teachers to notice who is struggling. That's true, but large classes also have their advantages, such as more diverse group work and better resources. Still, for most students, the personalized attention in small classes leads to better outcomes.

## 问题 3：How important is school in a person's life?

**思路**：观点（非常重要但不唯一）→ 原因（知识、社交、习惯）→ 让步（家庭与社会同样重要）→ 回观点。

**示范**：I'd say school plays a very important role, though it's not the only factor. The main reason is that school provides not only knowledge but also the social skills and habits we need for life — we learn to cooperate, to compete properly, and to manage our time. That said, family and the wider community also shape a person deeply. Still, school is the place where most people build the foundation for their future.

## 问题 4：Do you think students should be given more freedom at school?

**思路**：观点（适度自由）→ 原因（培养自主能力）→ 让步（过度自由导致松懈）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say students should be given a reasonable amount of freedom. The main reason is that allowing choices helps students develop independence and take responsibility for their own learning, which is essential for adult life. On the other hand, too much freedom can lead to a lack of discipline, especially for younger students. So I believe freedom should be given gradually, together with clear guidance.

## 问题 5：What makes a good teacher?

**思路**：观点（耐心+专业+能激发学生）→ 逐点解释 → 举例 → 总结。

**示范**：I'd say a good teacher needs a combination of qualities. First of all, patience matters a lot, because students learn at different speeds and a patient teacher never gives up on anyone. Then, professional knowledge is essential — a teacher who really knows the subject can make even complex topics easy to understand. What I find most important, though, is the ability to inspire, because a teacher who sparks curiosity can motivate students to learn beyond the classroom.
