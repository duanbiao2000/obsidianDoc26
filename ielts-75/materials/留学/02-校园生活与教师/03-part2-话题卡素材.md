# 校园生活与教师 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材：一位让你印象深刻、对你有影响的老师（人物类，可一材多用）。

## 素材骨架（核心故事）

**主题**：一位曾经帮助过你、让你成长的老师

- **人物**：什么科目的老师、性格特点
- **事件**：一次具体的帮助（课后辅导 / 鼓励你参加比赛 / 帮你重拾信心）
- **影响**：你因此学到了什么、改变了什么

## 话题卡 1：Describe a teacher you liked

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：my high school English teacher, Ms. Chen
- 点 1：what she was like — patient, supportive, made classes lively
- 点 2：a specific event — encouraged me to join the speech contest, stayed after class to help
- 收尾：impact — built my confidence, taught me to persist

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about a teacher I liked very much — my high school English teacher, Ms. Chen.
First of all, she was incredibly patient and supportive. She always made her classes lively, and no matter how many questions we asked, she never seemed to get tired of explaining things.
Then, there's one event I remember clearly: she encouraged me to enter a speech contest, even though I was too nervous to speak in public. She stayed after class with me to practice, one sentence at a time.
In the end, her support made a real difference to me — it built my confidence and taught me that persistence pays off.

## 话题卡 2：Describe a person who influenced you（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：There's a person who influenced me deeply — my high school English teacher.
- 重点从"喜欢的课程氛围"改为"她对我的影响"：从害怕开口到敢于表达
- 收尾：I still think of her advice whenever I face a challenge.

## 话题卡 3：Describe an important conversation you had（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about an important conversation I had with my English teacher.
- 重点：一次课后谈话——她指出我的问题，鼓励我别怕犯错
- 收尾：那次对话改变了我的学习态度，让我明白错误是学习的一部分

## 避免背稿感

- 讲老师的故事时，细节（课后辅导、逐句练习）要讲具体，这是"讲满 2 分钟"的燃料；
- 三个话题卡共享人物，但开场与重点按题目重新组织。
