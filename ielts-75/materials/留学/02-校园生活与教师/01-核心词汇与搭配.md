# 校园生活与教师 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、校园生活

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| campus（校园） | on campus, live on campus | the school grounds |
| campus life（校园生活） | enjoy campus life | life at school/university |
| dormitory / hall of residence（宿舍） | live in a dormitory | student accommodation |
| facilities（设施） | sports facilities, teaching facilities | equipment, resources |
| campus culture（校园文化） | a positive campus culture | the atmosphere at school |
| extracurricular（课外的） | extracurricular activities | after-school activities |
| routine（日常安排） | a busy daily routine | schedule, daily schedule |

## 二、师生与课堂

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| teacher / instructor（教师） | a memorable teacher | a teacher who left an impression |
| explain（解释） | explain sth clearly | make sth clear |
| inspire（启发） | inspire students | motivate, encourage |
| patient（耐心的） | be patient with students | stay calm and supportive |
| strict（严格的） | be strict about discipline | demand high standards |
| supportive（支持的） | a supportive teacher | encouraging, helpful |
| participation（参与） | encourage participation | get students involved |
| group work（小组合作） | do group work | teamwork, collaborative tasks |

## 三、感受与收获

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| make a difference（产生影响） | make a real difference to students | have a real impact on |
| leave an impression（留下印象） | leave a deep impression on me | stay in my memory |
| gain confidence（获得自信） | gain confidence in speaking | become more confident |
| go beyond（超出） | go beyond the textbook | extend beyond |
| support each other（互相支持） | classmates support each other | look out for one another |

## 四、高频句型

- My school/campus is …, which means …（我的校园…，这意味着…）
- What I remember most about … is …（关于…我印象最深的是…）
- … has made a real difference to …（…对…产生了真正的影响）
- It was … that I first realised …（正是在…时，我第一次意识到…）
- Without …, I wouldn't have …（如果没有…，我就不会…）

## 五、易错提醒

- 描述老师避免只用 "kind/good"，换用 supportive / patient / inspiring 等更具体词汇；
- "campus" 前用 on（on campus），不用 in the campus；
- 回忆类话题用过去时为主，感受用现在时，注意时态切换。
