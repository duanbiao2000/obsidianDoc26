# 校园生活与教师 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. What do you like about your school/campus life?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 具体两点 → E 场景细节。

**示范**：I really enjoy the atmosphere of campus life. The main reason is that it's both lively and supportive — there are always activities going on, and classmates are easy to reach out to. For example, I love spending time in the library with friends after class, which makes studying feel less lonely.

## 2. Do you prefer studying on campus or at home?

**思路**：A 表态（可折中）→ R 原因 → E 具体场景。

**示范**：I'd say I prefer studying on campus. The main reason is that campus gives me a clear boundary between study and rest, and there are fewer distractions than at home. When I'm at home, I find it hard to stay focused, whereas in the study rooms on campus, I can concentrate much better.

## 3. What do you usually do with your classmates?

**思路**：A 列举 → R 原因 → E 细节。

**示范**：We usually do group work and hang out after class. I find group work useful because discussing assignments together often leads to better ideas, and it's also a good way to get to know each other. After class, we sometimes grab a coffee or play sports together, which helps us relax.

## 4. Do you think teachers should be strict with students?

**思路**：A 折中表态 → R 严格的好处 → E 让步（过于严格的弊端）→ 回观点。

**示范**：I'd say teachers should be strict in some ways, but not in every way. Being strict about standards and discipline helps students build good habits and stay on track, but being too strict can make students afraid to ask questions. So I believe the ideal is strict about the essentials, while staying supportive and approachable.

## 5. Is there a teacher who impressed you?

**思路**：A 点名 → R 为什么印象深刻 → E 具体事件。

**示范**：Yes, my high school English teacher impressed me most. She was incredibly patient and supportive — whenever I struggled with speaking, she never just corrected me, but encouraged me to try again. One time I failed a presentation, and she helped me prepare a second one, which really built my confidence.

## 6. Do you think school life is too busy?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 原因（课业+活动）→ E 如何应对（展示自我管理）。

**示范**：I'd say it can be quite busy, especially around exams. The workload and extracurricular activities both take up a lot of time, so it's easy to feel overwhelmed. What helps me is planning ahead and setting aside fixed study hours, which keeps me from getting stressed out.
