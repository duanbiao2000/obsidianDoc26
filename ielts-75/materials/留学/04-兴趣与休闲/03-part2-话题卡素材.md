# 兴趣与休闲 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材：一个你坚持了很久的爱好（可一材多用）。

## 素材骨架（核心故事）

**主题**：一个长期坚持的爱好（如摄影/跑步/烹饪/乐器）

- **是什么**：什么时候开始的、怎么开始的
- **为什么喜欢**：两个原因（乐趣 + 收获）
- **具体经历**：一次印象深刻的与该爱好相关的事件
- **影响**：对你性格、生活、朋友的影响

## 话题卡 1：Describe a hobby you enjoy

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：photography, started three years ago on a school trip
- 点 1：why I enjoy it — seeing ordinary things carefully, capturing moments
- 点 2：a specific experience — the first photo I was proud of (sunset over the river)
- 收尾：impact — patience, a way to relax, shared with friends

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about a hobby I really enjoy — photography.
First of all, I started it about three years ago, on a school trip, when my teacher encouraged me to take more photos. What I enjoy most about it is that it makes me notice ordinary things more carefully — a sunset, a street scene, even small details I'd normally ignore.
Then, there's one photo I still remember clearly: a sunset over the river, which took me several attempts to get right. When I finally captured it, I felt a real sense of achievement.
In the end, photography has taught me patience and gives me a perfect way to relax, and I often share my photos with friends, which brings us closer.

## 话题卡 2：Describe something you do that makes you happy（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：There's one thing that always makes me happy — my hobby of photography.
- 重点：从"喜欢"转到"快乐"——按下快门的瞬间、回看照片的感受
- 收尾：它是我生活中不可或缺的一部分

## 话题卡 3：Describe a skill you learned（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about a skill I learned — photography.
- 重点：从零开始的过程（构图、光线、后期）、遇到的困难、如何克服
- 收尾：这项技能带来的收获超出预期

## 避免背稿感

- 爱好类话题要讲得"有温度"，用真实细节（第一次、一次具体的拍摄）而非套话；
- 一材多用时注意切换重点：不同题目问的焦点不同，不要照搬同一段。
