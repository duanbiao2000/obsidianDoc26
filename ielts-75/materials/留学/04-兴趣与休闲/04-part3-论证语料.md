# 兴趣与休闲 · Part 3 论证语料

> 框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步。本话题多讨论休闲、运动、娱乐的价值与影响。

## 语料句（通用）

**表态**：I'd say that … / In my view, … / It depends on …
**解释**：The main reason is that … / This is because …
**举例**：For example, … / Take … for instance.
**让步**：That's true, but … / On the other hand, …

## 问题 1：Is it important to have hobbies?

**思路**：观点（重要）→ 原因（减压、技能、社交）→ 举例 → 让步（不要过度）。

**示范**：I'd say having hobbies is very important. The main reason is that they offer a way to switch off from work or study pressure, which protects both mental and physical health. Beyond that, hobbies help people build skills and social bonds. For example, people who join running clubs or music groups often make friends through shared interests. That said, hobbies should stay a hobby, not become another source of pressure.

## 问题 2：Should children spend more time on sports?

**思路**：观点（应该）→ 原因（健康、品格）→ 举例 → 让步（学业平衡）。

**示范**：I'd say children should spend more time on sports. The main reason is that physical activity is essential for their health, and team sports also teach cooperation and discipline. For example, children who play football learn to work with others and handle both wins and losses. That said, sport should not come at the cost of study, so a balanced schedule matters more than simply adding more hours.

## 问题 3：How has leisure time changed compared to the past?

**思路**：观点（选择多了，但质量未必更高）→ 变化 1（选择丰富）→ 变化 2（被屏幕占据）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say leisure time has changed a lot. On the one hand, people now have far more choices — streaming services, games and social media offer endless ways to spend free time. On the other hand, I feel that a large part of leisure time is now spent on screens, and people may spend less time on real-life activities, such as meeting friends or playing sports. So while options have increased, the quality of leisure may not have improved accordingly.

## 问题 4：Do you think hobbies can become a job?

**思路**：观点（可以，但有代价）→ 原因（热爱转化为动力）→ 让步（兴趣变工作可能失去乐趣）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say hobbies can become a job, but it's not always easy. The main reason is that turning something you love into work can provide strong motivation and a sense of purpose, as many successful artists and athletes have shown. That said, when a hobby becomes work, it also brings deadlines and pressure, and the original joy may fade. So I believe it's possible, but people should think carefully about whether they are ready for that change.

## 问题 5：Should governments provide more leisure facilities?

**思路**：观点（应该）→ 原因（公共利益、健康、公平）→ 让步（财政压力）→ 折中。

**示范**：I'd say governments should provide more leisure facilities. The main reason is that accessible facilities — parks, sports centres, libraries — benefit everyone, especially those who cannot afford private options, and they contribute to public health and well-being. That said, governments also have to manage limited budgets and other priorities, such as education and healthcare. So I believe public leisure facilities should be expanded gradually, alongside efficient use of existing resources.
