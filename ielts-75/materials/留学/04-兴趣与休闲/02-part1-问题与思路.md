# 兴趣与休闲 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. What do you do in your free time?

**思路**：A 列出主要活动 → R 原因 → E 具体场景。

**示范**：In my free time, I usually read and go for a run. The main reason is that both help me switch off after a busy day — reading lets me escape into a story, while running clears my head. If I have a whole weekend, I also love cooking for my family, which I find quite relaxing.

## 2. Do you have a lot of free time?

**思路**：A 表态（不算多）→ R 原因（学业/工作）→ E 如何挤时间、珍惜时间。

**示范**：Not really, my schedule is quite full most days. The main reason is that study takes up most of my time, especially around exams. What I try to do is set aside at least half an hour a day for something I enjoy, because even a short break helps me recharge for the rest of the day.

## 3. What's your favourite hobby?

**思路**：A 直接点出 → R 为什么喜欢 → E 具体细节（什么时候做、带来什么）。

**示范**：My favourite hobby is photography. I've been into it for about three years, and what I enjoy most is that it makes me look at ordinary things more carefully — a sunset, a street scene, even small details. It also gives me a real sense of achievement when I take a photo I'm proud of.

## 4. Do you prefer spending free time alone or with others?

**思路**：A 折中 → R 各自的好处 → E 具体场景。

**示范**：It depends on the situation. When I'm tired, I prefer spending time alone — reading or just listening to music helps me recharge. But on weekends, I enjoy being with friends, whether it's playing sports or grabbing a meal together, because it's a great way to build social bonds.

## 5. Do you think people have enough leisure time today?

**思路**：A 表态（很多人没有）→ R 原因（工作压力、节奏快）→ E 举例 → 让步。

**示范**：I'd say many people don't have enough leisure time. The main reason is that modern life is fast-paced, and people often work long hours, leaving little energy for hobbies. For example, many of my friends barely have time for anything beyond work and study. That said, people are also becoming more aware of the importance of balance, which I think is a positive sign.

## 6. How do you usually relax after a long day?

**思路**：A 直接回答 → R 原因 → E 具体做法细节。

**示范**：I usually relax by taking a walk and listening to music. The main reason is that it's a simple way to shift my focus away from the day's pressure, without needing any special equipment. Sometimes I also call a friend, which helps me unwind and catch up at the same time.
