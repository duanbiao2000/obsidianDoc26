# 兴趣与休闲 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、休闲活动

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| spare time / free time（空闲时间） | in my spare time | when I'm free, in my free time |
| relax / unwind（放松） | relax after a busy day | unwind, wind down |
| take up（开始从事） | take up a hobby | start doing, pick up |
| keep fit（保持健康） | keep fit by exercising | stay healthy, stay in shape |
| work out（锻炼） | work out at the gym | exercise, do workouts |
| hang out（闲逛/聚会） | hang out with friends | spend time with, catch up with |
| escape from（逃离） | escape from daily pressure | get away from |

## 二、具体爱好

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| reading（阅读） | I'm a big reader | I love reading |
| listening to music（听音乐） | listen to music to relax | enjoy music |
| playing basketball / football（打球） | play basketball on weekends | play ball games |
| photography（摄影） | take up photography | get into photography |
| cooking（烹饪） | enjoy cooking on weekends | I like making food |
| hiking / camping（徒步/露营） | go hiking in the mountains | head to the mountains |
| travelling（旅行） | love travelling around | I'm keen on travelling |

## 三、好处与感受

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| relieve stress（缓解压力） | relieve stress from work/study | reduce pressure, ease tension |
| refresh（恢复精神） | refresh my mind | clear my head |
| social bond（社交纽带） | build social bonds | connect with others |
| a sense of achievement（成就感） | a real sense of achievement | feel accomplished |
| well-being（幸福感） | improve my well-being | make me feel better overall |
| escape the routine（摆脱日常） | escape the daily routine | get away from the daily grind |

## 四、高频句型

- In my spare time, I usually …, because …（空闲时我通常…，因为…）
- I've been … for about … years, and it has become part of my routine.（我…大约…年了，它已成日常）
- What I enjoy most about it is …（我最喜欢的是…）
- It not only …, but also …（它不仅…，还…）
- Whenever I feel stressed, I …（每当我感到压力大时，我会…）

## 五、易错提醒

- "hobby" 搭配动词 have / take up，不要与 "interest" 混用；
- 谈爱好用现在时为主，回忆经历用过去时；
- 避免反复用 "like"，换用 I'm into / I enjoy / I'm keen on 等变化表达。
