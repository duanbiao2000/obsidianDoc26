# 出行与旅行 · Part 2 话题卡素材

> 核心素材：一次难忘的旅行（可一材多用，覆盖经历、地点、感受类话题卡）。

## 素材骨架（核心故事）

**主题**：一次难忘的旅行——四要素齐全

- **时间/人物**：什么时候、和谁去的
- **地点**：去哪里、为什么选那里
- **事件**：做了什么、有什么具体经历（1–2 个细节）
- **感受**：为什么难忘、带来什么改变

## 话题卡 1：Describe a trip you enjoyed

**提纲（关键词）**

- 开场：a trip to a mountain town last summer with two friends
- 点 1：why we went — escape the heat, see the old town
- 点 2：what we did — hiked to a waterfall, tasted local food, stayed in a small inn
- 收尾：why unforgettable — the scenery, the relaxed pace, the time with friends

**示范提纲展开**：
I'd like to talk about a trip I really enjoyed — a journey to a mountain town last summer.
First of all, we chose this destination because we wanted to escape the summer heat and see a place with a slower pace of life. It was a small old town surrounded by green hills, which felt completely different from our busy city.
Then, what made the trip special was the experiences we had. We hiked to a waterfall in the morning, and in the evening we tasted local food at a small inn, chatting with the owner about the town's history.
In the end, it's an unforgettable trip, not just because of the beautiful scenery, but because it showed me how relaxing life can be when you slow down, and I still think of it often.

## 话题卡 2：Describe a place you would like to recommend（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：There's a place I'd highly recommend — a mountain town I visited last summer.
- 重点：推荐的理由——风景、氛围、适合什么人去
- 收尾：如果你也想找地方放松，这里是理想选择

## 话题卡 3：Describe an unforgettable experience（一材多用）

**改编思路**：
- 开场：I'd like to talk about an unforgettable experience — a trip to a mountain town.
- 重点：最难忘的瞬间（到达山顶看到日出/与店主的交谈）
- 收尾：这次经历改变了我对旅行的理解：旅行的意义在于体验，而非打卡

## 避免背稿感

- 旅行故事容易讲得流水账，注意用"最难忘的瞬间"制造重点，不要按时间平铺；
- 一材多用时，不同题目的焦点不同，调整开场与重点。
