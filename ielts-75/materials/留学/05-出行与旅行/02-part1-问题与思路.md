# 出行与旅行 · Part 1 问题与思路

## 1. Do you like travelling?

**思路**：A 表态 → R 原因（开阔视野、放松）→ E 具体经历。

**示范**：Yes, I'm really keen on travelling. The main reason is that it broadens my horizons and gives me a break from the daily routine. Whenever I visit a new place, I enjoy experiencing new things, such as local food and culture, which always feels refreshing.

## 2. Where do you usually go on trips?

**思路**：A 列举类型（自然/城市）→ R 偏好原因 → E 具体目的地。

**示范**：I usually head to places with nice scenery, like mountains or old towns. The main reason is that I find nature and history more relaxing than busy city centres. For example, I often choose to visit small towns in the countryside, where I can walk around and take photos.

## 3. How do you usually travel?

**思路**：A 按场景选择 → R 各自原因 → E 具体例子。

**示范**：It depends on the distance. For short trips within the city, I usually take the subway or cycle, because it's quick and avoids traffic jams. For longer journeys, I prefer trains over flights, since they are more comfortable and the views along the way are enjoyable.

## 4. Is travel more difficult than before?

**思路**：A 表态（更便利）→ R 原因（交通与科技）→ E 举例 → 让步（信息过载/拥挤）。

**示范**：I'd say travel is much easier than before. The main reason is that transport options are far more convenient, and the internet makes it simple to book tickets and find information in advance. That said, popular destinations can be extremely crowded during holidays, which can make the experience less comfortable than expected.

## 5. Do you prefer travelling alone or with others?

**思路**：A 表态（可折中）→ R 各自好处 → E 场景。

**示范**：I'd say I prefer travelling with friends or family. The main reason is that sharing experiences with others makes the trip more fun and safer, and we can look out for each other. That said, I also enjoy travelling alone sometimes, as it gives me the freedom to decide the schedule and spend time however I like.

## 6. Would you like to travel abroad?

**思路**：A 表态（想）→ R 原因（文化、语言、体验）→ E 具体想去的地方。

**示范**：Yes, I'd love to. The main reason is that travelling abroad offers a chance to experience different cultures and practise a foreign language in real situations. If I had the chance, I'd like to visit Europe, especially the old cities there, because I'm fascinated by their history and architecture.

## 练习任务

- 重点练第 3 题的比较结构与第 5 题的折中表态；
- 把旅行故事的四要素（时间/地点/事件/感受）整理成一段，随时可用。
