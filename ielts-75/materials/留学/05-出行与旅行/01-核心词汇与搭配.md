# 出行与旅行 · 核心词汇与搭配

## 一、旅行经历

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| trip（短途旅行） | take a trip, go on a trip | journey, outing |
| journey（旅程） | a long journey | trip, travel |
| travel（旅行） | love travelling | be keen on travelling |
| destination（目的地） | a popular destination | a place to visit |
| itinerary（行程） | plan an itinerary | plan the trip |
| souvenir（纪念品） | buy souvenirs | gifts to remember the trip |
| scenery / landscape（风景） | enjoy the scenery | take in the views |
| unforgettable（难忘的） | an unforgettable trip | a memorable experience |

## 二、交通方式

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| public transport（公共交通） | by public transport | take the bus/train |
| subway / underground（地铁） | take the subway | use the underground |
| take a taxi / ride-hailing（打车/网约车） | call a taxi | hail a cab |
| cycle / bike（骑车） | cycle to work | ride a bike |
| carpool（拼车） | carpool with colleagues | share a ride |
| convenient（便利的） | convenient public transport | easy to get around |
| traffic jam（堵车） | stuck in a traffic jam | heavy traffic |

## 三、感受与评价

| 词/表达 | 搭配 | 转述/近义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| broaden one's horizons（开阔视野） | broaden my horizons | open my eyes, expand my view |
| experience new things（体验新事物） | experience new things | try something new |
| get away from（逃离） | get away from the daily routine | escape the daily grind |
| recommend（推荐） | I highly recommend … | I'd suggest … |
| worth visiting（值得一去） | a place worth visiting | a place you shouldn't miss |
| packed（拥挤的） | a packed train | a crowded train |

## 四、高频句型

- I went to … with …, and we spent … days there.（我和…去了…，待了…天）
- What impressed me most was …（印象最深的是…）
- I prefer … because …（我更喜欢…，因为…）
- Compared with …, I find … more …（与…相比，我觉得…更…）
- It was an experience I'll never forget, because …（这是一次我永远不会忘记的经历，因为…）

## 五、易错提醒

- "trip" 偏短途，"journey" 偏长途/过程，注意区分；
- 描述过去的旅行用过去时，谈一般偏好用现在时；
- 比较交通方式时用比较级（faster, more convenient），避免只堆形容词。
