Part 3 是口语上 7.5 的分水岭：很多考生 Part 1/2 表现尚可， 但 Part 3 只会说一两句、没有展开，分数被压在 6.5。本课解决的就是"怎么把抽象问题答出长度和深度"。

二、通用论证框架：观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步

步骤做什么示例句型
1. 观点直接表态，可以折中"I'd say that … / From my point of view, … / It seems to me that …"
2. 解释给理由：因为…所以…"The main reason is that … / This is because …"
3. 举例用具体例子或结果支撑"For example, … / Take … for instance."
4. 让步（可选）承认另一面，再回到观点"That's true, but … / On the other hand, …"
示范：Do you think technology has made our lives easier or more stressful? 观点：I'd say technology has generally made life easier, though it does bring stress. 解释：The main reason is that it saves us a huge amount of time — things that used to take days can now be done in minutes. 举例：For example, online banking and shopping mean we never have to queue or travel to a bank at all. 让步：That's true, but the downside is that we're always reachable, so work never really stops. Still, I'd say the convenience wins overall.

三、三种常考题型的答题套路

题型套路例题
表态类（Do you think…?）表态 + 原因 + 举例 +（可选）让步Do you think children should do homework every day?
比较类（A or B?）选一方 + 原因 + 另一方的让步Is it better to live in a city or in the countryside?
预测类（Will… in the future?）预测 + 理由 + 条件假设（If…, then…）Will online courses replace classrooms?

四、没观点时怎么办：三个"救场"思路

遇到陌生话题没想法，是正常现象，考官更看重你怎么组织语言。三个实用思路：

① 折中表态："It depends on … / It varies from person to person." ——不选边，但要说出"取决于什么"，依然算有效观点。

② 从个人经验切入："Speaking for myself, …" ——把抽象问题拉回你能说的内容。

③ 谈影响而非立场：说不清对错，就谈"会有什么影响"，照样能展开。

五、语法亮点：让论证更有"7.5 味"

结构用法例句
条件句预测/假设类话题"If schools invested more in sports, students would be healthier."
情态动词表推测表达不确定的观点"It's likely that / There's a good chance that …"
被动语态客观讨论现象"More and more people are said to be working from home."
定语从句给名词加细节"… the kind of technology that has changed our daily routines."
